Motion Prompt #2-082

What it is: A heartbeat trace sweeps across a monitor
What the sample shows: On a dark green grid a glowing ECG trace sweeps left to right, older trace dims, and a heart on the right swells with each beat.
Spec:
- A dark screen with a 16 px fine grid (darker every 80 px); the trace repeats a P wave, QRS (62 px up) and T wave every 96 px.
- The sweep head crosses left to right in 2.35 s; 8% ahead of it is blank and older trace dims with age (down to 25%).
- The trace is a 2 px bright green line with a 7 px glow; a glowing dot (3.5 px radius, 9 px halo) marks the head.
- When an R wave passes the head, the heart swells to 1.25x and settles, and the BPM readout changes per beat.
When to use it: Use it for medical, health or fitness explainers, or to stand for a heartbeat or being alive.
