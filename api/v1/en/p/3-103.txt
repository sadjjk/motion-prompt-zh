Motion Prompt #3-103

What it is: Looking premium through a restrained palette, generous empty space, soft light and precise material detail
What the sample shows: It starts with a perfume bottle among flowers and a ribbon, on a background of 4 loud stripes. First the colours drop to muted beige tones. Then the flowers and ribbon disappear and the bottle shrinks and moves right, leaving space around it. Next a large soft light comes in from the left, a faint blurred shadow stretches to the right of the bottle and the right side of the frame darkens gently. Finally a liquid line, a vertical highlight on the glass and fine grooves on the metal cap appear. A checklist on the right turns each numbered circle blue as its ingredient arrives, and the status reads plain version → adding → finished.
Spec:
- 5 s cycle. 0–0.6 s is the plain version; the 4 ingredients join from 0.6 s, the next every 0.7 s, each fading in over 0.5 s.
- The 4 stripes mute to #E4DFD8 and the flowers and ribbon leave; the bottle shrinks from x 196 at 1× to x 262 at 0.68×.
- A large soft light from the left and a soft shadow blurred by 6 px to the right of the bottle; material: a liquid line, a vertical highlight on the left of the glass and 6 fine grooves on the metal cap.
- The finished look holds until 4.4 s and slides back to the plain version over 4.4–4.95 s, so 5 s and 0 s are the same frame.
When to use it: For product shots of cosmetics, perfume or jewellery that should look premium.
