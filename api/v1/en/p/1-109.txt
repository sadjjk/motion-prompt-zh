Motion Prompt #1-109

What it is: Strength bars fill segment by segment as the password grows
What the sample shows: As the dots in the "New password" field grow from 4 to 16, the four bars below light up one at a time and the strength label shifts from red to green.
Spec:
- A "New password" card (429 wide, radius 15.4) with a 48.4-high field. The masked dots grow from 4 to 8 / 12 / 16 (1.00 / 1.55 / 2.15 s).
- 4 strength segments (7.7 high, 6.6 apart) fill in turn: 1.05 s red #ef4444 "Weak", 1.60 s orange #f59e0b "Fair", 2.20 s yellow-green #84cc16 "Good", 2.75 s green #22c55e "Strong".
- Each colour change takes 0.25 s (0.3 s for the last) with a quadratic ease-out; the label at lower left changes with it, while the lower right always reads "At least 12 characters · mixed case".
- Over 3.2–3.95 s the card rises 4.4px with sine.inOut. Reset over 3.95–4.35 s (easeInOutCubic): the extra dots fade, the 4 segments return to grey #44403c and the label returns to amber "Weak".
- Versus Step indicator: the segments show password strength and react to typing; they do not mark which step of a flow you are on.
When to use it: Use it in sign-up, password change and account settings forms where you want to nudge users toward a strong password.
