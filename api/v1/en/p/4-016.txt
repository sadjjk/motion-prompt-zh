Motion Prompt #4-016

What it is: An Art Deco ornament of gold rays and fan arcs unfolding on black
What the sample shows: Gold rays and fan arcs spread layer by layer from the bottom centre of a black field, framed by stepped arches, as a warm band of light sweeps across.
Spec:
- Gold lines rgba(214, 176, 98) on black #0B0B0D; 25 rays extend to 300 px between 0.2 and 1.8 s.
- 9 fan arcs: arc i starts at 0.4 + 0.18i s and opens over 0.8 s, with radius 26 + 19i px.
- 5 stepped arches stay on screen, starting 182 px wide and narrowing by 26 px per step.
- A warm 60 px light band sweeps left to right 1 time every 6.4 s; rays and arcs retract from 5.4 to 6.2 s.
When to use it: Use it as a hero background for hotels, luxury brands or jazz events, an opener for award or gala videos, or an animated invitation.
