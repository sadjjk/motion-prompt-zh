Motion Prompt #2-057

What it is: A soft-glow tech look: half-transparent frosted-glass cards floating over a dark aurora gradient
What the sample shows: Aurora glows flow slowly over a dark background, 2 glass cards bob gently, chat lines appear one by one, and a glass lens sweeps back and forth magnifying the scene.
Spec:
- Deep indigo-black #040312 background with 5 aurora glows (#7050FF, #2F46F2, #34CFF0, #B653F0, #3320A8), drawn on a 68 × 26 low-res canvas and scaled up into a soft blur; they drift 1 loop per cycle.
- 2 frosted-glass cards: white fill at 8%, 1 px white border at 30%, a top highlight, and the backdrop scaled 1.08× and offset to fake refraction. They bob 2 times per cycle (2.5 px amplitude).
- Chat lines fade in one by one while rising 8 px (at 0.6, 1.3, 2.0, 2.7 s). At 3.4–4.0 s a Focus toggle slides on, and 18 waveform bars keep pulsing.
- A liquid-glass lens of radius 30 px sweeps across 1 round trip per cycle, magnifying what is below 1.35× with cyan/magenta fringes. Text fades out at 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Openings and feature demos in launch films for AI products, phone operating systems or smart devices.
