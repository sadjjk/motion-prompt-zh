Motion Prompt #2-058

What it is: A sci-fi film style cyan wireframe heads-up display that builds up layer by layer and locks onto a target
What the sample shows: Cyan frames, radar and globe light up in layers, readouts scramble then settle, brackets fly in, hunt and snap onto the target, and the lock flips everything magenta.
Spec:
- Dark teal-black #020A0D background. Cyan #00E5FF wireframes grow in staggered layers: outer corners at 0.12 s, outer ring at 0.4 s, radar at 0.55 s, globe at 0.9 s, right panel at 1.15 s.
- The radar sweeps 1 lap every 1.6 s (4 laps per cycle) with 4 blips. From 1.2 s, 5 readouts each scramble for 4 frames before settling, and the log types at 45 characters per second.
- The wireframe globe decelerates through 360° over 0–3.4 s. At 3.0 s corner brackets fly in from the frame corners, hunt by ±4 px at 3.25–3.55 s, then snap from 1.4× to 1× at 3.55–3.8 s.
- Lock at 3.8 s: everything turns magenta #FF2A8A, with a white flash and 3 expanding rings (at 3.8, 4.3, 4.8 s). Scanlines every 3 px cover the whole piece.
When to use it: Tech product promos, game trailers, sci-fi openings and data-visualisation explainers.
