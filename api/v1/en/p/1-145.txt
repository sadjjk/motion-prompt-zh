Motion Prompt #1-145

What it is: The box bounces and the check draws itself in
What the sample shows: The cursor ticks 3 boxes in turn; each squashes and bounces, fills blue as a white check draws in, and its text is struck through and greyed, then "Clear" resets them.
Spec:
- 3 to-do items (42 apart) with 22×22 boxes; the cursor clicks the boxes at 0.8, 1.5 and 2.2 s.
- Within 0.4 s the box squashes to 0.85, bounces to 1.1 and settles at 1; it fills blue in 0.15 s and a white check draws from lower left to upper right over 0.1–0.35 s.
- A strike-through grows left to right across the text over 0.2–0.5 s (easeInOut power 3) and the text turns grey.
- At 3.35 s "Clear" is clicked and all 3 items reverse together in 0.3 s.
When to use it: Use it in to-do, shopping or checklist screens where finishing an item should feel satisfying.
