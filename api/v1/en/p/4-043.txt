Motion Prompt #4-043

What it is: An explainer that grows a metro line drawn only at 0°, 45° and 90°, station by station, to walk through a route or a sequence of steps
What the sample shows: On white, red Line 1 grows and pauses at each station, which pops in on arrival; 博物馆 greys out with a closed tag, a yellow interchange branch draws at 市中心, and a train runs from 火车站 to 机场 before the map fades out.
Spec:
- Background #FAFAF7; the red line #E4002B (6 px) turns only at 0°, 45° and 90°. The badge 1 号线 fades in at 0.2 s.
- 0.2–4.2 s the line grows and pauses at each station; all 6 stations pop in with backOut on arrival, named in Chinese with small English.
- 1.65 s 博物馆 turns grey with a 暂停服务 tag; 3.1–3.5 s a yellow #F2A900 branch draws from 市中心 with the tag 换乘 2 号线.
- 4.2–5.3 s a train runs from 火车站 to 机场, turning with each segment; 5.4–6.0 s the whole map fades out.
When to use it: Use it for step-by-step processes, roadmaps and timelines that move from one stage to the next.
