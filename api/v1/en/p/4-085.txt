Motion Prompt #4-085

What it is: A 1930s black-and-white cartoon: a round-bodied character bounces to the beat with arms and legs that bend like rubber hoses, under film grain, flicker and scratches
What the sample shows: A character with a white face and pie-cut eyes bounces 4 times on the spot. One arm rises as the other drops and its body squashes at every landing. Film grain, flickering brightness, an occasional vertical scratch and a vignette cover the frame.
Spec:
- A warm grey card with a grey floor below an ink line at y 182. The character has a black round body 30 px in radius, a white face with 2 pie-cut eyes and a smile, hose arms with white gloves and hose legs with black shoes.
- The 6.4 s loop holds exactly 4 bounces of 1.6 s: the body rises 16 px at the top of each bounce and the feet 10 px, and the shadow shrinks as it rises.
- At each landing the body squashes from its base, up to 12% shorter and 8% wider, easing off as (1 − b)^4 with b the bounce height from 0 to 1; the arms swing ±0.7 rad, one rising while the other drops.
- The film effects change on a 12.5 fps frame count, 80 frames per loop: 160 grain specks, a brightness flicker of up to 4%, a faint vertical scratch on about a quarter of the frames, and a vignette.
- There is no fade-out: 6.4 s is exactly 4 bounces and 80 film frames, so the last frame flows straight back into the first.
When to use it: Use it for retro ads, jingles, a mascot dancing to music or an old-cinema style opening.
