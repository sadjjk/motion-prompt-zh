Motion Prompt #1-227

What it is: When the cursor enters a large menu row, a colour band slides in from the side it came from, with the item name scrolling inside it as a marquee
What the sample shows: On a dark background, the cursor runs down a 4-row menu and a band slides into each row from above with the name scrolling; returning up to the 3rd row, the band comes in from below.
Spec:
- There are 4 rows, each 44 px tall. The band is clipped to its row, slides in over 0.35 s and slides out over 0.35 s.
- The entry and exit directions come from whether the cursor was moving up or down when it crossed the row boundary.
- Inside the band, the item name and a dot repeat seamlessly while scrolling left at 70 px per second.
- The cursor enters from above at 0.5 s, runs down the 4 rows, goes back up to the 3rd row and leaves to the right at 4.8 s.
- The entry and exit times and directions are found once at load by stepping along the cursor path in 1/120 s steps.
When to use it: Use it for full-screen menus on portfolio or agency sites and big-type category lists where the menu itself is a showpiece.
