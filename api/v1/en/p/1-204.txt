Motion Prompt #1-204

What it is: A gradient field of dots seen through a lens that follows the cursor and magnifies or swirls the dots under it
What the sample shows: A lens of radius 72 px follows the cursor over a gradient dot grid with 13 px spacing; first it magnifies the dots inside, then it twists them into a swirl.
Spec:
- Dots on a 13 px grid are coloured with a horizontal gradient from #6A5CFF to #22D3EE to #F472B6, with brightness moved by two diagonal sine waves.
- The cursor enters at 0.3–1.0 s and leaves at 4.6–5.3 s, carrying a lens ring of radius 72 px with a soft highlight.
- At 1.0–2.6 s it magnifies: with t = 1 − d/72, dots inside are pushed out to d·(1+0.9t²) and gain up to 2.2t px of radius.
- At 2.6–3.0 s it blends into swirl mode, rotating dots around the cursor by 2.2t² radians.
When to use it: Use it on hero sections for AI or data products where the cursor should feel like a lens peering into the page.
