Motion Prompt #4-064

What it is: A festive period-picture-book look: an indigo-to-orange dusk sky, a vermilion gate tower, red lanterns and sky lanterns, a vermilion seal and gold numerals
What the sample shows: A dusk sky shading from indigo to orange, with a warm moon. The vermilion gate tower glows through its lattice windows and gateway, with a crowd silhouetted in front. Red lanterns on a rope light one by one from the left and sway, the glow in front of the tower grows, and sky lanterns drift up. A vermilion seal 灯会 stamps down at top left, and a gold 1120 年 floats up at right. On reset the lanterns go out in turn and the seal and numerals fade.
