Motion Prompt #1-212

What it is: A background of gradient vertical blinds that sway slowly and turn their slats toward a spotlight following the cursor
What the sample shows: 18 vertical blinds sway gently; as the cursor's spotlight moves, the slats tilt toward it and glow whitish where the light falls.
Spec:
- 18 vertical blinds are coloured across the frame from pink #FF5C8A to violet #7B5CFF to cyan #2EC4FF.
- Slat angle θ = 0.35·sin(phase + 0.4i) sway + presence × 0.9·tanh((light x − slat x)/120).
- Visible width is proportional to 0.2 + 0.8·|cos θ|, and faces turned to the light are brighter.
- The light is an additive spotlight of radius 170 px following the cursor; with no cursor it rests top-centre at half brightness.
When to use it: Use it on hero sections for music, fashion or product launches that want an elegant, moving background.
