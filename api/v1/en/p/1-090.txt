Please use "Heart burst like" (ハートがはじける「いいね」), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-090

What it is: A like reaction where the heart pulses and bursts with a ring and confetti dots
What the sample shows: On click the heart squashes then pops, filling red as a ring and coloured dots burst out; the count rolls from 842 to 843 and back on a second click.
Spec:
- Clicking runs a 0.7 s beat: 1 → 0.72 → 1.24 → 0.94 → 1, while a red gradient #FF4D6D→#FF8A5B fills from the centre.
- After a 0.1 s delay a pink ring expands from 0.3× to 1× over 0.6 s, thinning out as it fades.
- 7 pairs of coloured dots burst outward about 51° apart, flying from 30 px to 62 px while shrinking away.
- The count rolls up from 842 → 843 in 0.45 s; in the demo a second click at 4.0 s unlikes, the heart returns to an outline and the count rolls down to 842.
When to use it: Use it on like or favourite buttons in social, community and content apps to give clear tap feedback.
