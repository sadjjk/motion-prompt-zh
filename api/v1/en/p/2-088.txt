Please use "Swing letters" (振り子の揺れ), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #2-088

What it is: Letters hang from a point and swing like pendulums
What the sample shows: Four characters hang on threads from a rail and swing lazily about yellow pivots above them, each slightly out of phase with its neighbour.
Spec:
- Each character hangs from a pivot above and swings ±14°.
- It makes 3 swings in 4.7 s (about 1.57 s each) on a cosine curve.
- Neighbouring characters are offset by 0.27 s.
- Every second character is coral, the rest white.
When to use it: Use it for relaxed logos, character intros or idle screens.
