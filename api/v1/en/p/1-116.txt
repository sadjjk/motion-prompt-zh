Motion Prompt #1-116

What it is: The label shrinks and floats up when the field is focused
What the sample shows: The cursor clicks the field: the label shrinks, moves up and turns blue, the underline spreads from the centre and an email address is typed letter by letter; it is then cleared and blurred.
Spec:
- A 350×52 input; the label "Email address" starts inside the field in grey at 15px.
- The cursor clicks in at 0.9 s: over 0.25 s (easeOut power 3) the label shrinks to 11px, rises 15px and turns blue, and the blue underline grows from the centre to 334px over 0.3 s.
- From 1.4 s "hello@kitten.cn" is typed at 0.08 s per character, with the caret blinking every 0.5 s.
- From 3.3 s it is deleted at 0.025 s per character; at 3.85 s a click outside blurs the field and the label and underline return over 0.25 s.
When to use it: Use it in sign-in, sign-up or contact forms where the field name should stay visible while typing.
