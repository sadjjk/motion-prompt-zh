Motion Prompt #1-107

What it is: When the layout changes, the remaining items glide to their new places
What the sample shows: Deleting the second card makes the following cards glide into the gap, with the wrapping card moving up diagonally; undo sends each back to its place.
Spec:
- A 4×2 card grid (cards 110×66, column pitch 122, row pitch 78). The cursor clicks card 2's × at 1.0 s; that card shrinks to 0.85 and fades out in 0.2 s with easeOut.
- The other cards glide to their new slots over 1.25–1.75 s (0.5 s, easeInOut, power 3); card 5 moves diagonally from the start of row 2 to the end of row 1.
- At 1.3 s a "1 item deleted · Undo" toast appears. Undo is clicked at 3.35 s: the cards move back over 3.5–4.0 s and card 2 grows and fades back in over 3.95–4.25 s.
- Versus Drag to reorder: there is no dragging; items reflow automatically after a delete or restore, never jumping.
When to use it: Use it for boards, photo grids, carts and tag lists — anywhere adding, removing or filtering changes the layout.
