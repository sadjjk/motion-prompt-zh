Motion Prompt #3-076

What it is: Sunlight through the leaves drops bright patches on the person and the ground
What the sample shows: The scene sinks into greenish shade and uneven patches of sun scatter over the person, buildings and ground; the wind moves them together, neighbouring patches merging and parting; oval spots line the ground and blurred leaves hang into the top corners.
Spec:
- Scene 1: a head-and-shoulders figure (head centre x 270, y 78, radius 29 px); #7F9682 is multiplied over everything as tree shade.
- Patch shapes: 64 leaf-gap dots and 12 flat ellipses for the ground are drawn on a 1/4-size canvas (135×52), blurred by 1.1 px and thresholded with 16× contrast so nearby dots join up.
- It is scaled up to full size, blurred by 1.6 px, multiplied with #FFE0A0 and screened on at 0.92 opacity.
- Wind: overall shift = 1.6·sin(T) + 0.6·sin(3T+1) (in small-canvas px) plus a small sway per dot; leaves #3B5442 blurred by 7 px at 0.55 opacity sit in the top corners.
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For parks, woods and summer afternoons that should feel calm and alive.
