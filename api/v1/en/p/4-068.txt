Motion Prompt #4-068

What it is: A close-reading archive look: film strips and bamboo slips on a cold white light table, a loupe magnifies one character and a red pen circles it
What the sample shows: In a darkroom the light table flickers on like a fluorescent tube. An amber film strip goes down, then three bamboo slips one by one from above. A loupe arcs from beside the film to the slips and stops over 年 on the third slip. Inside the lens everything is magnified 2.2 times; a red pen circles the character in one stroke and a numbered tag 关键字「年」 is stuck beside it. On reset the table goes dark and everything lifts up out of frame.
Spec:
- Light table: switches on over 0.1–0.5 s, flickering at first.
- Items: film at 0.6 s, slips at 0.9, 1.1 and 1.3 s.
- Loupe: moves to the key character over 1.7–3.0 s, radius 30, 2.2×.
- Red-pen circle: 3.2–3.7 s; numbered tag at 3.9 s.
- Reset: 5.4–6.0 s the light goes off and items lift 220 px.
When to use it: Use it for archaeology, artefacts and close reading of historical sources.
