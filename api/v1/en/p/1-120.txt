Motion Prompt #1-120

What it is: A folded card opens its cover to reveal the message inside
What the sample shows: The cursor clicks the navy cover; it swings open on its left edge to show the inner left half while 3 lines on the right fade up in turn, and it closes again at the end.
Spec:
- A closed 150×130 card with a navy cover reading "Invitation".
- The cursor clicks the cover at 0.8 s; over 0.9–1.6 s the cover opens on its left edge (scaleX 1 → −1, easeInOut power 3), showing the inner left half once past 0, while the whole card moves 75px right to stay centred.
- The 3 lines inside fade in and rise 6px from 1.6, 1.8 and 2.0 s, 0.3 s each.
- At 3.4 s the inside is clicked: the text fades out over 3.4–3.6 s, and over 3.6–4.2 s the cover closes and the card slides back.
When to use it: Use it for invitations, greeting cards or announcements where opening should feel like a small reveal.
