What it is: Copies of one character open out like a folding fan
What the sample shows: Seven stacked copies of 扇 swing open around a yellow pivot below into a fan, with translucent coral copies either side of the white centre one, then fold shut.

- The centre copy is opaque white; the others are coral, fainter further out (0.7 − 0.12 × distance).
