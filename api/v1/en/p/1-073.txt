Please use "Rolling chapter numbers" (回転する章番号), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-073

What it is: A sidebar whose chapter number rolls like a number strip as you read
What the sample shows: As the body scrolls, the big chapter number on the left rolls to the next like a digit strip, the chapter name slides in, and a ring fills.
Spec:
- The big chapter number in the left column rolls continuously like a digit strip, 64 px per step, only moving on after 75% of a chapter is read.
- On a chapter change, the chapter name below slides up and is replaced (.45 s spring, .3 s opacity).
- A progress ring of radius 16 px and 4 px stroke fills with how much of the chapter has been read.
- 4 chapters in total; demo scrolls the body to chapter 4 over 0.4→4.8 s and back over 5.4→6.3 s.
When to use it: Use it as sidebar navigation for chaptered long reads such as case studies, annual reports and brand stories.
