Please use "Add to cart fly" (カートに飛び込む), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-136

What it is: The product thumbnail arcs into the cart and the count bumps up
What the sample shows: When the product card button is pressed, the cup image arcs to the cart in the top right while shrinking; the cart bounces and the badge changes from 2 to 3.
Spec:
- The button is pressed at 1.0 s (dips to 0.95).
- From 1.05–1.8 s a copy of the product image flies to the cart: x accelerates with t², y moves linearly plus a 40 px arc, and its size goes 1 → 0.15.
- On arrival the cart bounces to 1.25 over 0.35 s and the badge changes 2 → 3, popping with a 0.3 s backOut.
- It cross-fades back to the starting frame from 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it on e-commerce listings and product pages to make "added to cart" unmistakable.
