Motion Prompt #1-224

What it is: A search field bent along an arc, with its text, caret and send button all following the curve
What the sample shows: A flat search field bends into an arc; on click a focus ring appears and letters are typed one by one along the arc; pressing send makes the text flow along the arc and vanish, and the placeholder returns.
Spec:
- At 0.2–0.8 s the flat field bends into an arc of 200 px radius, and at 5.4–6.0 s it straightens again to match 0 s.
- The field is a 44 px thick round-capped line drawn along the arc's centre line, 300 px long.
- The icon, text, caret and send button are placed at their positions on the arc and turned to the tangent there; the text is placed letter by letter.
- A click at 1.2 s focuses the field, letters are typed one by one at 1.4–3.0 s, and send is pressed at 3.8 s.
- In the 0.5 s after send the text flows 110 px along the arc as it fades, and the placeholder returns in 0.3 s.
When to use it: Use it on brand or campaign pages where a search or signup field should have a memorable shape.
