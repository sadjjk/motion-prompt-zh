Motion Prompt #3-108

What it is: A dream-like look from backlight, highlights that glow softly, blurred flowers or gauze in the foreground and a pink, lavender and cream palette
What the sample shows: It starts from a plain daytime picture of a person, head and shoulders, among flowers. First the sun appears behind them and a bright line traces the edge of the head and shoulder. Then the brightness around the sun blooms outward and small points of light on the flowers twinkle. Next the 4 near flowers grow and blur into soft blobs of colour. Finally the whole palette moves to pink, lavender and cream. A checklist on the right turns each numbered circle blue as its ingredient arrives, and the status reads plain version → adding → finished.
Spec:
- 5 s cycle. 0–0.6 s is the plain version; the 4 ingredients join from 0.6 s, the next every 0.7 s, each fading in over 0.5 s.
- The sun sits behind the person at (236, 60), with a 2.4 px rim light on the head and shoulder; the bloom reaches a 170 px radius and 9 points of light twinkle 2 times per cycle.
- The 4 near flowers grow from 0.5× to 1× and blur by 7 px; the 46 flowers, sky and ground shift to pink, lavender and cream.
- The finished look holds until 4.4 s and slides back to the plain version over 4.4–4.95 s, so 5 s and 0 s are the same frame.
When to use it: For flower-field and wedding portraits, fantasy-style photos and soft product shots.
