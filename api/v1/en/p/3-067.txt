Motion Prompt #3-067

What it is: Keep every camera on one side of the line between two people so left and right never flip
What the sample shows: A small top-down map at bottom right shows the line between the two people, the camera side in pale blue and the far side hatched as off limits; both shots stay on one side, so A is always on the left.
Spec:
- Camera 1: square on to the pair, 5 m away and 1.5 m high; A speaks from 0.5 s to 1.9 s.
- Camera 2: cut at 2.4 s, 2.2 m to the left and 2.8 m away, turned 0.702 rad, still on the same side of the line; B speaks from 2.7 s to 4.0 s.
- Top-down map, 66×66 px: a blue dashed line for the axis, a blue dot for A and an ink dot for B; the camera side is pale blue and the other side hatched and labelled "越轴".
- In the map the active camera is blue and the other grey; in the main picture A stays on the left and B on the right.
- Return: cross-fade back to the first shot over the last 0.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s).
When to use it: For dialogue, chases or stand-offs shot from several angles, so the audience never loses track of who is on which side.
