Motion Prompt #4-028

What it is: A Penrose impossible triangle assembled from falling blocks in isometric view
What the sample shows: Coloured blocks drop one by one onto a pastel ground to build 3 edges whose ends meet into an impossible triangle that slowly turns.
Spec:
- 18 blocks, 6 per edge on 3 edges, in isometric projection; the bounding circle is 196 px across.
- Blocks drop from above 1/6 s apart and settle in 0.5 s; all are in place over 0.2–3.5 s.
- Block 1 is redrawn clipped to the hexagon where it overlaps the last block, creating the illusion that the ends join.
- The whole figure makes 1 full turn per 6.4 s and floats ±4 px; it fades out over 5.4–6.0 s.
