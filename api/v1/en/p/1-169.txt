Motion Prompt #1-169

What it is: A hexagon badge draws in, a star stamps down and rays burst out
What the sample shows: A hexagon outline draws in one stroke and fills with gold; a large rotated star shrinks and stamps into the centre. Short rays shoot outward as the badge gives a small bounce, and the achievement name and 'Unlocked' rise in below.
Spec:
- The hexagon outline draws in one stroke from 0.3–1.1 s (inOut3), then fills from 1.1–1.4 s.
- The star goes from 3× and -60° to 1× and 0° from 1.4–1.9 s (backOut).
- From 1.8–2.35 s, 12 rays shoot from a 58 px to an 80 px radius and vanish while the badge bounces to 1.08×.
- The achievement name rises 8 px at 2.1 s and 'Unlocked' at 2.25 s (0.4 s out3).
- Everything fades out from 5.4–6.0 s to reset.
When to use it: Use it for gamification, learning apps and streak rewards.
