Motion Prompt #4-019

What it is: A Sankey infographic that shows energy flow through the width of each band
What the sample shows: Bands grow rightward from 4 energy sources on the left, shifting colour as they split into 4 uses on the right.
Spec:
- 4 sources on the left (Solar 120, Wind 90, Hydro 70, Gas 110) and 4 uses on the right (Industry 150, Homes 110, Transport 70, Storage 60).
- 13 bands at 0.4 px per unit, each a linear gradient from source colour to destination colour at 0.55 opacity.
- Bands flow in from left to right over 0.2–3.2 s, each starting 0.04 of progress after the previous.
- Nodes are 7 px wide with 7 px gaps, inset 62 px from each side. Everything fades out over 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Openers for annual reports or data stories, hero sections on energy and sustainability sites, or the title slide of a data section.
