Motion Prompt #1-132

What it is: An image that tilts towards the cursor while its layers shift for depth
What the sample shows: As the cursor moves over a sunset mountain image, it tilts and enlarges slightly, the sky, sun and ridges shift by different amounts, and a caption slides up from the bottom.
Spec:
- Image 280 × 150 px, corner radius 14 px; tilts up to 10° towards the cursor and scales to 1.04.
- Sky, sun, far ridge, middle ridge and foreground shift against the cursor by up to 2/3/5/10/18 px.
- A white glare (radius 120 px) follows the cursor; the caption slides up from 18 px below.
- In the demo hover starts from 0.7–1.0 s; from 4.6–4.95 s the cursor leaves and everything eases back.
When to use it: Use it on portfolios, travel or product photos and feature thumbnails to give images depth.
