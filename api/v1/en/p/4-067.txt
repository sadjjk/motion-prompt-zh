Motion Prompt #4-067

What it is: An investigative-journalism look: source text, scene picture and timeline side by side, linked by highlighter, red circle and timeline cursor, then a push-in and a verdict stamp
What the sample shows: Three panels sit on a dark gray desk: a newspaper clipping on the left, a riverside scene with a clock tower in the middle, and a 1920–1945 timeline on the right. A yellow highlighter runs over 钟楼刚刚落成, and a red pen loops a hand-drawn ellipse around the clock tower. The timeline cursor slides from 1920 and stops at 1934 钟楼落成, which lights up. The camera pushes in on the red circle, corner brackets appear, and a red-framed stamp 推定：1934 年 lands. On reset the marks and highlight wipe away and the camera pulls back.
Spec:
- Three panels: source 150 wide, scene 170 wide, timeline 148 wide.
- Marking order: highlighter 0.4 s, red circle 1.2 s, cursor 2.0–2.8 s.
- Timeline: 1920–1945, the cursor stops at 1934.
- Push-in: 3.3–3.9 s to 1.7×, centred on the red circle.
- Reset: 5.4–6.0 s marks wipe away and the camera pulls back.
When to use it: Use it for source checking, event reconstructions and debunking.
