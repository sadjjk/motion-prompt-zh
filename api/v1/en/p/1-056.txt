Please use "Thinking orb: connecting" (思考オーブ・接続中), one of the names in "Glow & liquid".
Motion Prompt #1-056

What it is: Nodes on the orb join up one by one and packets flow along the links, showing services are being connected
What the sample shows: On a turning orb, nodes are joined one link at a time like a constellation, then light packets run along the links before it all fades and restarts.
Spec:
- Orb radius 58 px: 240 faint dots plus 10 nodes; it spins once per cycle.
- 12 links are drawn one at a time from 0.15 s, every 0.17 s, each taking 0.35 s (cubic ease-out).
- At 2.4–4.0 s a light packet runs along each link, one pass every 0.8 s.
- Links and packets fade out at 4.0–4.8 s and the loop restarts. Label "Connecting sources…" at 15 px.
When to use it: Use it while connecting to outside services, linking data sources or reaching a tool server.
