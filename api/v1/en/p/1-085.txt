Please use "Cursor lens reveal" (カーソルのレンズで現れる文), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-085

What it is: A round lens at the cursor that reveals a different message underneath
What the sample shows: A round lens chases the pointer springily over a two-line headline, revealing another sentence on dark ground; a click expands it to full size, another shrinks it back.
Spec:
- 2 lines of 58 px bold text on #F5F1E8; a 54 px round lens reveals another sentence on #1D1A16, highlighted in #FFD43B / #FF8787.
- The lens follows the pointer on a spring (stiffness 260, damping 24) and stretches up to 35% along the direction of fast motion.
- In the demo, a click at 2.8 s expands the lens to the whole card at a rate of 5/s; a second click at 4.1 s shrinks it back to 54 px at 12/s.
- When the pointer leaves the area the lens shrinks to 0; its edge has a 1.5 px yellow stroke.
When to use it: Use it on brand or campaign page headlines to add the fun of discovering a hidden message.
