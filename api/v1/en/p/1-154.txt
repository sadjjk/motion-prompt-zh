Please use "Rolling text button" (ローリングテキストボタン), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects".
Motion Prompt #1-154

What it is: On hover the label rolls up letter by letter and swaps
What the sample shows: When the cursor lands, the label's characters roll up one after another and the same characters come up from below; the button flips from white to ink and the round arrow turns to point up-right.
Spec:
- A 220×56 pill button with the label on the left and a 40px round arrow on the right; the cursor moves onto it in 0.6 s.
- Each character rolls up one line (26px) over 0.55 s (easeInOut power 3), staggered 0.035 s per character, while an identical character rolls in from below.
- At the same time the button turns from white to ink, the text from ink to white, the circle from ink to accent orange, and the arrow turns from → to ↗ (−45°).
- The cursor leaves at 3.4 s and everything rolls back in reverse.
When to use it: Use it for the main buttons of portfolio or brand sites where the CTA should feel playful.
