Motion Prompt #2-070

What it is: Text with a rippling upside-down reflection below
What the sample shows: Under a night sky white text floats gently; below the line a coral upside-down copy sways, skews and breaks into horizontal ripples.
Spec:
- Above a horizon line (y = 118, a 1 px line brightest in the middle) sits white text; below it a vertically flipped coral copy.
- The copy skews ±7°, stretches 0.92–1.04x vertically and sways ±3 px on a 2.35 s cycle.
- The copy fades from 0.55 to 0 opacity over 80 px below the line and is split into 3 px bands shifted ±2.5 px to read as ripples.
- The text above bobs 2.5 px on the 2.35 s cycle while background stars twinkle slowly.
When to use it: Use it for calm titles about night, lakes or quiet brands, or a gentle end card.
