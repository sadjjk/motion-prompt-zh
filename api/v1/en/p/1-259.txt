Motion Prompt #1-259

What it is: A dark card where only a diamond lattice glows rainbow and a bright band sweeps diagonally across it
What the sample shows: A diamond lattice glows rainbow on a near-black card and a bright band passes diagonally each time it sways; a gold four-point star sits in the middle.
Spec:
- Card 126 × 168 px (3:4), corner radius 0.045 × width, thickness 0.036 × width; it swings on its own in a 6.4 s loop: yaw 24°·sin a, pitch 11.5°·sin 2a, roll 1.6°·sin a and a 1.3 % bob.
- Two |sin| directions form the lattice lines (width 0.24), modulated by a bright band of cos to the power 1.6; hue = u × 1.35 + v × 1.10 + tilt × 1.7. The emblem is a gold four-point star on a gold frame.
- Layers from back to front: base pattern (−0.082 W), emblem (−0.03 W), then surface foil and a bevelled metal frame; tilting shifts each layer by its depth, so the card reads as deep.
- The light direction turns with the angle and a diagonal sheen crosses the face; cross-star glints twinkle on frame corners and the emblem tip.
When to use it: Use it for black cards, invitations and limited items that should look quietly luxurious.
