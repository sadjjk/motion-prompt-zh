Motion Prompt #1-238

What it is: A black pill running a timer grows into its activity card on a slightly overshooting spring when pressed, and tucks back on a second press
What the sample shows: In the middle of a phone-wallpaper gradient running from orange through magenta to purple sits a small black pill with an amber timer glyph on the left and the time left, 4:59, on the right, ticking down each second. The pointer comes in from the right and presses it: the pill dips, then grows wider and taller at once, overshoots a little and settles as a large black rounded card. The small contents blur, shrink and fade, and a beat later an amber round pause button appears on the left with a grey Timer and a large amber time on the right. The pointer waits over the card; a second press brings it back to the small pill on the same spring, the contents swapping again.
Spec:
- The shut pill is 150×36 px with 18 px corners and the open card 300×76 px with 38 px corners, staying black #000 and growing out evenly from the middle of an orange-to-purple wallpaper gradient.
- Width, height and corners ride one spring: stiffness 380, damping 34 lowered by bounce 40 to 27.6, mass 0.9.
- The contents are two separate layouts that crossfade after 0.06 s over 0.22 s, entering and leaving through a 6 px blur and a scale from 0.92.
- The pointer enters from the right at 0.3 s and presses the pill at 1.2 s; it presses again at 3.8 s to tuck it back and is gone by 4.6 s.
- While pressed the island dips to 0.97×; the time left, 4:59, drops by 1 every 1 s; from 5.4 s to 6.0 s it dissolves back to the first picture.
When to use it: Use it to show ongoing activities such as timers, calls, music playback and delivery or trip progress.
