Motion Prompt #1-076

What it is: A hero where a tilted product screen rises upright as you scroll
What the sample shows: Scrolling lifts the backward-tilted product window upright as it grows and its shadow deepens; once flat, the chart bars rise in sequence.
Spec:
- The product window starts at .84 scale tilted back 34° (perspective 900 px) and stands up along 1−(1−p)² over 0→210 px of scroll.
- As it rises it scales to 1 and moves up 18 px, with the shadow deepening from 10/20 px to 34/54 px.
- Once upright (p > .88) its 8 bars grow in turn: .7 s each, 50 ms apart, cubic-bezier(.3,1.3,.5,1); scrolling back drops them to .15.
- Before scrolling, a 'Scroll' hint bobs 4 px every 1.6 s; demo scrolls over 0.4→2.6 s and back over 5.4→6.3 s.
When to use it: Use it in SaaS and data-product heroes to 'stand up' the product screenshot as the visitor scrolls.
