Motion Prompt #3-100

What it is: A picture sized to the screen ratio, keeping the clock and icon areas quiet and placing the subject where nothing covers it
What the sample shows: The phone screen shows a pale-blue sky, layered hills and a person standing in front of them. The lock-screen date, a large "09:41" clock, then 2 round buttons and a bar at the bottom appear in turn. Next a white dashed box marks the clock and an amber dashed box the person, with leader lines to "time area" and "subject", and ✓ "keep the time and icon areas clear · subject low and centre". A smaller phone on the right is the counter-example: the person is placed high and is crushed under the big clock (✕).
Spec:
- 5 s cycle. The centre phone is 184 px tall; the picture appears over 0.3–0.7 s, the date and "09:41" clock over 1.0–1.5 s and the bottom buttons over 1.6–2.0 s.
- Over 2.3–2.9 s the dashed boxes for the clock and subject, their leader-line labels and the ✓ verdict appear.
- Over 3.1–3.6 s a 136 px phone shows the ✕ counter-example.
- Everything clears over 4.4–4.95 s, so 5 s and 0 s are the same frame.
When to use it: For phone and desktop wallpapers, so the clock and icons never cover the subject.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/en/e/3-100.txt
Shared code it depends on: https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/kit/photo.js
