Motion Prompt #1-211

What it is: A background of swirling paint quantised into four colours of chunky pixels; the cursor stirs it and a click makes a whirlpool
What the sample shows: Chunky four-colour pixel paint swirls around the centre; the cursor twists the paint as it passes, and a click opens a whirlpool that then fades.
Spec:
- A 108×42 low-res buffer, each pixel 5 px, with smoothing off.
- Coordinates rotate around the centre by 2.4·e^(−1.4r) plus one full turn per cycle, then two layers of sine domain warping are applied.
- Values are quantised by 4 thresholds into navy, blue, red and pale pink.
- The cursor enters at 0.4–1.0 s and turns the paint near it an extra 1.8·e^(−d²/0.05) radians.
- A click at 3.6 s spins up a whirlpool of up to 5 radians that rises and settles over 1.4 s.
When to use it: Use it as a hero background for card games, indie games or playful brands.
