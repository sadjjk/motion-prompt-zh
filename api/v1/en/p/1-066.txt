Motion Prompt #1-066

What it is: Spoiler text hidden under shimmering sand that blows away on tap
What the sample shows: Fine grains flicker over part of a chat bubble; a tap scatters them from that point to reveal the words, and 'hide' gathers them back.
Spec:
- Spoiler phrases are covered in grains: 1 per 9 square pixels, .6–1.5 px wide, flickering between .45–1 brightness and jittering ±.8 px.
- A tap bursts the grains outward from the tap point at 2–5 px/frame; each frame speed ×.94 and opacity −.025.
- The text starts appearing .1 s after the tap and is fully shown after .5 s.
- 'Hide again' pulls grains back from 60 px out, closing 12% per frame, as the text fades; demo reveals at 1.2 and 2.6 s, hides at 4.2 s.
When to use it: Use it in chat apps, comment threads and community posts to hide spoilers, answers or sensitive details.
