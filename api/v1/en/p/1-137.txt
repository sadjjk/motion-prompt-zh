Please use "Send plane button" (紙飛行機の送信ボタン), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-137

What it is: A send button whose paper plane flies off as the label changes to Sent
What the sample shows: The purple button dips and recovers, its label slides off to the right, and the paper plane winds back before shooting off to the top right; the button turns green with a sent label sliding in, then everything returns and the plane flies back.
Spec:
- Button 210 × 54 px; pressed at 0.95 s, it dips to 0.94 and springs back.
- From 1.0–1.3 s the label moves 16 px right as it fades; the plane winds back 6 px from 1.0–1.2 s.
- From 1.2–1.8 s it accelerates with t³ to the top right (+300 px, −60 px, rotating −25°), clipped by the button.
- From 1.75–2.05 s the button turns green and the sent label slides in from the left; from 3.3–3.9 s the original label returns and the plane flies back from 40 px left and 24 px below.
When to use it: Use it on message, contact-form and newsletter submit buttons to confirm sending with some delight.
