Motion Prompt #1-099

What it is: A popup grows out of the button that opened it
What the sample shows: The same menu side by side: on the left it swells from its own centre in mid-air; on the right it grows out of the "More" button and shrinks back into it.
Spec:
- Two identical "More" buttons (92×34) and menus (200×82), left and right; only transform-origin differs. Left clicks at 0.9 s, right at 1.8 s.
- Open amount a = easeOutCubic over 0.3 s. Scale 0.5 + 0.5·a, opacity a.
- Left origin is the menu centre (140, 91), so it swells in mid-air; right origin is the midpoint of the button's top edge (341, 140), so it grows out of the button.
- Close: left at 2.9 s, right at 3.5 s, clicking the button again; a is multiplied by (1 − easeInOutCubic) over 0.2 s, shrinking to the same origin.
- A blue cross marks each origin; dashed guides from the origin to the menu corners show only while animating. Versus Scale in: the scaling centre is anchored on the trigger.
When to use it: Use it for dropdowns, popovers and context menus opened from a button, so it is clear where they came from.
