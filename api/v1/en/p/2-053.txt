Please use "Cel animation / line boil" (手描きアニメ・線のゆらぎ), one of the names in "MG styles".
Motion Prompt #2-053

What it is: Hand-drawn cel style: low frame rate and lines that jitter (boil) slightly every frame
What the sample shows: A match-flame sprite squashes and bounces, smoke puffs rise and star sparks pop, while the hand-lettered yellow HAND MADE keeps boiling.
Spec:
- Paper #f4e9d0 with ink #1c1613, red #e5402b and yellow #ffc425; everything is stepped to 12 fps, and the line art cycles 4 slightly different drawings frame by frame.
- The brush varies in width and tapers to 45% at both ends; fills are offset 2 px from the lines.
- The flame sprite bounces with a 14% squash every 0.8 s, 7 star sparks pop every 1.6 s, and 3 smoke puffs rise over 3.2 s.
- HAND MADE letters are 40 px tall, drawn as a 12 px black outline under a 6 px yellow stroke, with a 4 px block shadow.
When to use it: Use it for handmade-feel brand films, kids' content, craft or food ads and warm social shorts.
