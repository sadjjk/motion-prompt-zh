Motion Prompt #2-051

What it is: Morphing style: a single shape keeps transforming into new shapes in time with the beat
What the sample shows: The main shape squeezes back to a circle before opening into the next form, wobbling as it turns, while the background wipes in a new colour and the caption swaps.
Spec:
- The shape changes every 0.8 s (2 beats at 150 BPM): a 0.35 s hold plus a 0.45 s morph; 8 forms make one cycle.
- Each morph runs A → circle → B in 2 halves, each with inOut3; 120 vertices, base radius 62 px.
- During a morph it squashes and stretches by up to 13% and swings 0.35 rad.
- Palette #16151B / #F4EEE3 / #FFB627 / #FF4F2B / #2438E8; the background wipes with a 600 px circle from the centre, and the 40 px caption slides 24 px up as it swaps.
When to use it: Use it for beat-synced social shorts, brand key-visual sequences and title sequences that cycle through themes.
