Motion Prompt #1-114

What it is: Switching monthly and yearly rolls the prices to new values
What the sample shows: The cursor clicks Yearly: the knob slides over, the three prices roll down card by card and the recommended middle card grows with a blue outline; later it switches back to Monthly.
Spec:
- A 160×30 Monthly / Yearly pill toggle and 3 price cards (150×124, 18 apart).
- The cursor clicks Yearly at 1.0 s and the knob slides right over 0.3 s (easeInOut power 3).
- Card by card (0.08 s apart, 0.4 s each) the prices roll up to their new values: ¥29 / 59 / 99 → ¥23 / 47 / 79.
- The recommended middle card scales to 1.06 with a 2px blue outline over 0.35 s, and a "Save 20%" badge fades in beside the toggle.
- At 3.2 s Monthly is clicked and everything switches back the same way.
When to use it: Use it on SaaS pricing pages where plans change price with the billing period.
