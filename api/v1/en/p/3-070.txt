Please use "Prism effect" (プリズム), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #3-070

What it is: Rainbow-coloured, offset ghosts appear next to the bright areas
What the sample shows: On the night street only the windows and bulbs split into six colours from red to violet, offset towards the upper left; a faint rainbow band slants across.
Spec:
- Scene: the night street corner; only the bright parts (windows, bulbs #FFE3A3) are drawn on a separate layer.
- It is recoloured to six colours #FF4D4D, #FF9F3D, #FFE14D, #5CE27A, #4DB8FF, #9B6BFF; colour i is shifted (i+1)·3.12 px left and (i+1)·2.4 px up and added at 0.5 opacity.
- Rainbow band: a 70 px slanted band moves from x −160 to 260 once per pass (opacity 0.16).
- Dark areas do not change; top right reads "PRISM".
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For night scenes, parties and music videos that need a sparkling, dreamlike touch.
