Please use "Scratch reveal" (スクラッチ), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-134

What it is: A scratch card whose coating rubs away to reveal what is underneath
What the sample shows: Over a coupon covered by a silver coating, the cursor holds and zigzags side to side; its path rubs the coating away to show the free-shipping coupon, and the rest then fades.
Spec:
- A 300 × 140 px coupon is covered by a silver coating with diagonal sheen and a scratch-here label.
- The cursor presses at 0.85 s and scratches along 7 zigzag strokes (0.32 s each) until 3.0 s, with a 30 px round brush.
- Each frame redraws the coating on an offscreen canvas and erases the whole path up to that moment with destination-out.
- The remaining coating fades out from 3.1–3.6 s; it fades back in to reset from 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it for lucky draws, coupon drops and campaign result reveals that should feel like a game.
