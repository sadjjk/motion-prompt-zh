Please use "Confetti burst" (紙吹雪), one of the names in "Styled scenes".
Motion Prompt #4-036

What it is: Confetti shoots up as the title springs in
What the sample shows: On a dark purple background the word "恭喜" dips and springs back while confetti in 5 colors fans up from below, spinning and falling in arcs before fading; it repeats every 3.2 s.
Spec:
- A 64px bold "恭喜" shrinks to 0.6× over 0.25 s, then springs back to 1× over 0.8 s with elastic.out(1, 0.4).
- As the shrink ends, 60 confetti pieces of 8×5 in 5 colors burst from 62% of the card height, at 187–374px/s and angles of 215°–325°.
- They fall in arcs under 395px/s² of gravity, rotate ±540° over 2 s, and flip 1–2 times per second (vertical scale driven by cos).
- Each piece lives 2 s and fades out over 1.5–2.0 s.
- A burst fires every 3.2 s, 2 per 6.4 s cycle; all pieces are gone and the title is back at 1× before the next burst.
When to use it: Use it to celebrate a milestone, a win, a birthday or a launch announcement.
