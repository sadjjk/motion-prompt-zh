Motion Prompt #3-063

What it is: Foreground, middle ground and background are laid out in layers for depth
What the sample shows: The lake and woods behind, the person in the middle and the flowers in front slide sideways by different amounts, creating parallax; each layer is labelled.
Spec:
- Background: far hills, 6 trees across the water and the lake surface, sliding by 6 px.
- Middle ground: the person at x 270, 66 px tall, sliding by 18 px.
- Foreground: 4 clumps of flowers along the bottom edge sliding by 48 px; the nearer the layer, the more it moves.
- The sideways slide follows one sine per pass and grows with the effect; each layer carries a moving label "后景", "中景" or "前景".
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For landscapes, travel and story pictures that should show depth on a flat image.
