Motion Prompt #4-035

What it is: A sweep beam drawing a cyan waveform across a dark scope screen
What the sample shows: A dark scope panel fades in and a vertical light beam travels left to right, tracing a cyan waveform that swings 3 times with shrinking height, then the beam fades out.
Spec:
- A dark panel, 511×188 (#04111d, border #164e63), with a 44×16 grid and a 467×120 waveform box.
- The panel fades in and rises 6px over 0.3–0.96 s (quadratic ease-out); a vertical sweep beam switches on over 0.84–1.08 s.
- Over 0.96–4.08 s the waveform and beam advance together at a constant rate from left to right; the cyan #22d3ee trace swings 3 times with shrinking amplitude. The beam fades over 4.08–4.44 s.
- Over 4.2–5.4 s the panel lifts 2px with a sine ease-in-out; everything fades over 5.4–6.0 s, back to the empty background by 6.4 s.
- Versus Sci-fi HUD: a single waveform is traced gradually by a sweep beam, like a live measurement, rather than many gauges ticking at once.
When to use it: Use it for electronics, test equipment, audio or medical device promos, transitions in science and tech videos, or a loading screen.
