Motion Prompt #1-257

What it is: A holographic card tiled with small squares that each hold their own rainbow hue and brighten and dim separately with the angle
What the sample shows: As a deep teal card sways, its mosaic tiles flicker rainbow out of step with one another; a silver hexagon sits in the middle.
Spec:
- Card 126 × 168 px (3:4), corner radius 0.045 × width, thickness 0.036 × width; it swings on its own in a 6.4 s loop: yaw 24°·sin a, pitch 11.5°·sin 2a, roll 1.6°·sin a and a 1.3 % bob.
- The face is split into 9 × 12 tiles; a per-tile hash sets the hue and flash phase (cos to the power 2.2), and the joints are white at 0.25 opacity. The emblem is a silver hexagon on a silver frame.
- Layers from back to front: base pattern (−0.082 W), emblem (−0.03 W), then surface foil and a bevelled metal frame; tilting shifts each layer by its depth, so the card reads as deep.
- The light direction turns with the angle and a diagonal sheen crosses the face; cross-star glints twinkle on frame corners and the emblem tip.
When to use it: Use it for collection grids, tech or game cards and digital membership passes.
