Please use "Macro shot" (マクロ撮影), one of the names in "Camera moves & transitions".
Motion Prompt #3-056

What it is: A very small subject shot so it fills the frame
What the sample shows: A mushroom about 0.26 m tall fills the frame; focus pulls from soft to sharp so the white spots on the cap, the gills and a bug crawling along the rim come through, while the tree trunks and a person's legs behind stay blurred.
Spec:
- Frame: from 0.02 m below the ground to 0.26 m; camera close to the ground, 0.9 m from the mushroom.
- Focus: pulls from soft to sharp from 0.4 s to 1.1 s and goes soft again from 4.3 s to 4.7 s.
- Mushroom: cap red-brown #C34F40 with a #D97B57 middle ring and a #E7E0CE centre, three rings of white spots, radial gills under the cap and two fine lines on the stem.
- Bug: crawls along the rim from right to left and back once per pass; its legs move faster or slower with its speed.
- The two trees and the person's legs behind always stay blurred; top right reads "微距 MACRO"; one pass = 4.7 s.
When to use it: For insects, flowers, food or product textures, details the eye barely sees.
