Please use "Cursor photo trail" (カーソルの写真の軌跡), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-083

What it is: A playful effect that drops photos along the cursor's path
What the sample shows: As the pointer crosses a large heading, white-framed photos pop in at random angles and sink away; a click fans out 5 photos at once.
Spec:
- Every 46 px of pointer movement drops a 58 × 72 px white-framed photo, rotated by a random ±12° plus horizontal movement × 0.06.
- Each photo springs in from 0.3× over 0.32 s, then after 0.9 s shrinks, sinks 14 px and fades out over 0.45 s.
- At most 14 photos are visible; beyond that the oldest starts fading early.
- In the demo, a click at 3.5 s fans out 5 photos (0.06 s apart, 16 px and 12° per step, lasting 1.4 s).
When to use it: Use it in the hero of photographer, travel or event sites to add play around the main heading.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/en/e/1-083.txt
Shared helper code: https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/kit/uxmotion.js
