Motion Prompt #1-254

What it is: A holographic card filled with glitter and silk ribbons that slide towards the low side as it tilts, like quicksand
What the sample shows: A deep purple card sways slowly on a dark stand while gold, pink and cyan sequins and pink-gold silk inside flow and sparkle like quicksand.
Spec:
- Card 126 × 168 px (3:4), corner radius 0.045 × width, thickness 0.036 × width; it swings on its own in a 6.4 s loop: yaw 24°·sin a, pitch 11.5°·sin 2a, roll 1.6°·sin a and a 1.3 % bob.
- About 260 sequins (gold, pink, cyan, white) on a 15 × 19 grid drift with gravity, each circling slightly and flashing sharply by its normal against the light's half vector; two silk ribbons undulate. The base carries a faint starburst foil and the emblem is a gold five-point star.
- Layers from back to front: base pattern (−0.082 W), emblem (−0.03 W), then surface foil and a bevelled metal frame; tilting shifts each layer by its depth, so the card reads as deep.
- The light direction turns with the angle and a diagonal sheen crosses the face; cross-star glints twinkle on frame corners and the emblem tip.
When to use it: Use it for fan merchandise, acrylic stands and trading-card showcases or a gacha prize reveal.
