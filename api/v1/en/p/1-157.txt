Motion Prompt #1-157

What it is: The right arrow slides out as a new one slides in from the left
What the sample shows: When the cursor lands, the arrow on the right of the button slides out and fades while a matching arrow slides in from outside the left edge, and the label moves right to make room.
Spec:
- A 180×50 ink pill button with the label and an arrow on its right; the cursor moves onto it in 0.6 s.
- Over 0.4 s (easeInOut power 3) the right arrow slides 40px out to the right and fades out.
- A second arrow slides in from outside the left edge (−16px) to just left of the label (+30px) and fades in, while the label moves 20px right.
- The cursor leaves at 3.4 s and everything slides back.
When to use it: Use it on buttons that lead onward, such as 'Next' or 'Learn more'.
