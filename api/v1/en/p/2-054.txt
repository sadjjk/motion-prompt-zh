Motion Prompt #2-054

What it is: Isometric 2.5D: a miniature world seen from above at a fixed angle, with no vanishing point
What the sample shows: On a lavender floating island, shadows land first, then pastel buildings spring up one after another, roofs drop on, windows light and data pulses between towers.
Spec:
- True isometric at 30°, 28 px per tile, a 12 × 4 tile island; outline colour #2C2960 with 6 pastel tones.
- 14 buildings (8 back, 6 front) rise from 0.4 s, staggered by 0.25 s, each growing over 0.4 s with backOut, and the roof drops on 0.1 s later.
- Windows light up one by one at 3.6–4.6 s; from 4.2 s a cyan #62F0F2 data stream runs between towers (4 trips per cycle).
- 2 cars drive in opposite directions, 2 laps per cycle, and 5 trees pop up in turn; buildings shrink back into the ground at 5.4–6.0 s.
