What it is: 1980s retro-futurism: a neon sunset, a perspective grid and videotape picture quality
What the sample shows: A striped sun sits behind wireframe mountains, a magenta grid rolls toward camera and palms whip past; a chrome title slams down with a white flash and a neon script flickers on.
Spec:
- Deep purple night sky #060212→#751266 with stars. The striped sun is a 5-stop gradient #fff59e→#ff3d8f; the cut-out bands on its lower half widen downward (up to 58%) and slide down 8 bands per cycle.

- A VHS PLAY ▶ overlay shows at 0.1–1.15 s. At 1.2 s the chrome title NEON DRIVE slams down from 2.1× (in 0.13 s) with a white flash; a highlight sweeps across at 1.8–2.4 s.
