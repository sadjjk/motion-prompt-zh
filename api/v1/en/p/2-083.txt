What it is: A tree grows from trunk to branches to leaves
What the sample shows: A ground line spreads, the trunk grows up, 4 branches and 5 twigs sprout in turn, leaves pop open in random order, and a caption rises below.
Spec:
- Over 0.2–0.6 s a ground line grows outward from the centre (cubic ease-out).
- From 0.45 s the trunk (9 px) grows in 0.8 s; 4 branches (5/4 px) follow 0.15 s apart, 0.45 s each; 5 twigs (2.5 px) 0.12 s apart, 0.35 s each (quadratic ease-in-out).
- From 2.6 s, 14 leaves pop from 0 to 1 in random order, 0.07 s apart, 0.32 s each (back 1.7).
- Over 3.4–3.8 s the caption rises 6 px while fading in; everything fades over 4.15–4.6 s.
