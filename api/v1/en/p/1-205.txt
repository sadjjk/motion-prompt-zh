Motion Prompt #1-205

What it is: A wall of letters that flicker, swap and change colour in place; the cursor scrambles them and a click tears rows apart
What the sample shows: 49×13 monospace characters flicker and swap at random in 4 greens and violets; the area around the cursor scrambles white, and a click tears nearby rows sideways with a magenta flash.
Spec:
- A 49×13 grid of 13 px monospace characters, coloured from a 4-colour palette.
- Each cell changes its character 2–8 times per cycle (count and phase fixed by index), flashing bright and dimming over 0.18 s.
- Characters within 60 px of the cursor scramble 20 times a second and turn whitish.
- A click at 3.5 s shifts rows within 46 px above and below by up to ±30 px and turns some cells magenta, decaying over 0.25 s.
- A dark vignette darkens the edges.
When to use it: Use it on hacker-style, gaming or security pages that want a calm but uneasy background.
