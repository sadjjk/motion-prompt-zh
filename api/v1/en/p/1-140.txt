Please use "Circle reveal transition" (円形リビール), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-140

What it is: A circular mask grows from the centre with scrolling to reveal the next section
What the sample shows: As scrolling progresses, a dark chapter 2 grows as a circle from the centre of the light chapter 1 until it covers the card; the first title shrinks away while the second settles from larger to normal size, and scrolling back shrinks the circle.
Spec:
- Scroll progress goes 0 → 1 from 0.5–4.4 s (inOut3), with the scrollbar on the right in sync.
- The circular mask radius grows in proportion to progress, from 0 to half the card diagonal.
- The first chapter's title shrinks to 0.8 as it fades; the second chapter's title shrinks from 1.2 to 1.
- From 5.4–6.3 s the scroll returns and the circle shrinks back.
When to use it: Use it on story-led pages and product tours to mark a change of chapter or scene.
