Motion Prompt #1-209

What it is: A background that draws soft drifting glows using only ASCII characters chosen by brightness
What the sample shows: Three coloured glows drift across a 77×17 grid of monospace characters, brightness shown by how dense each character is, and characters scramble and glow white around the cursor.
Spec:
- A 77×17 grid of monospace characters, each cell 7×12 px.
- Three glows (orange, magenta, cyan; radius 95–120 px) drift on Lissajous paths with whole-number frequencies, so the loop closes.
- Brightness I = Σ e^(−d²/r²) picks a character from the ramp " .`-:;=+*#%@"; colour is the weighted mix of the glows.
- The cursor enters at 0.4–1.0 s and leaves at 4.5–5.2 s; characters within 55 px scramble 15 times a second and turn white.
When to use it: Use it behind developer tools, terminal-style sites or retro tech brands.
