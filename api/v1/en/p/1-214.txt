Motion Prompt #1-214

What it is: A background wall of tilted cards whose rows drift in opposite directions, shift with the cursor and lift the card beneath it
What the sample shows: Five rows of cards tilted −12° drift in alternating directions; as the cursor moves the rows shift by depth-like amounts, and the card right under it lifts and glows.
Spec:
- Five rows of 92×58 px rounded cards spaced 104 apart; the whole wall tilts −12°.
- Odd and even rows drift in opposite directions by 2 or 3 cards per cycle; colours repeat every 2 or 3 cards, so the loop is seamless.
- The cursor enters at 0.4–1.0 s; each row shifts by (cursor x − centre)·(0.12 + 0.07·row).
- The card under the cursor brightens, scales 1.08 times and gets a white outline.
- The cursor leaves at 4.6–5.3 s and the shift returns to 0; the left and right edges fade to the background.
When to use it: Use it as a hero for app galleries, template stores or portfolios that want to show lots of content at once.
