Motion Prompt #1-062

What it is: A circular text badge that spins and speeds up on hover and click
What the sample shows: Text on a ring spins slowly, accelerates when the pointer arrives, and jumps faster on each click as new words fade in letter by letter.
Spec:
- Monospaced letters are set around a ring of radius 84 px and spin at a steady 18°/s.
- On hover the target speed is 150°/s (closing 6% of the gap per frame); after the pointer leaves it coasts back to 18°/s at 2.5% per frame.
- Each click adds 120°/s and swaps the text; new letters fade in from 120 ms, staggered 22 ms apart, each over .25 s.
- The centre button grows to 1.06× on hover and its arrow turns from -45° to upright; a click squeezes it to .78 at 30% of a .6 s bounce.
When to use it: Use it as a corner badge or CTA on portfolio and brand-site heroes, such as 'Scroll to explore' or 'Open for projects'.
