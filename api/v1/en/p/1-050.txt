Motion Prompt #1-050

What it is: A light sweeps back and forth along the bottom edge of a field to show a search is running
What the sample shows: Only the bottom edge of a rounded search field lights up, and the light glides from left to right and back.
Spec:
- Search field 380 × 48 px, corner radius 24 px. Only the lower half of the border lights up.
- The light centre travels between 60 px from each end: right at 0.1–2.3 s, back left at 2.5–4.7 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- The lit band is 180 px wide, brightest at the centre: a 6 px layer with 14 px blur plus a 1.6 px core.
- A soft flattened glow sits under the edge (radius 70 px, squashed to 0.35 height, opacity 0.28).
When to use it: Use it on a search field, command bar or ask box between submitting and getting results.
