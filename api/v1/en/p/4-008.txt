Motion Prompt #4-008

What it is: A sci-fi heads-up display of turning concentric dials and live readouts
What the sample shows: Cyan concentric rings turn in opposite directions on deep navy while a scan sector sweeps and the central readout and side data keep changing.
Spec:
- Centred at (200, 104) with 8 concentric rings; 6 of them turn 0.5–3 laps per 6.4 s in mixed directions.
- An outer scale of 120 ticks (a 6 px long tick every 10); an inner scan sector turns 2 laps per 6.4 s.
- The central readout swings between 75 and 99 along a sine, set in 28 px thin type.
- 6 data rows CH-1 to CH-6 on the right rise and fall 2 times per 6.4 s; lines rgba(90, 220, 255) on #04101C.
When to use it: Use it to open tech launch videos, as a hero background for games or sci-fi titles, or as an idle screen for data dashboards.
