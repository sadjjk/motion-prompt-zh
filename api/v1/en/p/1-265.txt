Motion Prompt #1-265

What it is: A background of dark gold satin filling the screen, its folds rolling slowly as if in a breeze while a soft sheen slides along the ridges
What the sample shows: Dark gold cloth covers the screen and its folds slowly change shape as they roll. Soft light runs along the ridges, the hollows sink into shadow, and a fine grain covers it all.
Spec:
- No simulation: fold height = 0.62 · sin(6 (0.82 q + 0.57 y) + a + 1.5 sin(3q − 1.7y − a)) + 0.38 · sin(3.2 (0.5 q − 0.87 y) + 2a + 0.9 sin(2.4y + a)), with q = x + 0.2 sin(2.1y + 0.5x + a) and a = 2π·u / 6.4.
- Computed on a 270×104 grid (about 2 px per cell) and scaled up.
- Normals: height gradient × 0.22; diffuse from a light at (−0.45, −0.55, 0.7) raised to the power 1.3, and hollows darkened by up to 40% by height.
- Sheen: half-vector specular with exponent 28, adding #FFE5BD at 0.55.
- Colour mixes #0D0906 to #7C5631 by the light, then brightness × 1 ± 7% grain per pixel.
When to use it: Hero sections for luxury brands, cosmetics or jewellery, and backgrounds for invitations or events.
