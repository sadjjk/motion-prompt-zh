Motion Prompt #2-131

What it is: Branches reach out one by one from a central idea and the whole map turns slowly, showing an idea spreading in many directions
What the sample shows: A glowing gold circle 「想法」 sits in the centre. 6 branches spring out one by one clockwise from the top, each with a label beside its end dot. 3 faint concentric ellipses sit behind, and the whole map turns slowly.
Spec:
- A gold centre circle of radius 26 px labelled 「想法」 springs in at 0.1 s with a soft glow; 3 faint gold concentric ellipses stay behind it the whole time.
- 6 branches 「写作」「视频」「设计」「播客」「咨询」「编程」 are spaced evenly around an ellipse 340 × 144 px, starting from the top and going clockwise; branch i springs out from the centre at 0.4 + 0.25·i s.
- Each branch is a darker gold line ending in a gold dot of radius 7 px; its 15 px label sits just outside the dot and fades in over 0.3 s.
- The map turns by 0.12 rad per second; at 5.4–6.0 s it fades out and the angle eases back to 0, so at 0 s the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it to show at a glance the uses, ideas or related fields that grow out of one theme.
