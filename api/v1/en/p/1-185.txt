Motion Prompt #1-185

What it is: A short notification you can drag down to dismiss
What the sample shows: On a warm grey surface a dark toast, 「已复制链接」, bounces down from the top and the bar along its bottom begins to shrink. The cursor presses and the bar freezes; dragging down shrinks and fades the card while a hint below reads 「下滑关闭」, switching to 「松手即关闭」 past the threshold. On release the card speeds away downward; then 「已保存草稿」 drops in, runs out its timer and lifts away.
Spec:
- 542×191 card, 4.5 s cycle. A dark 300×64 toast with radius 16 rests 22 px from the top; a thin bar along its bottom shows the time left.
- The first toast drops in from above over 0.15–0.60 s with a small overshoot, and its 3.0 s countdown starts at 0.60 s.
- Pressing at 1.10 s pauses the countdown. Over 1.15–1.80 s the card follows the cursor down 74 px, shrinking by up to 13% and fading by up to 42%, while its shadow blur moves from 7 to 22.
- Past 46 px the hint below changes from 「下滑关闭」 to 「松手即关闭」. Released at 1.80 s, the card speeds up and drops 150 px out of view by 2.20 s.
- The next toast, 「已保存草稿」, drops in over 2.30–2.75 s, its countdown ends at 3.95 s and it lifts away by 4.25 s.
When to use it: Use it for copy, save or send confirmations that users should be able to flick away themselves.
