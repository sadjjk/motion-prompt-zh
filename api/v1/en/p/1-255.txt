Motion Prompt #1-255

What it is: A holographic card covered in shattered-ice facets that each flash rainbow at their own angle
What the sample shows: As a navy card sways, ice shards light up rainbow one by one and fade while white cracks glint; a silver crystal sits in the middle.
Spec:
- Card 126 × 168 px (3:4), corner radius 0.045 × width, thickness 0.036 × width; it swings on its own in a 6.4 s loop: yaw 24°·sin a, pitch 11.5°·sin 2a, roll 1.6°·sin a and a 1.3 % bob.
- The face splits into 10 × 13 Voronoi shards, each with a random normal; it flashes rainbow by its angle to the half vector (power 7.5) and the cracks between shards are white. The emblem is a silver diamond crystal on a silver frame.
- Layers from back to front: base pattern (−0.082 W), emblem (−0.03 W), then surface foil and a bevelled metal frame; tilting shifts each layer by its depth, so the card reads as deep.
- The light direction turns with the angle and a diagonal sheen crosses the face; cross-star glints twinkle on frame corners and the emblem tip.
When to use it: Use it for rare cards, winter or ice-themed rewards and limited-edition highlights.
