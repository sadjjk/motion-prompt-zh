Motion Prompt #2-052

What it is: Bauhaus-style geometry: circles, triangles and squares in primary colours, built up on a grid
What the sample shows: On gridded paper, each beat adds a move — a red circle drops, a black bar pulls down, a triangle flips, a square grows from a corner — until BAUHAUS cuts in.
Spec:
- Paper #F1E9DA with a 52 px grid (10 columns × 4 rows); red #E03C31, yellow #F2B705, blue #1E4FA3, black #111111.
- One move per 0.5 s beat at 120 BPM: each starts 0.25 s early and lands exactly on the beat with power2 inOut, no overshoot.
- 9 moves from the red circle at 0.5 s to the rule at 4.5 s, all landing on grid lines; the grid itself draws in during 0–0.5 s.
- BAUHAUS (21 px) hard-cuts in at 5.0 s with a beat counter bottom left; the page fades back to blank at 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it for design exhibition or museum promos, brand-philosophy films and beat-driven typographic title sequences.
