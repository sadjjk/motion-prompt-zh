Motion Prompt #1-097

What it is: A time-based bubble chart where bubbles travel along their paths quarter by quarter
What the sample shows: Behind a large quarter label, app bubbles glide across the chart while changing size, one leaving a dotted trail; dragging fast-forwards or rewinds time.
Spec:
- 8 app bubbles with radius 4 + √revenue × 2.6; x is monthly users on a log scale, y is 30-day retention from 10–70%.
- Keyframes sit at quarters 0, 8, 16 and 23, interpolated with smoothstep, and user counts interpolate logarithmically.
- In the demo it autoplays from 0.2–2.8 s at 2.4 quarters per second; dragging the background moves 1 quarter per 8 px.
- The photo-editing app leaves a dotted trail with a dot per quarter; the large background label runs from 2021 Q1 to 2026 Q4.
When to use it: Use it in market analysis, investor decks and annual-report data stories that show several metrics changing over time.
