Please use "Collapsible thinking" (思考過程の折りたたみ), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-191

What it is: AI reasoning steps list up while thinking, then fold into one line as the answer appears
What the sample shows: Next to the AI avatar a card counts up the thinking time while reasoning steps appear one at a time from below, each new line swept by a purple shine. When thinking ends the title becomes “Thought for 4.8 s” with a chevron, the steps stack upward from the bottom and vanish, the card shrinks and the first line of the answer rises in.
Spec:
- From 0.2–3.4 s the title timer counts from 0.0 to 4.8 s while an arc spins around the avatar.
- 4 reasoning steps appear every 0.7 s from 0.45 s, each rising 6 px in 0.35 s; a purple shine crosses each new line over 0.85 s.
- At 3.4 s the title cross-fades in 0.28 s to “Thought for 4.8 s” and a chevron appears.
- From 3.62–4.36 s the steps fold up to the 1st row, bottom one first and 0.06 s apart, squashing to 0.55× and fading; the card shrinks and the chevron turns to point right.
- From 4.12–4.6 s the first answer line rises 4 px and fades in.
- From 5.6–6.1 s the card shrinks back to the title alone, then holds until 6.4 s.
When to use it: Use it in AI chat, coding assistants and search assistants that show reasoning in progress but hand the stage to the answer at the end.
