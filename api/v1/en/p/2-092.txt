Motion Prompt #2-092

What it is: Overlapping letters spread to normal spacing as they appear
What the sample shows: On a cream ground the blurred, overlapping 展开视野 spreads out from the centre and sharpens while the rule below stretches with it, then blurs away again.
Spec:
- Letter spacing goes from −0.55 em (overlapping) to 0.06 em.
- It opens over 0.3–1.4 s with a cubic ease-out, blur 6 px → 0, fading in over 0.3–1.0 s.
- A 3 px coral rule below stretches to the line's width.
- Over 3.8–4.4 s it blurs up to 4 px and fades out.
