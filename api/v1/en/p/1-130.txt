Motion Prompt #1-130

What it is: A donut chart whose arcs draw in one after another
What the sample shows: From the top, the first arc draws clockwise and each next arc continues from where the last ended until the ring closes; the total appears in the centre and the legend slides in on the right.
Spec:
- 4 segments of 42/28/18/12%; the ring has a 62 px radius and 20 px width, with a 2° gap between segments.
- Starting at 0.3 s from the top, segments draw clockwise one at a time, 0.45 s each (inOut3).
- The total in the centre fades in from 0.3–0.8 s.
- Legend rows slide in from 24 px to the right over 0.4 s (out3), starting at 0.5 s and 0.12 s apart; it all fades and resets from 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it on dashboards and report pages that show shares or breakdowns.
