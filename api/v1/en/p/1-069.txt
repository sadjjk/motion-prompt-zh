Motion Prompt #1-069

What it is: Cards that stick to the top one after another and stack as you scroll
What the sample shows: Gradient cards scroll up and pin near the top; each time the next card slides over, the ones beneath shrink and darken into steps.
Spec:
- 4 gradient cards, 150 px tall with 16 px gaps; each pins at 14 + index × 8 px from the top.
- When the next card overlaps, the one beneath shrinks (−6% per layer) and darkens (18% black per layer) in proportion to the overlap.
- Cards have a 22 px corner radius and end up as a stepped stack at the last card.
- Demo scrolls to the bottom over 0.4→4.6 s and back to the top over 5.4→6.3 s.
When to use it: Use it for 'How it works' or feature-highlight sections on product pages that explain steps one card at a time.
