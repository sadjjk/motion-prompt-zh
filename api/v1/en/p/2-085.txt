Please use "Fingerprint scan" (指紋スキャン), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #2-085

What it is: A fingerprint is read and access is granted
What the sample shows: In a rounded sensor box fingerprint arcs draw from the inside out, a green light bar sweeps down and back, the ridges dim, a check mark draws and the frame glows green.
Spec:
- 7 elliptical arcs (radii growing 6.2/6.6 px each) draw inside a 112 px rounded box, from 0.3 s, 0.08 s apart, 0.7 s each.
- A light bar sweeps down over 1.25–2.05 s and back up over 2.05–2.85 s (sine ease-in-out both ways).
- From 2.95 s the ridges dim to 0.3; over 3.0–3.3 s a check mark (7 px) draws with a cubic ease-out and the frame glows mint.
- The caption changes from “touch the sensor” to “scanning” to “verified”; over 4.2–4.6 s it returns to the start.
When to use it: Use it for login, payment, identity checks or showing security features.
