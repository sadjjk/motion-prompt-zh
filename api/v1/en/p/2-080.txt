Motion Prompt #2-080

What it is: A cursive signature writes itself on
What the sample shows: A one-stroke cursive signature is written left to right behind a coral pen-tip dot, then a gently curved underline sweeps in beneath it.
Spec:
- A single-stroke path: a large initial loop, 6 joined loops, and an upward exit stroke to the right.
- It writes on over 0.3–2.9 s with a sine ease-in-out; a pen-tip dot (4 px radius, 8 px halo) rides the tip.
- Line width varies 2–4.2 px with the stroke direction, like pen pressure.
- Over 2.95–3.4 s a gently curved underline (2.4 px) sweeps in (cubic ease-out); everything fades over 4.0–4.5 s.
When to use it: Use it for a portfolio intro, a brand signature, or signing off a letter or video.
