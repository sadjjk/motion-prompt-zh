Motion Prompt #3-062

What it is: A door, window or arch in the foreground frames the subject a second time
What the sample shows: A dark doorway fades in close to the camera and frames the distant person a second time; a light strip along its inner edge shows that it has thickness.
Spec:
- The person stands at the centre, x 270, 56 px tall; a tree on the left and a building on the right behind.
- Doorway: 168 px wide from y 30 to the bottom edge, centred; everything outside it is filled with dark #33405A.
- A 6 px light edge #CFCECB runs along the inside of the frame to show its thickness; a dashed line traces the opening and "框中框" is labelled at the top.
- Guides: amber #F5A524 dashed lines, 1.6 px wide, fading in with the effect amount.
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: To pull the eye to the subject, add layers, or suggest peeking or separation.
