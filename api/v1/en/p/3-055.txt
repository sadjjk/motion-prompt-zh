Motion Prompt #3-055

What it is: Only a detail such as the eyes, magnifying the smallest change
What the sample shows: The frame holds only a band across the eyes: the gaze sweeps left and right once, with one quick blink along the way.
Spec:
- Frame: 1.58 m to 1.72 m, a band across the eyes only 0.14 m tall; camera 2.0 m away.
- Gaze: sweeps left and right once per pass on a sine, 0.05 m each way, centred at the start and end.
- Blink: the eyes close from 2.4 s to 2.5 s and reopen from 2.6 s to 2.75 s.
- The background is heavily blurred; top right reads "大特写 ECU" and the scale shows only a thin band at the eyes.
- The camera never moves; one pass = 4.7 s and the first and last frames match.
When to use it: To build tension, stress a key detail or show a character's inner moment.
