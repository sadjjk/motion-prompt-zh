Motion Prompt #4-020

What it is: A guilloche pattern of fine banknote-style curves layered into a slowly turning rosette
What the sample shows: An emerald-and-gold rosette turns slowly with layers rotating in opposite directions, while wavy bands on both sides drift sideways.
Spec:
- 32 hypotrochoids in 4 groups form a rosette with a 96 px outer radius, in emerald #0F5A4A and gold #B88A2E.
- The 4 groups have 13 / 13 / 17 / 11 petals and each turns exactly 1 petal per 6.4 s, alternating direction.
- Side bands: 2 families of 10 sine lines each, green and gold, wavelength 48 px and amplitude 10 px, drifting opposite ways by 1 wavelength per cycle.
- 2 thin rings of radius 115 px and 118 px frame the rosette; a 7 px monospace serial number sits bottom left.
When to use it: Openers for finance, fintech or luxury brands, backgrounds for certificate or ticket designs, or a formal title slide.
