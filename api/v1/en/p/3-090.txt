Motion Prompt #3-090

What it is: Open on the result, the conflict or a close-up straight away, and tell how it got there afterwards
What the sample shows: Three panels light up in turn. Panel 1 is the result in close-up: a two-tier cake with strawberries, its candle flame flickering, background blurred, the camera creeping in. Panel 2 is a close shot of the face: the mouth opens, a "Wow!" bubble pops up and the eyes blink once. Panel 3 is the wide establishing shot: the person stands by the table and raises an arm. On the timeline below, the first 3 s are filled blue and labelled "first 3 s · hook", and panel 1 ends exactly as the playhead leaves that stretch.
Spec:
- 9.4 s cycle. The 3 panels start at 0.3, 3.3 and 5.8 s and end at 8.5 s; panel 1 lasts exactly 3 s.
- Result close-up: background blurred by 3 px, slow push-in; the flame flickers irregularly from 2 overlaid frequencies.
- Timeline: the 0–3 s stretch is blue, with ticks "0" and "3s"; the playhead moves in real seconds.
- From 8.8 to 9.4 s the picture fades back to the opening frame.
When to use it: For the opening of a short video that has to stop the scroll in the first seconds.
