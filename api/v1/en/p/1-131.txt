Please use "Holographic card" (ホログラムカード), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-131

What it is: A collectible card that tilts with the cursor while rainbow foil and glare shift across it
What the sample shows: As the cursor moves over a card on a dark background, the card tilts in 3D, rainbow foil streams across its face and a soft glare follows; it straightens when the cursor leaves.
Spec:
- Card 112 × 164 px, corner radius 10 px; tilts up to 13° by the cursor position on it (perspective 600 px).
- Rainbow diagonal stripes blend with color-dodge and follow the cursor; their opacity rises from 0.15 to 0.75 as the tilt grows.
- A white radial glare (radius 90 px, opacity 0.35) follows the cursor.
- In the demo hover starts from 0.7–0.95 s; from 4.6–4.9 s the cursor leaves, the card straightens and the foil dims.
When to use it: Use it for collectibles, membership cards and reward showcases that should feel tangible and premium.
