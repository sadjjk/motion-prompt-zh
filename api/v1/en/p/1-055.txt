Please use "Thinking orb: listening" (思考オーブ・聞き取り中), one of the names in "Glow & liquid".
Motion Prompt #1-055

What it is: Latitude rings ripple like a sound wave to show the mic is listening
What the sample shows: Rings of dots swell and shrink one after another from top to bottom, like a wave rolling over the orb.
Spec:
- Orb radius 58 px with 11 latitude rings (−75° to 75°, every 15°), 8–34 dots per ring depending on latitude.
- Each ring's radius swells by up to ±10% (about 6 px); 2 waves per cycle, offset 0.7 rad from ring to ring.
- Swollen rings are brighter. The orb spins once per cycle.
- Label "Listening…" at 15 px on the right.
When to use it: Use it while the mic is open for voice input, voice mode or dictation.
