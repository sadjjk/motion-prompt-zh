Motion Prompt #4-097

What it is: Early Cubism in the manner of Picasso and Braque: objects broken into overlapping translucent facets of brown and grey, the same bottle or glass seen from two angles at once, with broken outlines and stencilled letters
What the sample shows: Brown and grey facets pile up one by one on buff paper, and a bottle and a glass seen from two viewpoints emerge in broken lines. Dot and hatch textures and the letters CAFÉ are added; then a few facets slide and the letters hop.
Spec:
- Buff paper with faint brown speckles; it stays when everything else fades. The palette is ochre, umber, dark brown, putty, blue-grey and pale grey.
- 0.3–3.3 s: 46 triangles and quadrilaterals fade in one after another, each in 0.2 s; each facet is a gradient from light to dark at 18–70% opacity, so the layers show through each other.
- 1.5–2.85 s: 22 broken outline strokes 2 px wide are drawn, 0.05 s apart, for a bottle seen from the front, the same bottle shifted and leaning, and a glass, plus 3 long lines that cross the picture. 2.0–2.6 s: a dot grid and a patch of hatching appear; 2.2–2.8 s: the glass's mouth is drawn twice, once as a flat ellipse and once as a circle seen from above.
- 2.8–3.3 s: the letters C, A, F and É appear one by one in a serif face at the upper right. From 3.4 s 5 of the facets slide back and forth by up to 6 px, and from 3.6 s the letters hop by up to 4 px in turn.
- At 5.4–6.0 s the picture fades back to blank paper; at 0 s and 6.4 s only blank paper is left, so the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it to suggest many viewpoints, analysis or reconstruction, for art and museums, or for an intellectual, classic-modern look.
