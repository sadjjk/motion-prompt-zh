Motion Prompt #1-092

What it is: An isometric 3D chart of blocks showing data by region and month
What the sample shows: Isometric blocks rise from the front with a bounce; hovering greys out all but the bar's row and column and shows its value, and switching metrics re-stacks heights in a wave.
Spec:
- Isometric blocks for 4 regions × 6 months; grid spacing 32 × 12 px, bars 0.72 of a cell wide.
- Rows rise from the front with a delay of (8 − row − column) × 70 ms, a 650 ms duration and a bouncy ease.
- Hovering keeps colour only on the bar's row and column and greys the rest in 0.2 s; the value tooltip springs in over 0.22 s. The demo hovers from 1.9–2.7 s and 4.0–4.7 s.
- In the demo the metric switches at 3.1 s and heights re-stack in a (row + column) × 45 ms wave; from 5.4 s blocks drop to the floor in 0.5 s, staggered by (row + column) × 30 ms.
When to use it: Use it in regional sales or traffic dashboards and report pages to compare two dimensions in 3D.
