Motion Prompt #2-075

What it is: Letters crumble and fall, then rebuild
What the sample shows: Four characters tilt slightly in turn, then fall spinning and accelerating while shedding debris, vanish, and come back down from above into place.
Spec:
- 4 characters (64 px) starting at 0.3 s, 0.16 s apart; one character's cycle is 3.6 s.
- 22–27%: tilts to a random angle within ±12°, held until 30%.
- 30–62%: falls 109 px with a cubic ease-in, rotating to within ±90° while fading; 5 debris bits fall with it.
- 80–92%: comes down from 32 px above with ease-out while fading in, back in place.
When to use it: Use it for captions about endings, collapse or failure, or an outro.
