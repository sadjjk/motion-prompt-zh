Motion Prompt #1-084

What it is: A grid whose overlay slides in from the side the cursor entered
What the sample shows: The pointer crosses a 3 × 3 thumbnail grid from many directions; a dark caption panel slides in from the entry edge and out the exit edge.
Spec:
- A 3 × 3 work grid with 7 px gaps and 10 px corner radius.
- On entering a tile, a dark rgba(20,18,30,.82) panel slides in from the edge the pointer crossed over 0.32 s (fast start, soft stop), and slides out toward the exit edge.
- Direction is decided by normalising the pointer's offset from the tile centre by width and height, then comparing the horizontal and vertical offsets.
- In the demo the pointer passes through 8 tiles from the left, bottom, top and right between 0.3–4.9 s.
When to use it: Use it on portfolio, gallery or product grids to give hover states a sense of direction.
