Motion Prompt #1-201

What it is: A sticker you pinch by the corner and pull: it folds back along a crease to show its back, and sticks down again when released
What the sample shows: The cursor pinches the bottom-right corner of an orange sticker on a dotted backing and pulls it up-left; the crease advances as the back folds over, and on release it springs back flat with a wobble.
Spec:
- A die-cut sticker 160×140 px with corner radius 22 and an 8 px white border sits on a dotted backing.
- The cursor moves to the bottom-right corner at 0.3–1.0 s, presses, then drags it to (222, 72) at 1.2–3.2 s.
- The crease is the perpendicular bisector between the corner and the drag point; the lifted part is mirrored across it and drawn as a pale back with a gradient and a 14 px blurred shadow.
- At 3.6 s it lets go and the lift falls back as e^(−4.5t)·cos(8t), clamped at 0, so it snaps flat with a small wobble.
- The cursor leaves at 3.9–4.8 s; the sticker is flat from then until the cycle restarts.
When to use it: Use it on logos, badges or reward cards that should feel tactile and fun to fiddle with.
