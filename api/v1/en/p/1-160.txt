Please use "Expanding panels" (エキスパンディングパネル), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects".
Motion Prompt #1-160

What it is: The hovered panel in a row of narrow images widens and shows its title
What the sample shows: Among 5 tall image panels, the 2nd and then the 4th widen as the cursor rests on them while the others narrow, and a title appears at the lower left of the wide panel.
Spec:
- 5 equal vertical panels (471 wide in total, 136 tall, 6px gaps), each flex 1, showing geometric pictures cropped around their centres.
- The hovered panel widens to flex 4 over 0.6 s (easeOut power 3) while the others narrow.
- Its title fades in at the lower left once the expansion passes 0.6.
- Timeline: panel 2 from 0.35 to 1.75 s, panel 4 takes over from 1.75 to 3.6 s, then all panels return to equal width.
When to use it: Use it for portfolio categories, travel destinations or gallery entrances chosen by picture.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/en/e/1-160.txt
