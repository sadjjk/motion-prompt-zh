Motion Prompt #4-063

What it is: An ink-wash look on xuan paper: distant hills bleeding in shades of ink alone, a bold stroke, a vertical inscription and a vermilion seal
What the sample shows: Xuan paper with visible fibres. The far hills bleed in pale, and nearer hills overlap a shade darker. Faint horizontal strokes mark the water, and a rock at the lower left is laid down in a single bold stroke. A small boat with a figure holding a pole drifts slowly from left to right. At the top right the vertical inscription 孤舟一叶 远山青 appears one character at a time, and a vermilion seal is pressed last. On reset the ink spreads and fades, and the paper turns clean again.
Spec:
- Paper #F4EDDC → #E2D6BC, ink in one colour only, seal #B23A2E.
- 0.2–1.6 s far hills, 0.8–2.0 s middle hills bleed in from 12 stacked layers.
- 2.0–2.6 s the bold rock; 1.2–5.4 s the boat drifts from x 140 to 300.
- From 3.0 s the 7 inscription characters appear 0.25 s apart; 4.4 s the seal.
- 5.4–6.3 s the ink spreads and fades.
