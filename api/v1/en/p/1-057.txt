Motion Prompt #1-057

What it is: Bands of dots flow and undulate around the orb to show the AI is writing
What the sample shows: Three bands of dots wrap the orb at different tilts, rippling as they flow, with light running along them.
Spec:
- Orb radius 58 px (240 faint dots) with 3 bands tilted 0.5, −0.45 and 1.15 rad, 120° apart in phase.
- Each band is 72 points around × 3 rows, waving ±0.32 rad in latitude with 2 crests around.
- The wave moves once per cycle; the bright part runs round the band 2 times per cycle.
- The orb spins once per cycle. Label "Writing…" at 15 px on the right.
When to use it: Use it while the AI is writing, drafting or summarising text.
