Motion Prompt #1-162

What it is: A clock whose second hand ticks with a small overshoot
What the sample shows: On a white face with 60 ticks, the hour and minute hands stay at 10:10 while the orange second hand jumps from major tick to major tick, overshooting slightly each time.
Spec:
- A 140px clock face with 60 ticks (every 5th thicker and longer); the hour and minute hands rest at a sample time of 10:10 instead of reading the real time.
- The accent second hand jumps to the next major tick (30°) every 0.4 s.
- Each jump takes 0.15 s with back-out (overshoot 1.7), passing slightly beyond the tick and settling back.
- 12 jumps per cycle make exactly one turn, so the loop is seamless.
When to use it: Use it as an accent on screens about time, such as opening hours, time zones or countdowns.
