Motion Prompt #1-183

What it is: A slider made of thin bars that rise into a trailing wave as you drag
What the sample shows: Thin bars line a white plastic-style base, blue to the left of the value and grey to the right. The cursor presses and drags slowly, raising a small bump; then it flicks back and forth and a tall wave trails behind it. When the cursor stops the wave settles, and after release the value eases back.
Spec:
- 542×191 card, 4.5 s cycle. No round thumb: a row of bars 4 px wide at a 7.5 px pitch, 11 px tall at rest, blue left of the value and grey to the right.
- The cursor presses at value 34 at 0.78 s, drags slowly 34 → 52 over 0.90–1.85 s, then flicks 52 → 90 → 24 → 68 by 2.82 s.
- Bars under the cursor lift up to 12 px. Behind it a wave built from the last 1.4 s of motion grows with speed, decays with a 0.36 s time constant and is capped at 48 px.
- The cursor holds still until 3.50 s while the wave settles, then lets go.
- From 3.70–4.15 s the value eases back to 34 and the cursor returns to rest.
When to use it: Use it for brightness, volume or zoom sliders in settings panels where values are adjusted by dragging.
