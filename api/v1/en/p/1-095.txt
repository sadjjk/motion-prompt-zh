Motion Prompt #1-095

What it is: A horizontal bar chart race where rankings change year by year
What the sample shows: Drink bars grow year by year, overtaking bars slide up to swap ranks and a large year ticks on; dragging the slider back plays the ranking in reverse.
Spec:
- 8 drinks race on sales from 2019–2026, showing only the top 6, with 22 px rows.
- Rank positions approach their targets at 10/s (16/s while dragging); the axis maximum is 1.08 × the leader and follows at 4/s.
- In the demo it autoplays from 0.3–4.5 s at 0.6 s per year; a 58 px year in the lower right and the bar-end values update in sync.
- In the demo the year slider is grabbed at 5.0 s and dragged back to 2019 from 5.05–5.8 s, replaying ranks in reverse; the play button is clicked at 6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it in data stories, annual reports and marketing pages that show how market share or sales rankings changed over time.
