Please use "Laser cut" (レーザーカット), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #2-086

What it is: A laser line slices the text into two offset halves
What the sample shows: A sparking laser runs left to right along the middle of the word; once it passes, the top half slides up-left and the bottom half down-right with a slight tilt, then they rejoin.
Spec:
- Over 0.3–1.2 s a laser (2.5 px white core with 16/8 px coral glow) runs along the middle from left to right, with 8 sparks at its tip.
- Over 1.2–1.55 s the top half moves to (−16, −12 px) at −3° and the bottom half to (16, 12 px) at 2°, with a cubic ease-out.
- A red hot edge shows along the cut and fades by 2.6 s.
- Over 3.7–4.3 s the halves return with a cubic ease-in-out.
When to use it: Use it for sci-fi or action titles, or as a cut between scenes.
