Motion Prompt #1-065

What it is: Hand-drawn red-pen marks that circle, box or underline key words
What the sample shows: Each time a style is picked, the old marks rewind away and new red strokes are drawn word by word, each followed by a lighter second pass.
Spec:
- 3 words are marked in red #D93A55: a 2.4 px main stroke plus a 1.6 px second pass at 70% opacity.
- 4 styles: underline, circle, box and strike-through; switching first rewinds the old marks (.26 s).
- New marks are drawn word by word, .24 s apart, the second pass .13 s later; each stroke lasts .25 s + path length × 0.9 ms.
- Easing cubic-bezier(.55,.1,.35,1); in the demo the style switches at 0.2 / 1.75 / 3.3 / 4.9 s.
When to use it: Use it in marketing taglines, pricing comparisons or an article's key sentence to make words stand out with a handwritten feel.
