Motion Prompt #1-119

What it is: A book page turns over the spine to the other side
What the sample shows: The cursor clicks the right page of an open book twice; each time a leaf narrows over the spine, flips and lands on the left; a final click on the left page turns both leaves back.
Spec:
- An open book: 2 pages of 150×148 meeting at a spine at x 271.
- The cursor clicks the right page at 0.9 s and 2.1 s; each time one leaf turns over the spine in 0.7 s, its scaleX following cos(π·easeInOut) from 1 to −1.
- Past 0 the back of the leaf shows, and near 0 a shade of up to 0.25 suggests depth. The spreads go 1-2 → 3-4 → 5-6.
- At 3.4 s the left page is clicked: the upper leaf from 3.5 s and the lower leaf from 3.6 s each turn back in 0.55 s, returning to 1-2.
When to use it: Use it for catalogues, picture books, portfolios or annual reports that should feel like turning paper.
