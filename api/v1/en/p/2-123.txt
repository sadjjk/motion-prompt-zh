Motion Prompt #2-123

What it is: A type animation where 2 rows of big words keep flowing sideways, speed up with the scroll speed, lean like italics as they go, and run backwards when scrolling up
What the sample shows: On a dark background, 2 rows of big words flow in opposite directions; a fast scroll down makes the words rush and lean like italics, and scrolling up reverses them. When scrolling stops, the letterforms straighten up again.
Spec:
- The 2 rows of words flow in opposite directions and normally travel 570 px, 1 set of words, per cycle. Words are laid out by their measured widths, with equal gaps and a dot centred in each gap.
- Every 1 px/s of scroll speed adds 0.3 px/s to the flow of the words, and the letterforms shear sideways like italics with the speed, up to 0.3.
- The page scrolls down at 1.0–1.8 s and up at 3.0–3.8 s with a sin² speed curve peaking at 1500 px/s; both distances are equal, so the scrollbar returns to where it started.
- The distance travelled is integrated at load in 1/120 s steps and comes to exactly 570 px at the end of the cycle, so it joins up with 0 s.
- At the lower right, an arrow shows the scroll direction with the multiplier against the normal speed; scrolling up makes it negative.
When to use it: Use it for brand names, slogans, portfolio headline words or event keywords where big type should come alive with the scroll.
