Motion Prompt #1-152

What it is: Lifts the hovered card while the other cards fade and shrink
What the sample shows: As the cursor moves over 4 cards, only the pointed card lifts and grows a little while the others turn faint, greyish and smaller.
Spec:
- A row of 4 cards (100×120, 16 apart). While the cursor is over the row, the pointed card rises 8px and scales to 1.04 over 0.45 s (easeOut power 3).
- The other cards fade to opacity 0.45, shrink to 0.95 and get a 35% grey wash that mutes their colour.
- The cursor rests on cards 1, 2 and 4 in turn; after it leaves the row at about 4.1 s, everything returns to normal.
When to use it: Use it for pricing plans, product lists or portfolio cards where attention should go to one item in a row.
