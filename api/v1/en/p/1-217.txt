Motion Prompt #1-217

What it is: An aiming cursor whose four corner brackets snap onto the outline of the element under it
What the sample shows: The cursor moves to a nav pill, a button and a card in turn; each time the four brackets bounce onto its outline, and it clicks the button at 2.9 s.
Spec:
- At rest, 4 L-shaped brackets sit 14 px around a dot and turn slowly.
- Over an element the brackets move to its four corners 6 px outside the outline and straighten up; the snap follows a spring curve that overshoots once.
- Targets are a nav pill (1.0–1.8 s), a button (2.3–3.4 s) and a card (3.9–4.7 s); on leaving, the brackets return around the dot within 0.3 s.
- The click at 2.9 s pulls the brackets 4 px inward and presses the button down a little.
When to use it: Use it on gaming or tech landing pages and portfolios to make clickable targets obvious.
