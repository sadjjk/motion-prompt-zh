Motion Prompt #4-050

What it is: A magazine feature-page look on warm white paper: hairline masthead rule, big serif headline, two-column body text and a large pull quote
What the sample shows: On warm white paper the masthead rule draws out from the centre and the serif headline 慢生活 drops in; two columns of grey text bars set line by line, a right-hand paragraph turns into a large italic pull quote with a red rule on its left, and a page number appears bottom right. Then the whole page turns away to the left, revealing a blank next page.
Spec:
- Paper #F6F1E7, text #1E1B17, body grey #8A8174, accent red #B3261E; 30 px side margins.
- 0.2–0.7 s the masthead rule draws out from the centre; 0.7–1.2 s the 50 px serif headline drops from 18 px above.
- From 1.3 s grey body bars draw in one line every 0.09 s (2 columns × 7 lines, 10 px leading, 140 px columns), then 5 lines in the right column.
- 3.2–3.8 s the right column turns into a two-line 20 px italic pull quote with a red rule; 4.0 s the page number bottom right.
- 5.4–6.2 s the whole page turns off to the left; the blank page underneath matches the 0 s frame.
When to use it: Use it for essays, interviews, book notes and brand stories that should read calm and considered.
