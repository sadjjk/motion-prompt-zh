Motion Prompt #1-263

What it is: A background with a glowing ring of smoke in the middle whose edges fray into wisps that keep drifting outward while the ring slowly breathes
What the sample shows: A glowing ring sits in the middle of a dark screen, with thin wisps of smoke flowing out from its edge. The ring swells and shrinks slowly, glowing warmer toward the inside.
Spec:
- No simulation: brightness = e^(−((d − R) / 0.15)²) + 0.2 · e^(−2 (d / R)²), where d is the radius scaled by 0.7–1.5 × noise and R = 0.6 of the half height × (1 ± 4%) over the cycle.
- Noise is a 3-layer value-noise fbm sampled in polar coordinates, 7 noise cells around the circle and wrapping seamlessly.
- Drift: two noise layers offset by half a cycle each move 1.4 cells outward per cycle and jump back when their weight is 0, crossfading as 1 − |2f − 1|.
- Brightness is computed on a 135×52 grid, scaled up and multiplied per pixel by 1 ± 8% grain.
- Colour looks up brightness^1.6 in #1A0F3D → #5B21B6 → #C026D3 → #F472B6 → #FDE68A (OKLab) over a #07060C ground.
When to use it: Hero sections for AI products or voice assistants, and backgrounds for loading or idle screens.
