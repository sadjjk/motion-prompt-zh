Motion Prompt #2-135

What it is: A faceless line-art boy in one fine ink line on warm paper pedals a bicycle, knees and ankles following the cranks, and rings the bell on the way
What the sample shows: A boy in a black school uniform and cap rides a step-through bike with a brown bag in the front basket. The view follows him, so the paving joints and street lamps slide past. Halfway his thumb flicks the bell, the bell cap shakes, and 2 curved sound lines rise and fade.
Spec:
- Warm paper #fdfcf8 and one ink colour #1c1c1c, with a brown bag #4a3a2c and a pale bell #d8d2c0; the bike's tyres sit on the kerb line 172 px down and the rider keeps the same place on screen while the street moves.
- Bike geometry as in the original: wheel radius 13.5, cranks 6.2, thigh and shin 21 each; legs and sleeves are two pen lines filled with ink, the head is left blank with a black cap.
- In each 6.4 s loop the wheels turn exactly 10 times and the cranks 4 times; the wheels roll without slipping, so the ground moves at the rim speed, and 8 spokes per wheel turn with it.
- The two pedals are half a turn apart; each ankle rides just above its pedal and the knee is solved from hip and ankle, and the body rises and falls by 0.35 units with each pedal stroke.
- At 3 s the thumb flicks toward the bell for 0.2 s; the bell cap shakes up to 14° and settles, and 2 sound arcs of radius 5 and 9 fade out over 1 s.
When to use it: Use it for commuting and school-run stories, delivery or "heads-up" notices, everyday town life, or notifications and loading screens that want a hand-drawn warmth.
