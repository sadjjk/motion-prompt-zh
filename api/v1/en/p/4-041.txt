Motion Prompt #4-041

What it is: Moving forward through a corridor lined with text
What the sample shows: In a one-point corridor the floor, ceiling and both walls are filled with 文字走廊 in different colours that keep flowing toward the viewer, with the vanishing point sunk in shadow.
Spec:
- One-point perspective with a 300 px focal length; the corridor is 1 half-wide and 0.5 half-high.
- Depth z = 0.5–8 is cut into 15 sections (0.5 deep each), each surface carrying a text strip.
- It moves forward 4 sections per 6.4 s at a constant rate (whole sections, so the loop is seamless).
- Sections fade in over the far 3 units; a dark glow of radius 90 px sits at the vanishing point; floor, ceiling and walls each have their own colour.
When to use it: Use it for immersive openings, music or exhibition visuals, or behind a title.
