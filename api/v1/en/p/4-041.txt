Motion Prompt #4-041

What it is: An explainer drawn like a patent blueprint: construction lines, then object lines, then dimensions on Prussian-blue grid paper
What the sample shows: Dashed construction lines appear on grid paper, the flange's front and side views draw in order, dimensions and a FIG. 1 title block follow, and a red pencil rings the bore and notes a tolerance; the sheet then rolls away upward.
Spec:
- Prussian blue #123F74 with a 10 px fine grid and a 50 px coarse grid; line colour #EAF2FF.
- 0.2–0.8 s construction lines fade in as 50% dashes; from 0.8 s the object lines (front view, side view with 4 bolt holes) draw one after another, staggered 0.16 s, 0.45 s each.
- 2.5–3.3 s dimension lines, arrows and Ø140 / 40 / 4×Ø12; 3.4–4.7 s the title block box, then FIG. 1, FLANGE · 法兰 and SCALE 1:2 NO. 07 typed letter by letter.
- 4.6–5.0 s a red pencil #FF6B5B rings the bore and writes 公差 ±0.02 by 5.3 s; 5.4–6.1 s the sheet rolls up out of frame while a blank sheet slides in from below.
When to use it: Use it for inventions, how a machine works, product structure and history-of-technology explainers.
