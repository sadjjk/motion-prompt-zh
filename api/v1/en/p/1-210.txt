Motion Prompt #1-210

What it is: A retro background of flowing waves rendered through a 4×4 Bayer dither into four colours of chunky pixels
What the sample shows: Waves roll in four colours of pixels, indigo, violet, coral and cream, with dithered dot patterns at the edges, and a bright tail stretches behind the cursor and fades.
Spec:
- A flowing value v from 3 summed sine waves is computed on a 135×52 low-res buffer; phases turn a whole number of times per cycle.
- A 4×4 Bayer matrix applies ordered dithering and quantises to 4 colours: deep indigo, violet, coral and cream.
- Each pixel is scaled up 4 times with smoothing off for chunky dots.
- The cursor enters at 0.5–1.1 s and leaves at 4.6–5.3 s, adding a bright trail of radius 18 px that decays over 0.35 s.
When to use it: Use it for hero or section backgrounds on pixel-art, gaming or retro-styled products.
