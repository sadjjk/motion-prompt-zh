Motion Prompt #4-001

What it is: A calligraphy scene where a brush writes the character 永 on rice paper, with dry-brush streaks and a red seal
What the sample shows: A brush writes 永 stroke by stroke on rice paper with streaky dry edges; a red seal then shrinks into place, holds, and fades away.
Spec:
- Canvas 539 × 208 px on cream paper #EDE6D6. A 196 px character box; the 8 strokes of 永 are written from 0.2 to 4.2 s, 0.5 s per stroke.
- Each stroke is built from 60 fine hairs; past 45% of the stroke, edge hairs break up by noise to form dry-brush gaps.
- The seal is 44 × 44 px in #B5281D; from 4.4 s it shrinks from 1.6× to full size over 0.3 s.
- Cycle 6.4 s: ink and seal fade out from 5.4 to 6.0 s, returning to blank paper by 6.4 s.
When to use it: Use it as a video opener for East Asian or heritage brands, or as the hero of a calligraphy, tea or cultural event page.
