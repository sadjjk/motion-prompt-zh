Motion Prompt #4-061

What it is: A bullet-journal look: dot-grid paper with washi tape, marker handwriting, highlighter, sticky notes and ballpoint ticks
What the sample shows: On dot-grid paper three strips of washi tape are pulled out and stuck down. A marker writes 本周计划 and a highlighter swipes under it. Three to-do lines are written one by one, and a blue ballpoint ticks them off in turn. A yellow sticky note reading 别忘了 喝水！ bounces on, and a doodled star is added last. On reset the note peels away and the writing and tape fade.
Spec:
- Paper #FBF8F1, 12 px dot grid, ballpoint #2D5BD8.
- 0.2 / 0.35 / 0.5 s three tape strips, each pulled out over 0.3 s.
- 0.6–1.3 s the title; 0.9–1.4 s the highlighter.
- 1.5 / 1.8 / 2.1 s three to-dos written in 0.3 s each; ticks at 2.6 / 3.0 / 3.4 s.
- 3.9 s the sticky note; 4.4–4.9 s the star.
- 5.4–5.8 s the note peels off; 5.6–6.4 s everything fades.
When to use it: Use it for study methods, life hacks, habit-building and planning tips that want a friendly, personal feel.
