Motion Prompt #1-072

What it is: A list that leans in the scroll direction the faster you scroll
What the sample shows: A fast flick skews and slightly stretches the column of cards, which springs straight again on stopping; slow scrolling barely tilts it.
Spec:
- The list leans with speed: skewY = −speed × 3.2°, clamped to ±10°; it stretches 0.6% per 1° of lean, up to 6%.
- Speed is smoothed (.4 new, .6 old); the lean is driven by a spring closing 0.2 per frame with 0.72 damping.
- The red needle of a semicircle gauge at the top swings from −88° to +80° with speed.
- Demo: fast scroll at 0.4–0.9 s, slow scroll at 1.9–3.5 s, quick return to top at 4.2–4.6 s.
When to use it: Use it on content feeds, product lists and long portfolio lists to give fast scrolling a sense of inertia.
