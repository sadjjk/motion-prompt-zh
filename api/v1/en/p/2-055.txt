Motion Prompt #2-055

What it is: 3D render look: soft rubbery and glossy objects that bounce with real weight
What the sample shows: Soft 3D letters drop in turn, squash and spring back, a chrome ball lands as the full stop, and a row of glossy pills ripples with each landing.
Spec:
- Studio backdrop #efe2ea; 112 px letters s/o/f/t fall over 0.32 s to land at 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 s, stretching 12% vertically on the way down.
- Landings squash on a damped spring: 28% at first, decay factor 5.5, settling in about 0.6 s; the contact shadow scales with height.
- At 2.8 s a 14 px-radius chrome ball drops as the full stop. Materials: vertical gradient + white top-left highlight + dark bottom-right thickness + specular spot.
- Below, 2 rows × 21 pills wave 2.5 px 2 times per cycle, and each landing sends a 7 px ripple outward for 1.6 s; the tagline fades in at 3.2 s.
When to use it: Use it for cosmetics, lifestyle or app promos, and soft, playful brand logo reveals and social ads.
