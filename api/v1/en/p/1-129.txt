Motion Prompt #1-129

What it is: A line chart that draws itself left to right as its points pop in
What the sample shows: Over faint gridlines the line grows from left to right, points pop in turn, a gradient area fades in below and a growth label floats up at the end.
Spec:
- 10 data points; the 2.5 px line draws left to right at constant speed from 0.3–1.9 s.
- Each point (radius 4 px) pops from 0 with a 0.3 s backOut as the line tip passes it.
- Once the line reaches 60%, the gradient area under it fades in over 0.6 s.
- From 1.9–2.3 s a growth label rises 8 px into view above the last point; everything fades out and resets from 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it on dashboards and growth slides to make a trend readable at a glance.
