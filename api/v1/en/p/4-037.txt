Please use "Falling petals" (花びらの舞い落ち), one of the names in "Styled scenes".
Motion Prompt #4-037

What it is: Petals drift down and scatter at the cursor
What the sample shows: Pink petals sway, spin and flip as they fall over a dark plum background; a cursor sweeps once from left to right, pushing petals along its path aside before they settle back into their fall.
Spec:
- 40 elliptical petals with a radius of 4–8px; each falls through a 248px span 1 or 2 times per 6.4 s and sways 8–22px sideways 1–3 times.
- Each petal turns 1–2 times per 6.4 s and flips 2–4 times by driving its horizontal scale with cos.
- The cursor sweeps from x -16 to 555 over 0.8–4.0 s (cubic ease-in-out), with y going from 64 to 132 plus a 22px wave.
- Petals within 52px of the cursor are pushed up to 46px along its direction and away from it, peaking at 0.35 s and easing exponentially back to their paths.
- Any remaining push fades to 0 over 5.4–6.2 s; falling, swaying, turning and flipping are whole counts per 6.4 s, so the loop is seamless.
When to use it: Use it behind spring campaigns, Japanese-style titles or wedding invitations; swap the petals for snow or rain for other seasons.
