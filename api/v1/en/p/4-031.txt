Motion Prompt #4-031

What it is: A 3D look built from a few triangles, each painted a single flat shade
What the sample shows: Three mountains, the pines along the shore and a raft on the lake are all built from a handful of triangles, each a single shade. The light swings around horizontally along with a hexagonal sun at the top right; each face's brightness comes from its facing against the light and is quantised into a few steps, so as the light turns, the facets flip lighter and darker one by one. The lake mirrors the mountains, darkened and broken by horizontal ripples.
Spec:
- 6.4 s cycle: the light makes 1 full turn around the vertical axis; continuous loop with no reset segment.
- Each mountain's triangle (peak and base) is subdivided into 5 rows, with interior points jittered by a hash.
- Face brightness = face normal · light direction, quantised into 6 steps.
- The shoreline is at y = 128; the lake is the mountains mirrored, darkened and shifted by horizontal ripples.
When to use it: Use it for games, tech and outdoor visuals, or as a light 3D backdrop behind a title.
