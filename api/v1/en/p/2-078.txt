Motion Prompt #2-078

What it is: Reels spin and stop left to right on the result
What the sample shows: Random characters stream vertically in four windows, slowing left to right to land on the result; when all stop the frame flashes twice.
Spec:
- 4 windows (76×92 px, 14 px apart); reel i is a strip of 18 + 6i characters whose first and last are the target.
- From 0.3 s each strip scrolls up over 1.4 + 0.5i s with cubic-bezier(.2,.75,.25,1.04), overshooting slightly and settling.
- At speed, ghost copies sit 14 px above and below; the top and bottom 22% of each window get a dark inner shadow.
- Over 3.2–4.2 s the frame flashes yellow twice on a 0.5 s period; the strip starts and ends on the same character, so the loop is seamless.
When to use it: Use it to build suspense before a result: draws, fortunes, rankings or prizes.
