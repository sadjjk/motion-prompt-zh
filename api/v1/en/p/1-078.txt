Motion Prompt #1-078

What it is: A constellation-like mesh of drifting dots that link up when close
What the sample shows: Dots drift on a dark field joined by faint lines; the pointer pulls nearby dots in with yellow links, and a click sprays out new dots.
Spec:
- 72 dots drift on small closed paths and link within 72 px: hue 210 + x/width × 90, opacity (1 − d/72) × .45.
- The pointer becomes a node, drawing bright yellow links (255,224,130, (1 − d/120) × .8) to dots within 120 px and pulling them in by up to 16 px.
- A click bursts 9 new dots at 60–130 px/s, with speed above 30 px/s decaying at 1.5 per second; their radius shrinks from 4 to 1.6 over .6 s.
- The top-left badge shows the live link count; demo pointer enters at 0.6 s and clicks at 2.8 s.
When to use it: Use it as the hero background for tech, data and social-network products.
