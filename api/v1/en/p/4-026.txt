Motion Prompt #4-026

What it is: An isometric array of cubes that flip in a diagonal wave and change colour
What the sample shows: On a dark ground, a slab of cubes rolls over in a diagonal wave, their tops cycling through 4 colours like a flipping display.
Spec:
- A 22 × 6 cube array in orthographic isometric view (yaw 0.42, pitch 0.62 rad) on dark #121214.
- A diagonal wave flips them in turn; each cube rolls 90° 8 times per 6.4 s, each roll taking about 0.4 s.
- Top faces cycle through 4 colours #FF5C40, #F5F0E6, #3C5AE6 and #FFC83C, going round 2 times per cycle back to the start.
- Side faces blend between the current and next colour and darken according to their facing.
When to use it: Openers for tech brands or design studios, hero sections for product launches, or rhythmic section transitions in a presentation.
