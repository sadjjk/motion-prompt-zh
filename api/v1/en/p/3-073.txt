Motion Prompt #3-073

What it is: A hard light from above draws a sharp-edged circle of light on a dark stage
What the sample shows: The stage lights drop and a round pool of light follows the person walking left and right; outside it is almost black, a beam comes down from above, and the audience heads are silhouetted in front.
Spec:
- Stage: curtain #4A4C55 with 18 vertical folds, floor #5A5B62 from y 140; the person is 104 px tall and walks x = 270 + 120·sin, one round trip per pass.
- Pool: centre y 110, radius 62 px; outside is darkened with #07080B at 0.92 opacity, the circle and an ellipse on the floor are brightened by screening #FFE2A8.
- Beam: a trapezoid from above the frame down to the circle, #FFE8B4 added at 0.34 opacity at the top and 0.1 at the bottom.
- Audience: 13 head-and-shoulder silhouettes #0B0C10 in front, bobbing slowly by 1.2 px.
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For stage shows, concerts or a lead entrance when all eyes should be on one person.
