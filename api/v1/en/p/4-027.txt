Motion Prompt #4-027

What it is: Dominoes laid along an S-curve that topple in a chain reaction, then rewind upright
What the sample shows: Black and red dominoes along an S-curve topple in an accelerating chain, then stand back up in reverse as the clip rewinds.
Spec:
- 56 dominoes evenly spaced along an S-curve; every 8th is red, the rest black.
- Domino 1 falls at 0.3 s and the chain runs until 4.3 s, the gaps shrinking along a power-0.7 curve.
- Each falls in 0.28 s, tilting 1.22 rad to lean on the next; the final one tips to 1.53 rad.
- Over 5.4–6.2 s time plays backwards and they stand up in reverse order; each casts an angled ground shadow.
When to use it: Openers for videos about chain reactions or ripple effects, title slides for process explainers, or campaign teasers.
