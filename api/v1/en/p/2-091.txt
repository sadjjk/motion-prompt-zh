Motion Prompt #2-091

What it is: Neon tubes flicker before they light up
What the sample shows: On a dark brick wall the tubes of 营业中 flicker irregularly and light, washing the wall in coral; later only the last character drops out briefly, then the whole sign flickers once more.
Spec:
- The unlit tubes are 3 px outlines; over 0.45–0.92 s they flicker irregularly 3 times and light.
- The glow is 3 strokes of 24/14/7 px plus a 3.5 px bright core, with a 240 px radial light on the wall.
- From 1.6 s only the last character drops out 2 times; at 3.1 s the whole sign flickers once more.
- Over 4.25–4.55 s it switches off and resets.
When to use it: Use it for night-life, bar or game titles and sign-style shots.
