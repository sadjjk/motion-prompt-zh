Motion Prompt #3-107

What it is: Showing a surface so you can tell what material it is and how it would feel to the touch
What the sample shows: It starts from a plain picture of a ceramic cup on a table. First the camera moves in and the cup grows large. Then a low light from the left makes the left side bright and the right dark; the surface grains pop out, each lit on the upper left with a small shadow on the lower right, and a long shadow stretches across the table. Next a soft, blurred vertical highlight appears on the left. Finally 3 fine scratches show up as dark lines with light edges. A checklist on the right turns each numbered circle blue as its ingredient arrives, and the status reads plain version → adding → finished.
Spec:
- 5 s cycle. 0–0.6 s is the plain version; the 4 ingredients join from 0.6 s, the next every 0.7 s, each fading in over 0.5 s.
- Push in to a 2.3× close-up; low side light from the left: bright on the left and dark on the right, each of the 90 grains lit at the upper left with a shadow at the lower right, plus a long shadow on the table.
- Soft highlight: a tall ellipse on the left blurred by 2 px; the 3 scratches are each a dark line paired with a light edge.
- The finished look holds until 4.4 s and slides back to the plain version over 4.4–4.95 s, so 5 s and 0 s are the same frame.
When to use it: For product shots of ceramics, leather, wood or fabric that should convey how the material feels.
