Motion Prompt #1-222

What it is: Cards fanned out in a stack pop in with a bounce; the hovered one straightens and comes forward while its neighbours are pushed aside
What the sample shows: 5 cards bounce in as a fan; the cursor stops on each in turn from left to right, the hovered card straightens and comes forward while its neighbours slide out, and finally all shrink away.
Spec:
- 5 cards of 96×124 px sit 58 px apart, fanned from −14° to 14°.
- From 0.1 s, one card every 0.12 s pops in with a 0.5 s scale that overshoots and settles.
- The hovered card turns to 0°, scales to 1.08 and lifts 12 px, and is drawn on top.
- Its neighbours are pushed out 30 px and the next ones 15 px; how hovered each card is gets recomputed from the cursor's distance to its centre every frame.
- The cursor enters at 0.9 s, stops on the 5 cards in turn at 1.5–4.3 s and leaves at 5.0 s. At 5.4–6.0 s every card shrinks to 0, matching the not-yet-shown state at 0 s.
When to use it: Use it for portfolios, photo galleries or team sections where picking a card should feel playful.
