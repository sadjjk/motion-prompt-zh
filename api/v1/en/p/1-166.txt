Please use "Blend difference cursor" (反転ブレンドカーソル), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-166

What it is: A round cursor that grows over text and inverts colours where it overlaps
What the sample shows: The cursor becomes a white circle that inverts the text and background beneath it. Over the big headline the circle grows to cover more letters, and shrinks again when it leaves.
Spec:
- A white circle is drawn with the difference blend mode, inverting what lies underneath.
- The circle trails the pointer path by 0.08 s.
- Over the headline the radius goes 12 → 46 px (0.45 s inOut3) and returns when it leaves.
- The pointer enters at 0.3 s and leaves the frame from 4.1–4.8 s.
When to use it: Use it on portfolio and agency sites that want a playful custom cursor.
