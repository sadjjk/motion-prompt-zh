Motion Prompt #2-079

What it is: Outlined text fills with liquid from the bottom
What the sample shows: Mint-outlined hollow text fills from the bottom with a wavy surface and rising bubbles, holds full, then drains quickly; a percentage shows below.
Spec:
- 132 px text outlined in mint at 3 px; the liquid is clipped to the letter shapes.
- The level rises over 0.3–3.3 s (quadratic ease-in-out) and drops back over 4.2–4.6 s.
- The surface is two sine waves with height 6 px·sin(π·level) (flat when empty or full), flowing on a 1.57 s period.
- 10 bubbles rise through the liquid; a 0–100% readout sits below.
When to use it: Use it for progress, charging, hitting a goal, or a drinks brand headline.
