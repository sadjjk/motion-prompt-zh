Motion Prompt #4-049

What it is: A cute clay look on a pink cream background: little clay characters with faces squash and bounce as they pop in
What the sample shows: On a pink floor a milk-tea stand, a cup and three bean characters pop in one after another with a squash and bounce, and blink; the cup arcs over to the first bean, who smiles and hops twice. Then everything squashes flat into the floor.
Spec:
- Background #FFF1F5, floor #FFD6E3; the clay uses radial gradients with soft shadows and a top-left highlight.
- 0.2 s the stand (mint #9EE6D0, 110 px wide), 0.6 s the cup, and 1.0 / 1.3 / 1.6 s three beans (36×48 px) pop in with a backOut squash and stretch.
- Each bean blinks once at its own moment; 3.0–3.5 s the cup moves to the first bean along a 30 px arc and its face turns to a smile.
- 3.7–4.5 s the first bean hops twice, 26 px high; from 5.4 s everything squashes flat, the last to arrive first, 0.08 s apart.
When to use it: Use it for kids' explainers, light everyday stories and friendly brand or shop intros.
