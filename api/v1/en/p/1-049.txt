Motion Prompt #1-049

What it is: A coloured light travels around the border to show that work is in progress
What the sample shows: A rainbow beam, bright at the head and fading at the tail, keeps travelling clockwise around a dark input field.
Spec:
- Input 380 × 64 px, corner radius 16 px. The beam laps clockwise once every 4.8 s at a constant speed.
- Beam length is 22% of the perimeter, fading toward the tail (power 1.5), with a white dot at the head.
- Three layers: 9 px wide at 0.16 opacity with 16 px blur; 4 px at 0.45 with 8 px blur; a 1.6 px core at 1.0.
- Hue shifts 150° from tail to head, and the whole palette rotates 360° per cycle.
When to use it: Use it on the input or card that is doing the work while an AI reply or agent run takes more than about 3 s.
