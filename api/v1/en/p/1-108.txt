Motion Prompt #1-108

What it is: A one-time code fills in box by box and is confirmed once complete
What the sample shows: Digits drop into six boxes in turn, the active box glowing blue; when all are filled every box turns blue, the hint switches to "Verified" and the group lifts slightly.
Spec:
- 6 code boxes (66×77, gap 13.2, radius 11) with 35px digits. Empty boxes sit at opacity 0.55, filled ones at 1.
- The digits 8 4 2 9 1 6 pop into one box every 0.35 s (0.95–2.70 s). The box just filled gets a blue #60a5fa border with a 3.3px light blue halo, while the others return to the grey border #374151.
- At 3.2 s all 6 boxes light blue and the hint changes from "Code sent to ····4821" to "Verified · continuing"; over 3.4–3.95 s the group rises 4.4px with sine.inOut.
- Reset: over 3.95–4.35 s (easeInOutCubic) the digits fade and the blue borders and lift return; 4.35–4.5 s holds the empty starting frame.
- Versus Stepper: a fixed-length code is typed box by box and checked automatically when complete, rather than one value nudged with plus/minus buttons.
When to use it: Use it for SMS or email code entry at sign-in, sign-up or payment confirmation, and on two-factor authentication screens.
