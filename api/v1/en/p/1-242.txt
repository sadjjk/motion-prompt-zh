Motion Prompt #1-242

What it is: Press the arrow on the seam between two slabs and it springs half a turn; paying and receiving trade places and both amounts are worked out again
What the sample shows: On a peach-to-mint gradient two coloured slabs sit one over the other, Bitcoin orange on top and deep HYPE teal below, lettered in white: You pay 0.05 BTC on top and You receive 127.496 HYPE below, each with its dollar value and a round-logo coin pill in the bottom-right corner. A white round button with a down arrow sits on the seam. The pointer presses it; the button dips and springs back while the arrow swings half a turn, overshoots a little and points up. The slabs stay put, the labels trade places, and the top now receives 0.03898 BTC while the bottom pays 100 HYPE. The pointer brushes the HYPE pill, comes back and presses again, and the arrow carries on round the same way to point down.
Spec:
- Each slab is 300 × 116 px with a 20 px corner, an orange gradient for BTC on top and a teal gradient for HYPE below, stacked with a 3 px gap; a white 40 px arrow button sits on the middle of the seam.
- The payer holds a round lot of its coin (0.05 BTC / 100 HYPE); the receiver shows that value less 0.3%, divided by its own price (BTC 97700, HYPE 38.2 dollars).
- The arrow is pressed at 1.4 s and 3.6 s; it shrinks to 0.9 while held and springs back over 0.42 s with bounce 0.35.
- Each press turns the arrow another 180° the same way, on a 0.6 s spring with bounce 30 × 0.5% = 0.15.
- From 2.1–2.9 s the pointer rests on the lower HYPE pill and its translucent white fill rises from 20% to 34%.
- The pointer leaves by 4.8 s and the frame matches 0 s.
When to use it: Use it for swap screens in crypto exchanges and wallets, currency exchange and points-conversion forms.
