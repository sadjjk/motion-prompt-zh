Motion Prompt #4-040

What it is: Columns of glyphs pour down like rain
What the sample shows: On black, columns of katakana, digits and letters keep falling; the head of each column glows white and the trail darkens to green behind it, the glyphs changing as they go.
Spec:
- 39 columns at a 14 px glyph size; each column's start, speed (1–3 passes per 6.4 s) and trail length (10–18 glyphs) are fixed by index.
- The head glyph is white with a 14 px glow; the trail darkens to green further back (opacity falling from 0.9).
- The first 3 glyphs change every 0.1 s (64 times per 6.4 s); the rest every 0.4 s.
- A dark gradient covers the top and bottom 18%.
When to use it: Use it behind hacker, cybersecurity or data themes, or for a sci-fi opening.
