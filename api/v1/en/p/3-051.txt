Motion Prompt #3-051

What it is: Framed from the knees up, keeping both movement and expression
What the sample shows: The bottom edge falls at the knees; the person lifts a leg, steps out and draws back, so the arc of the thigh rising and falling is clear against a softly blurred background.
Spec:
- Frame: 0.42 m to 1.92 m (knees to the top of the head); camera 4.2 m away.
- Action: steps out 0.28 m from 0.4 s to 1.0 s with the leg lifting and landing inside the frame; draws back from 3.4 s to 4.0 s.
- The person stays sharp on the foreground layer; the background is softly blurred.
- Labels: top right reads "中全景 MLS"; the blue band on the scale figure runs from the knees to the head.
- The camera never moves; one pass = 4.7 s and the first and last frames match.
When to use it: When a character moves or gestures but you still want the face readable; common in scenes with two or three people.
