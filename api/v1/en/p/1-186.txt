Please use "Tear-off ticket stub" (チケットのもぎり), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects".
Motion Prompt #1-186

What it is: Drag the stub to tear it off along the perforation, and the ticket gets stamped
What the sample shows: The cursor grabs the stub at the right end of a papery concert ticket and pulls down. The perforation tears from top to bottom with a fibrous edge, the stub spins out of frame, the main ticket springs back and a red 'checked' stamp lands on it; once the stamp fades, the stub reappears in place.
Spec:
- The main ticket is 262 px wide and the stub 82 px, both 128 px tall, divided by a perforated line.
- The cursor moves to the stub over 0.1–0.6 s and presses at 0.66 s.
- From 0.78–1.98 s it drags down: the tear runs top to bottom along the perforation with a fibrous edge, and the stub swings open to 0.3 rad.
- Torn free at 1.98 s, the stub spins and falls in an arc, gone by 2.9 s, while the main ticket springs back.
- At 2.42 s a 'checked' stamp drops from 1.6× to 1× (a 0.12 s fall) and lands; it fades over 3.7–4.05 s.
- From 3.95–4.45 s the stub fades back in from 9 px above, then holds until the 4.8 s cycle ends.
When to use it: Use it for event checkout success, entry check-in or coupon redemption, where using or claiming something should feel tactile.
