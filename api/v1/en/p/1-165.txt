Motion Prompt #1-165

What it is: The cursor acts as a light; the text's long shadow points away from it
What the sample shows: On a dark background a small warm light circles a big headline. The long shadow behind the headline always points away from the light and swings round as it moves; when the light goes out the shadow folds back under the text.
Spec:
- The shadow has 18 layers; layer i is offset by direction × i × 3.2 px.
- Direction = (headline centre − light) divided by the canvas width and height.
- Layer opacity falls linearly from 0.6 at the front toward the far end.
- The light turns on from 0.6–1.0 s and goes out from 4.7–5.2 s, after which the shadow sits right under the text.
When to use it: Use it on hero headlines or portfolio landing pages that should react with depth when touched.
