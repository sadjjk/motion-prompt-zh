Motion Prompt #2-100

What it is: Cards line a horizontal 3D helix and stream along it endlessly as it turns
What the sample shows: Against a navy background, a row of small photo cards follows a horizontal helix, turning around its axis while moving right. Cards that swing to the front grow larger and brighter, with a shadow and a sweeping highlight; cards that swing to the back show a navy reverse and turn dim and hazy. The motion pushes and glides: it almost stops at the loop seam, speeds through the middle, and cards at both ends melt into the vignette.
Spec:
- 70 × 56 px cards sit on a horizontal helix with radius 55 px, 7 cards per turn and 36 px between cards along the axis.
- Perspective uses a 220 px focal length with the camera tilted down 0.24 rad, and cards are drawn far to near.
- Every 4.7 s the helix moves right by exactly one set of 6 different cards, so the loop has no seam.
- Speed follows 1 − 0.8·cos2πt: 20% of the average at the seam and 180% at the midpoint.
- Cards turned away show a navy back, and an edge-on card narrows to 0.22× its width.
- Far cards dim and blur, near cards get a shadow and a highlight, and both ends fade out over 110 px.
When to use it: Use it in portfolio, photo gallery and product showcase heroes to stream many cards past in 3D.
