Motion Prompt #2-125

What it is: The end ball strikes the row and only the ball at the far end swings out, back and forth, as a picture of a chain reaction
What the sample shows: 5 balls hang on strings from a bar. The gold ball on the left and the grey ball on the right swing out in turn, making 5 full swings in 5.4 s before coming to rest. The caption reads 「一个动作，连锁反应」.
Spec:
- 5 balls of radius 16 px hang 34 px apart on strings 120 px long from a bar near the top; the left ball is gold, the others grey, each with a small highlight.
- The swing follows a sine wave with 5 full periods in 5.4 s: in one half of each period the left ball swings out to the left, in the other half the right ball swings out to the right, both up to 0.6 rad; the middle 3 balls stay still.
- The caption 「一个动作，连锁反应」 sits under the balls in 16 px grey text.
- At 5.4 s the sine wave is back at zero and the balls hang still until 6.4 s, so the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it to explain a chain reaction or cause and effect, where one action passes its impact along.
