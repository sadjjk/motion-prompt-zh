Motion Prompt #1-059

What it is: A moving indicator stretches and trails a droplet tail behind it
What the sample shows: A white indicator moves between three options; the faster it goes, the more it stretches and the bigger the droplet clinging behind it.
Spec:
- Segmented control 288 × 44 px with three options 96 px apart; the indicator is 84 × 34 px.
- It moves every 1.2 s, starting at 0.1, 1.3, 2.5 and 3.7 s, 0.45 s per move (cubic ease-in-out): 1 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 1.
- With speed it widens by up to 25% and flattens by up to 12% (full at 320 px/s).
- The tail sits where the indicator was 0.09 s earlier; its blob grows from 6 to 16 px radius, joined by a neck. Labels invert over the indicator.
When to use it: Use it for the sliding indicator of a tab bar or segmented control, or for a slider thumb.
