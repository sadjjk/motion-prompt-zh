Motion Prompt #2-096

What it is: Countdown numbers slam down from huge
What the sample shows: 3, 2 and 1 slam down in turn from huge, each impact shaking the frame and sending out a ring; the final GO! is coral with burst lines.
Spec:
- From 0.3 s, 3 · 2 · 1 · GO! appear every 0.75 s.
- Each takes 0.7 s: from 3× it lands at 0.9× at 35%, reaches 1.05× at 55%, then settles at 1×.
- Each landing shakes the frame by 5 px and sends a ring from radius 60 px out to 200 px.
- GO! is coral with 12 burst lines and fades out over 3.9–4.4 s.
When to use it: Use it before a start, at the top of a live stream or at the start of a game round.
