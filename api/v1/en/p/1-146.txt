Motion Prompt #1-146

What it is: A field where typed words become tags on Enter
What the sample shows: Two words are typed one after another and each pops out as a pill tag on Enter; then the cursor clicks × twice and the tags shrink away as the rest closes up.
Spec:
- A 400×48 input that starts with one tag, "Design".
- From 0.6 s a word is typed at 0.25 s per character and Enter is pressed at 1.3 s: the text becomes a pill tag that pops from 0 in 0.35 s with back.out(1.4), and the Enter keycap at lower right sinks at the same time.
- From 1.8 s a second word is typed and pops out the same way on Enter at 2.5 s.
- The cursor clicks a tag's × at 3.3 s and 3.9 s; each tag shrinks away in 0.25 s (easeInOut power 3) and the rest closes up to the left, back to the first tag.
When to use it: Use it in forms that collect several short values such as interests, skills, keywords or recipients.
