Please use "Halftone reveal" (網点リビール), filed under "Pointer interactions / Cursor".
Motion Prompt #1-219

What it is: A dark field of halftone dots where tracing with the cursor swells the dots and brings up the picture beneath
What the sample shows: The cursor moves over dark halftone dots and a sunset mountain picture appears in dots along its path; a click at 4.0 s reveals the whole picture before it fades.
Spec:
- Halftone dots 8 px apart, normally dark grey and 1 px in radius.
- Dots near the cursor (Gaussian radius 46 px) swell up to 4.6 px by the source picture's brightness and take its colour.
- The trail shrinks back as e^(−Δt/0.9). The source picture is drawn in code: a sunset sky, a sun and three ridges of mountains.
- A click at 4.0 s spreads a reveal ring over the whole picture at 420 px/s; all dots shrink back at 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it on heroes that show a photo or artwork when visitors should explore the image with the cursor.
