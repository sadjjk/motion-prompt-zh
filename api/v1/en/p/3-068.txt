Please use "Bloom" (ブルーム), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #3-068

What it is: Light spills out of the bright areas while the dark areas keep their detail
What the sample shows: A car crosses a night street corner; glows spread only around the windows, street lamps and car lights, while the dark sky and buildings stay unchanged.
Spec:
- Scene: a street corner at night (sky #2C3B5E, windows and bulbs #FFC75A); a car crosses from x −60 to 600 once per pass.
- Only the bright parts go on a separate layer: windows, bulbs #FFE3A3, headlights #FFE9B8 and tail lights #FF5B4D.
- Glow: that layer is blurred by 3, 7 and 10 px and added on, plus a wide glow blurred by 5 px at half size (about 10 px at full size).
- The dark parts do not change under the additive glow; top right reads "BLOOM n%".
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For night scenes, neon and backlight when the light sources should glow softly and dreamily.
