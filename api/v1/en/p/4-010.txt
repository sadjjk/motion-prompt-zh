Motion Prompt #4-010

What it is: Flight arcs and moving lights criss-crossing an angled dot-matrix map
What the sample shows: On an angled dot map, orange arcs spring from hubs one after another, and lights travel along each finished arc to the far end.
Spec:
- An angled, top-down dot-matrix map with 8 hubs and 14 routes on #060912.
- Routes appear one by one between 0 and 2 s, each arc drawn in 1.2 s; arc height is 0.28× the route length.
- Once drawn, a light loops along each arc, covering 0.35 of the route per second.
- Routes are orange rgba(255, 170, 90), hubs glow blue; routes fade from 5.4 to 6.0 s while the map stays.
When to use it: Use it as a hero background for logistics, airline or global companies, an opener for talks on international reach, or a data-viz transition.
