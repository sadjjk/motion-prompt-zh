Motion Prompt #1-176

What it is: Floating balls that merge like liquid when they come close
What the sample shows: On a dark background, purple-to-blue balls drift slowly, grow a neck and join when they meet, then snap apart into separate balls again.
Spec:
- 5 balls with radii of 22–36 px follow Lissajous paths with horizontal amplitudes of 70–150 px and vertical ones of 30–45 px, 1 or 2 swings per cycle.
- The area where the field Σ r²/d² exceeds 1 is filled as liquid, so close balls grow a neck and distant ones thin out and split.
- The contour comes from marching squares on a 3 px grid, giving a smooth outline that stays sharp when scaled.
- The liquid uses a horizontal hue gradient from 265° to 190°; a 16% white core where the field exceeds 1.8 adds depth.
- Every path repeats a whole number of times per 4.8 s cycle, so the loop is seamless.
When to use it: Use it behind heroes, idle screens and brand visuals that need a soft, organic, lava-lamp feel.
