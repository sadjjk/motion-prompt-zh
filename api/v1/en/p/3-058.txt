Motion Prompt #3-058

What it is: Light focused by a rippling surface acting like a lens, casting a moving net of bright curved lines on underwater walls, the floor and skin
What the sample shows: The whole cycle is underwater in a pool. At the top a wavy band of surface is seen from below, and 5 slanted beams of light come in. The back wall is tiled, and the floor has perspective tiles and a lane line. A swimmer in a red suit, cap and goggles waves, and bubbles rise. A net of bright curved lines spreads over the wall, the floor and the skin, brightest where the lines meet; on the floor the net looks squashed. The net slowly changes shape and drifts, but the tile lines and the figure never distort. At the start and end the surface is calm and the net gentle; the waves pick up from 0.2 s and calm down again at the end.
Spec:
- 5 s cycle. The surface gets choppy over 0.2–1.0 s and calms over 4.4–4.95 s; the net is at 0.6× strength when calm.
- Net: 34 points each circle a little (1–2 loops per cycle), and brightness comes from the difference between the distances to the 2 nearest points, giving a connected net of curved lines that is brightest at the junctions.
- The net is screened at 0.55 over everything, as a copy stretched to 1.6× height and squashed onto the floor at 0.7, and on skin at 0.5; it is computed at half resolution.
- The wall tiles are a 26 px grid and the floor has perspective lines; the 5 beams start at x 60, 104 px apart, swaying 8 px; 5 bubbles rise.
- The swimmer is at (318, 96): skin #E8B48E, red suit #C8414B, cap #1F3A5F, goggles; the arm waves 1 time per cycle.
When to use it: For pools, the sea or aquariums when the picture should feel underwater.
