Motion Prompt #4-066

What it is: A caption-led social news look: one huge line at a time on a dark screen, keywords blocked in yellow and a number rolling up, readable with the sound off
What the sample shows: Night-street bokeh drifts sideways on a dark screen. A yellow rule grows down the left and 外卖平均 types in character by character. A huge number rolls from 0 to 28 and lands, while a yellow arc on the timer ring at right grows with it. A yellow block wipes across 28 分钟 from the left, flipping the text inside it to black. A follow-up line types in, and a small source credit slides up from below. On reset the whole caption moves up out of frame.
Spec:
- Number: rolls from 0 to 28 over 1.0–2.0 s.
- Yellow block: wipes over 28 分钟 at 2.05–2.4 s, text inside turns black.
- Timer ring: arc length = number / 60 minutes.
- Source credit: slides up from below at 3.6 s.
- Reset: 5.4–6.0 s the whole caption moves up 208 px.
When to use it: Use it for data news, stories with a twist and quick updates meant to be watched muted.
