Motion Prompt #2-113

What it is: Viewer comments stream across the video from right to left
What the sample shows: A person's silhouette stands in front of seaside footage while short comments in 5 rows above slide from right to left at steady speeds. Rows move at different speeds, so comments in the fast row overtake the slow ones. Most are white with a dark outline, a few are yellow, mint or coral, and my own comment has a white frame. Around the person's head and shoulders the comments are softly cut away, so the person always sits in front of them. A player bar with a 弹 button runs along the bottom.
Spec:
- 5 rows with baselines at y = 30, 54, 78, 102 and 126 px (24 px apart); text is 15 px at weight 600 with a 3 px dark outline.
- Each row covers a span of 700–840 px once per 4.7 s (one row twice), about 150–300 px/s, so fast rows overtake slow ones.
- Every row moves a whole number of spans, so the 4.7 s loop has no seam.
- 3 comments are yellow, mint and coral; my own comment has a 1.5 px white frame.
- Comments are cut out inside the person's outline (24 px head radius plus shoulders) grown by 8 px and blurred 3 px.
- A 22 px player bar at the bottom shows 42% progress and a 弹 button.
When to use it: Use it to fake audience reactions in an edit, to keep the live feel in stream clips, or to bring up viewer questions in a talking-head video.
