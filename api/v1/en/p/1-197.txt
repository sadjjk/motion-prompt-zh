What it is: A glass lens glides over the content, bending the background at its rim with colour fringes
What the sample shows: Over a dark grid with a large headline and 6 colour chips, a capsule-shaped glass lens glides right, pauses, then glides back. The middle of the lens is clear, while content near the rim is pulled inwards and bent, with red and blue fringes along the edge; an arched highlight sits on top and a soft shadow falls outside.
Spec:

- It slides right over 0.4–2.0 s and back over 2.8–4.4 s with a cubic ease-in-out.
- Refraction lives in a 26 px band at the rim: the outermost ring samples 11 px further in, falling off quadratically to 0 towards the inside.
- Dispersion: red, green and blue sample at distances in the ratio 1 : 1.18 : 1.36.
- An arched highlight of 22% white covers the top 42%, with a 24 px blur shadow offset 8 px down and a 1 px rim.
