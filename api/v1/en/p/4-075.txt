Please use "Charcoal and eraser" (木炭と練り消し), filed under "Craft & patterns / Drawing & painting media".
Motion Prompt #4-075

What it is: Charcoal darkens the whole sheet and is smudged, a kneaded eraser lifts out the light shapes, and the lines are redrawn over the erased traces
What the sample shows: Charcoal goes on in vertical strokes and is smudged, then a pear and the light of a window are erased out of it. The outlines and window frame are redrawn on top, with a faint offset ghost of the earlier line.
Spec:
- Pale grey paper. 0.3–1.4 s: 26 vertical charcoal strokes 22 px wide at 12% opacity are laid from left to right across x 120–420.
- 1.4–2.0 s: the charcoal is smudged even, a flat layer rising to 45% opacity over the same area.
- 2.0–3.4 s: a pear about 80 px wide and a 72 px square of window light are lifted out in paper colour, rising to 85% opacity, with an 8 px soft edge.
- 3.4–4.6 s: the pear, its stem and the window frame with its cross bars are redrawn in 2 px charcoal; a 1.6 px ghost of the pear outline sits 3 px right and 2 px down at 25% opacity.
- At 5.4–6.0 s the drawing fades back to blank paper; at 0 s and 6.4 s the card is blank paper, so the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it for quiet documentary moments, flashbacks, reflective scenes or art and education topics.
