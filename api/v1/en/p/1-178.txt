Motion Prompt #1-178

What it is: Blurred glows drift and blend, flowing like an aurora
What the sample shows: On a dark background, large green, blue, purple and pink glows drift on separate paths and overlap, with a greeting headline on top.
Spec:
- 4 glows with radii of 160–210 px and hues of 165°, 205°, 270° and 320° are stacked with additive blending (lighter).
- Each glow drifts on a closed loop spanning 70–120 px horizontally and 25–40 px vertically, with 1 or 2 swings per cycle on each axis.
- The radius breathes by ±12% once per cycle.
- Each radial gradient has 0.4 opacity at the centre, 0.15 at half the radius and 0 at the edge.
- A 24 px heading and a 14 px subline are drawn on top and stay clearly readable.
When to use it: Use it for welcome screens, AI idle states and hero backgrounds that should move quietly and continuously.
