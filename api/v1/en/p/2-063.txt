Motion Prompt #2-063

What it is: Variety-show styling: loud multi-outlined captions with comic burst bubbles and speed lines
What the sample shows: Over a ginger cat, captions spring in letter by letter and sway, question marks hop and a sweat drop slides, then the camera punches in, a burst bubble and speed lines pop and a red stamp lands.
Spec:
- Captions have 3 layers: a gradient fill, a thick white outline, and a coloured outer outline with a dark extruded shadow (title: #FFFBD6→#FFE23A→#FF9A1F, white, #FF3D7F, #A3124F).
- Entrances use a damped spring 0→1.15→1 (ζ=0.517, ω=22) with a drop and a spin-in, each character 2 frames after the last; held text sways slightly.
- The title enters at 0.3 s and a whispered aside at 1.2 s; both pop away at 2.3 s. At 2.4 s cyan question marks pop in one by one and hop, and the cat's eyes turn to dots.
- At 2.9 s the camera zoom jumps by 0.14 and shakes for 3 frames; white speed lines boil at 12 fps behind a 2-layer yellow #FFF15A / pink #FF3D7F burst bubble. A stamp slams down in 4 frames at 4.3 s, and from 5.4 s everything pops out in turn (0.17 s each).
When to use it: Variety-show packaging, pet and vlog shorts, and captions on livestream clips.
