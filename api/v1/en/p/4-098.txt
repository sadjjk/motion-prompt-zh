Please use "Klimt golden phase" (クリムトの黄金様式), filed under "Art movements / Modern & postmodern".
Motion Prompt #4-098

What it is: Klimt's golden phase: the figure's robe becomes a flat field of gold leaf packed with spirals, squares and eye-shaped motifs, while only the face and hands are painted naturally, set against a gold mosaic background above a flower meadow
What the sample shows: In front of a gold mosaic, small flowers open in a meadow at the bottom, a figure in a gold robe appears and spirals, squares and eye motifs fill the robe from top to bottom. A band of light crosses the picture and the mosaic twinkles, the eyes blink and gold dust drifts down.
Spec:
- The whole background is a gold-leaf mosaic of 3167 tiles 5 px square in staggered rows over a brown grout, in 6 gold tones plus a few dark and pale tiles; it stays when everything else fades.
- 0.3–1.0 s: a robe shaped like a bell fades in as a gold gradient, with dark hair, a pale face with closed eyes, a hand, and 3 small red, orange and blue flowers in the hair; the robe has a dark outline and a soft shadow. 0.3–0.8 s: a green meadow with 150 grass blades fades in along the bottom, and 64 small five-petalled flowers in 6 colours pop open from the sides towards the middle at 0.9–2.1 s.
- 1.0–3.4 s: 53 motifs grow in from top to bottom inside the robe: 17 black spirals, 23 black or cream squares with a gold centre and 13 cream eyes with a blue iris.
- From 3.4 s a pale band sweeps across the whole mosaic by 5.4 s and 362 tiles twinkle on their own; a soft light band crosses the robe by 5.2 s, each eye blinks once every 1.6 s at its own moment, 70 gold flecks drift slowly down and twinkle, and from 3.6 s the flowers sway.
- At 5.4–6.0 s the picture fades back to the dark gold wall; at 0 s and 6.4 s only the dark gold wall is left, so the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it for jewellery, luxury brands, anniversaries, themes of love and celebration, or any lavish, ornamental look.
