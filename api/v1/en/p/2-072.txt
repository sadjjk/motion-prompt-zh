Motion Prompt #2-072

What it is: Letters fall upward, flip over, then drop back
What the sample shows: Four characters accelerate up to the ceiling in turn, rotating 180° to hang upside down, bounce off it, then fall back to the floor completing 360° and bounce.
Spec:
- 4 characters (60 px) starting at 0.3 s, 0.12 s apart; one character's cycle is 3.6 s.
- 10–33%: rises 90 px with a cubic ease-in while rotating 180°.
- 33–46%: rebounds 19% of the way off the ceiling and back, then hangs upside down until 58%.
- 58–80%: falls with ease-in while turning to 360°; hops 16 px until 87% and rests at 93%.
When to use it: Use it for copy about reversal or upending norms, or a playful logo animation.
