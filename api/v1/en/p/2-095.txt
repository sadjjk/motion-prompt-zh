Motion Prompt #2-095

What it is: Greetings in different languages take turns
What the sample shows: Inside a pill-shaped frame 你好, Hello, こんにちは and Bonjour blur out upward and blur in from below, while the frame width and the dot after it glide to fit each word.
Spec:
- 4 words switch every 1.175 s (one round in 4.7 s).
- The old word moves up 18 px and blurs to 6 px as it fades over 0.3 s.
- The new word comes in from 18 px below over 0.15–0.5 s, blur 6 px → 0.
- Over 0–0.5 s the frame width eases (cubic in-out) to the new word plus 30 px each side, carrying the dot.
When to use it: Use it in heroes for global brands, travel or language services, or on welcome screens.
