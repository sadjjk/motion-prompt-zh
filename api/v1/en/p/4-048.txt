Motion Prompt #4-048

What it is: An explainer drawn in glowing amber lines on charcoal, building an everyday scene stroke by stroke like a luminous notebook
What the sample shows: With a fixed camera, glowing amber lines on charcoal draw the floor, desk, chair, a person reading, an open book and a lamp in turn; the lamp flickers twice and comes on, casting a warm pool on the desk, and a page turns. Then the lines retract in reverse order and the light goes out.
Spec:
- Charcoal #15130F, 1.8 px amber #F0B44C lines with an 8 px outer glow; the camera does not move.
- 0.3–3.5 s 15 strokes draw in the order floor → desk → chair → person → book → lamp.
- 3.6 s the lamp flickers for 0.3 s and comes on, adding a light cone and an elliptical pool (90 px radius) on the desk; 4.2–4.8 s a page turns over the spine.
- From 5.4 s the strokes retract from their ends, the last drawn first, 0.3 s each; the light goes out at 5.4–5.65 s.
When to use it: Use it for explainers about work, psychology and daily-life problems that open on a familiar scene.
