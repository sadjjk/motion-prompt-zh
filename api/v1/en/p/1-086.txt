Motion Prompt #1-086

What it is: A stacked card UI you swipe left or right to sort
What the sample shows: The pointer drags the top card right with a tilt, flings it away with a Save stamp, swipes the next one left to Skip, and finally both fly back.
Spec:
- 4 stacked cards, 190 × 150 px with 18 px corner radius; each lower card sits 9 px lower and 5% smaller.
- While dragging the card tilts by drag × 0.06°; the Save / Skip stamps fade in with drag distance, fully visible at 110 px, tilted −14° / 14°.
- On release the card flies a further 270 px off in 0.45 s and the next card moves up.
- In the demo the top card is dragged 150 px right from 0.5–1.6 s and card 2 left from 2.6–3.7 s; the 2 cards fly back in turn from 5.0–6.1 s.
When to use it: Use it in recommendation, matching or bookmarking mobile apps where users sort items one by one.
