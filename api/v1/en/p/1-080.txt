Please use "Particles that form words" (粒子が文字になる), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-080

What it is: A hero background where hundreds of glowing particles gather into a word and scatter when touched
What the sample shows: On a dark violet background, glowing dots form HELLO, part around the passing pointer, and burst apart on each click to re-form as NICE, then LUCIAN.
Spec:
- 720 particles spring (stiffness 38, damping 7.5) into a 5 × 7 dot-matrix font, 3 × 3 dots per cell at 4 px spacing; spare particles scatter on an outer ring at 0.18 opacity.
- The pointer pushes away particles within 60 px, displacing them by up to 26 px.
- In the demo, a click at 1.8 s switches to NICE, 3.6 s to LUCIAN, and 5.4 s back to HELLO; each switch gives every particle a random kick of ±130 px/s.
- Additive blending; hues span 250–330, and faster particles shift hue by up to 60 and brighten from 68% to 88% lightness.
When to use it: Use it in the hero of a product launch or event landing page to make a brand name or tagline memorable.
