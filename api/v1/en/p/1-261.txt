Motion Prompt #1-261

What it is: A fluid-simulation background where bursts of vivid ink keep shooting across a black screen, collide and curl, with glow around the bright parts and light rays spreading out
What the sample shows: Bursts of colourful ink shoot out from around a black screen, run into each other and swirl together. The bright parts glow and light rays stretch outward from the centre.
Spec:
- The real thing runs as a WebGL2 simulation; the preview replays a fixed sequence on a 108×42 grid baked at load (1/30 s steps, curl 30, pressure × 0.8, 20 Jacobi iterations).
- Every 0.8 s a jet fires for 4 steps: a Gaussian blob of radius 2.2 cells pulls velocity to 120 cells/s and adds 0.2 ink per step; 8 jets per cycle, position, direction and hue from a hash of the jet number.
- Velocity dissipation 0.2 and ink dissipation 1, both applied as ÷ (1 + dissipation·dt).
- Shading: the brightness gradient darkens steep slopes and lifts slopes facing up-left (factor 0.6–1.15).
- Bloom keeps the part above 0.45 brightness and blurs it with two box blurs of radius 3, added at 0.9; rays take 16 bloom samples toward the centre over 0.6 of the distance, weight × 0.9 per sample, added at 0.5.
- Final colour is 1 − e^(−1.7·x); the last 0.6 s of the cycle crossfades into the previous lap.
When to use it: Hero sections for music, gaming or event sites, or any dark screen that should make a strong first impression.
