Please use "Sentence reading focus" (読んでいる文のフォーカス), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-067

What it is: A reading focus mode that keeps only the sentence under the pointer sharp
What the sample shows: As the pointer moves between sentences, only that one sharpens while the rest blur and fade, and a purple bar on the left follows and resizes.
Spec:
- Of the paragraph's 5 sentences only the current one is sharp; the rest drop to 25% opacity with a slight blur, over .45 s.
- A 4 px purple bar on the left moves to the current sentence and stretches to its height (.55 s spring).
- The 'N / 5' counter at the top rolls 16 px per step (.5 s spring).
- In the demo the pointer moves to sentences 1→5 at 0 / 1.2 / 2.2 / 3.2 / 4.2 s and back to sentence 1 at 5.6 s.
When to use it: Use it as a reading mode for long blog posts, help docs and learning apps so readers keep their place.
