Motion Prompt #1-094

What it is: A live table where new rows slide in and rows pass each other when re-sorted
What the sample shows: New orders slide into the table with a flash; clicking the amount header makes rows pass each other into a new order, and the time header restores it.
Spec:
- 5 visible rows, 24 px tall; a new order slides in from the top with a purple flash that fades in 1.4 s.
- Other rows move to their new slots in 0.45 s, and rows pushed out fade over 0.35 s.
- In the demo new orders arrive at 0.8 s and 3.1 s, the amount header sorts descending at 2.2 s, and the time header restores order at 4.2 s.
- Each row's N s ago label counts up, showing just now under 3 s; the header's red dot pulses every 1.4 s.
When to use it: Use it in admin panels and dashboards where orders, transactions or logs arrive in real time.
