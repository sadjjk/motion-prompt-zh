Motion Prompt #4-042

What it is: A maths explainer on near-black where only what is being explained appears, colour-coded, and one object moves smoothly to make the point
What the sample shows: On black, axes grow from the origin, ticks light up in turn and y = sin x rises letter by letter; a blue curve traces left to right with a gold dot and tangent riding it, turning red where the slope hits 0; then everything dims out.
Spec:
- Background #0F1014; 0.2–0.9 s the axes grow from the origin, and ticks π/2, π, 3π/2, 2π and ±1 fade in 0.12 s apart.
- From 0.6 s the blue #58C4DD formula y = sin x rises letter by letter at top right, 0.08 s per letter.
- 1.6–4.6 s the sine curve (2π spans 420 px, amplitude 62 px) traces from the left, with a gold dot #F4D35E and an 84 px tangent riding it.
- The readout k = cos x updates live; near the peak and trough, where the slope is close to 0, tangent and readout turn red #FC6255. 5.4–6.0 s everything dims to black.
When to use it: Use it for maths, statistics and AI concept explainers, or whenever a function or graph needs to move.
