Motion Prompt #4-042

What it is: Thin lines ripple like cloth and lift under the cursor
What the sample shows: On a dark ground 26 thin horizontal lines undulate like contour lines, with a brighter thicker line every few; as a simulated cursor passes left to right, the nearby lines lift softly and settle back.
Spec:
- 26 lines of 80 points each: y = base + 7·sin(0.018x + phase + 0.35j) + 4·sin(0.043x − 2 × phase + 0.35j).
- The phase turns once per 6.4 s; the second wave runs the other way 2 times.
- 1 line in every 5 is a brighter 2 px line; the rest are 1 px.
- A simulated cursor travels from −200 px off the left to 740 px off the right over 0.8–5.4 s, weaving 46 px up and down, lifting nearby lines by up to 26 px with a Gaussian (σx 56 px, σy 40 px).
When to use it: Use it for hero backgrounds, music or tech sites, or to show a hover-reactive background.
