Motion Prompt #4-002

What it is: A globe made of Chinese characters, mapped onto a rotating sphere
What the sample shows: Characters scattered over a sphere slowly spin; those in front turn bold and dark while those behind fade to pale shadows.
Spec:
- 160 characters from the Thousand Character Classic placed on a sphere of radius 84 px by a Fibonacci spiral; background #F2EEE6.
- The sphere spins 1 lap around its vertical axis every 6.4 s, tilted by 0.35 rad.
- Glyph size 11 px times the perspective factor 2.4 /(2.4 − z); front glyphs use weight 900, back glyphs weight 400 as faint shadows.
- The frontmost glyphs (z > 0.9) turn vermilion #C8231B.
When to use it: Use it as a hero background for publishing, education or culture brands, or as a transition in talks about language and characters.
