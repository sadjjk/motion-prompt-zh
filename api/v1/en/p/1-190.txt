Please use "Task status icon" (タスク状態アイコン), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-190

What it is: One icon that morphs smoothly between waiting, running, done and failed
What the sample shows: A three-row task list shows a raised disc with a dashed ring at the start of each row. Row by row the dashes gather into a spinning purple arc; when the arc closes the disc fills green, a check is drawn and the status on the right switches to done. The third row's arc stalls, turns red, shrinks to the centre and becomes a cross that shakes, then every row resets to waiting in turn.
Spec:
- The 3 task rows start at 0.40, 1.55 and 2.75 s; the waiting icon is a ring of 12 dashes (8.6 px radius) on a disc of 13 px radius.
- On start the dashes gather within 0.35 s into an arc covering 22% of the ring, turning from grey to purple and spinning at 1.25 turns per second.
- Rows 1 and 2 close their arcs at 1.65 and 2.90 s; 0.08 s later the disc fills green with a 7% pop, and the check is drawn from 0.22–0.5 s after closing.
- Row 3 fails: its arc slows to a stop from 4.05–4.20 s, turns red by 4.45 s and shrinks into the centre by 4.80 s, then the disc fills red and a cross is drawn (4.75–5.03 s).
- From 4.80–5.20 s the failed icon shakes side to side 3 times, up to 2.6 px, decaying.
- From 5.6 s the rows reset to the waiting dashes one by one, 0.08 s apart and 0.35 s each; still from 6.11–6.4 s.
When to use it: Use it in AI agent task lists, CI step views and batch job queues where several steps show their status side by side.
