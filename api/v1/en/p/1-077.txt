What it is: A starfield hero where stars rush toward you and a long press triggers warp
What the sample shows: Stars drift out from the centre; holding the button accelerates them into blue streaks with a glowing core, and releasing eases back to cruise.

- The pointer shifts the heading (x × 70, y × 60); demo presses at 1.2 s and releases at 3.6 s.
When to use it: Use it in hero sections for games, space-tech or AI product launches, paired with a long-press 'Start' or 'Launch' button.
