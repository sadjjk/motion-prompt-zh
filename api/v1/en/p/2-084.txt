Motion Prompt #2-084

What it is: Meshed gears turning at their tooth ratios
What the sample shows: A large blue gear turns clockwise while coral and yellow pinions meshed with it spin faster the other way, teeth staying in each other's gaps; a status line sits beside them.
Spec:
- Gears of 16, 10 and 8 teeth (module 6.4 px, pitch radius = 6.4 × teeth ÷ 2); the two small ones mesh with the big gear at −20° and 160°.
- The big gear turns 180° (8 teeth) clockwise per 4.7 s; the small ones counter-rotate at their tooth ratios (all 8 teeth per cycle).
- Starting angles are computed along each contact direction so teeth sit in gaps.
- 6, 5 and 4 holes; on the right a status line with dots that grow every 0.39 s.
When to use it: Use it for processing, settings or system status, or to explain how parts work together.
