What it is: A Fair Isle knit that grows row by row from V-shaped stitches
What the sample shows: Plump V stitches appear row by row from the bottom, each row running back and forth, building bands of lattice, snowflake and reindeer motifs.
Spec:

- 4 colours #F2ECE0, #C8322E, #1B2A3A and #E8B44A, repeating 12-row bands of lattice, snowflake and reindeer.
- Rows are knitted from the bottom up over 0.2–4.6 s, the current row advancing back and forth across the width.
- Holds until 5.4 s, then fades out over 5.4–6.0 s.
