Motion Prompt #3-091

What it is: A product sequence in three beats: the whole product, a close-up of the selling point, then the product in use
What the sample shows: The same blue thermos is the focus of all 3 panels. Panel 1, "whole · orbit", stands it on a turntable for one slow full turn, its highlight and logo sliding from side to side. Panel 2, "selling point · side-lit close-up", is a big close-up on black: a light sweeps left to right and the cap's grip ridges light up one by one, then a "non-slip ridges" label appears. Panel 3, "in use", is outdoors: a hand holds the bottle and sways it once, keeping it in the centre of the frame.
Spec:
- 9.4 s cycle. The 3 panels start at 0.3, 3.1 and 5.9 s, 2.8 s each.
- Whole: the bottle makes exactly 1 turn per panel; the logo is wide face-on, narrow side-on and hidden at the back.
- Close-up: the light runs from beyond the left edge to beyond the right; ridges are 7 px apart and brighter the nearer the light; the label appears at 45–60% of the panel.
- In use: the bottle sways once per panel. From 8.8 to 9.4 s the picture fades back to the opening frame.
When to use it: For shop and ad videos that must show what it is, why it is good and how it is used in a short time.
