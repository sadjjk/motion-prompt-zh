Motion Prompt #1-225

What it is: Dragging left or right on a number field changes its value, and pulling past the limits stretches the field like rubber until it springs back on release
What the sample shows: On a dark background the cursor presses the Opacity field and drags right; once the value hits 100 the right edge stretches, then springs back on release. Next it drags left past 0, and the left edge stretches and returns.
Spec:
- The field is 220×48 px with a range of 0–100, and the value changes by 1 for every 2 px the cursor moves.
- The d px beyond the range push the field's edge out by r(d) = 40·(1 − 1/(1 + d/80)), which grows ever more slowly.
- Only the edge on the pulled side stretches, and the height narrows by up to 6% with the stretch.
- On release the stretch returns to 0 as e^(−9τ)·cos(18τ). The drag goes right at 1.0–2.6 s and left at 3.2–4.6 s.
- A bar inside the field follows the value; while dragging the outline turns blue and the cursor becomes a left-right arrow. At 5.4–6.0 s the value goes back to the 40 of 0 s.
When to use it: Use it for property fields in design tools, video or audio editing panels and settings screens where numbers get nudged quickly.
