Please use "Cursor-lit borders" (カーソルで光る枠線), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-081

What it is: A cursor-following light that illuminates the borders of nearby cards
What the sample shows: The pointer sweeps in a wave across four dark cards; borders near it glow from violet to blue, then dim slowly once it passes.
Spec:
- 4 cards on a dark #0A0B10 background, corner radius 18 px, 1.5 px borders.
- A 160 px gradient glow centred on the pointer (violet to blue, transparent at 70% of the radius) lights the borders of adjacent cards at once.
- A faint violet glow of 220 px at 0.14 strength is layered inside the cards.
- Brightness approaches 1 at a rate of 8/s on enter and fades at 2.5/s on leave; in the demo the pointer sweeps across in a wave from 0.3–4.9 s.
When to use it: Use it on feature grids or pricing cards in dark-themed SaaS and developer-tool landing pages.
