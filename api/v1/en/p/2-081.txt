Please use "Map route animation" (ルートアニメーション), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #2-081

What it is: Pins drop on a map and a route draws between them
What the sample shows: On a faint map with grid lines, three pins drop and bounce in turn; a blue line draws along the dashed planned route, a glowing dot travels with it, and the end pin pops.
Spec:
- A faint landmass with a 54 px grid; the route (2 cubic Béziers) is shown dashed from the start.
- 3 pins drop from 30 px above with a bounce over 0.6 s, starting at 0.3 s and 0.18 s apart.
- Over 1.3–3.5 s a blue line (3.5 px, 9 px glow) draws with a sine ease-in-out, a dot with a halo riding its tip.
- Over 3.5–3.85 s the end pin pops to 1.25x; pins and line fade over 4.1–4.55 s.
When to use it: Use it for travel stories, delivery and logistics explainers, or directions to a store.
