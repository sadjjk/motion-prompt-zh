Motion Prompt #1-192

What it is: Inside a fixed frame an image goes from blurred colour blobs through mosaic levels to a sharp picture
What the sample shows: A prompt bubble sits on the left and an empty image frame on the right. Soft colour blobs drift inside the frame and settle into the composition, turn into a coarse coloured mosaic, then the squares get finer until a sunset lighthouse scene comes into focus. On completion the card gives a small bounce, a download button pops in and the lighthouse beam swings once, before it all fades back to the empty frame.
Spec:
- The image area stays 228 × 136 px; from 0.3–0.65 s the empty frame fades into soft colour blobs, which settle into the composition from 0.9–1.75 s.
- Mosaics of 6, 12, 24, 48 and 96 columns take over at 1.75, 2.25, 2.7, 3.08 and 3.42 s, each cross-fading in 0.09 s (the first in 0.28 s).
- From 3.76–3.98 s the finest mosaic gives way to the sharp image.
- The progress bar below the card covers Compose 0–24%, Colour 24–52%, Refine 52–97% and Done 97–100%, fast then slow within each stage.
- At 3.9 s the card bounces by up to 3.5%, at 4.12 s a download button springs in, and from 4.2–5.5 s the lighthouse beam swings out and back.
- From 5.62–6.08 s everything fades back to the empty frame.
When to use it: Use it for AI image generation, photo editing and upscaling, where image processing takes a few seconds and the wait needs to show progress.
