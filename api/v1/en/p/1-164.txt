Motion Prompt #1-164

What it is: Typing dots turn into a message, then a reply pops in
What the sample shows: The other person's bubble pops in on the left with 3 dots bouncing in turn; it widens into a text message, and an accent-coloured reply pops out on the right. Both then drift up and fade.
Spec:
- The other bubble pops in at 0.3 s from its bottom-left corner over 0.35 s (backOut, overshoot factor 2).
- Inside, 3 dots of radius 3 px bounce up to 4 px on a 0.9 s cycle, each 0.15 s behind the last.
- At 2.05 s the bubble widens from 58 → 128 px, swaps to text and springs from 0.85 → 1 (0.3 s, factor 3).
- At 3.05 s the accent reply pops in from its bottom-right corner over 0.4 s.
- From 4.8 s both bubbles rise 10 px and fade over 0.4 s, 0.08 s apart.
When to use it: Use it for support chat, messaging apps and chatbot introductions.
