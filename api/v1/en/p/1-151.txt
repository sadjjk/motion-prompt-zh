Motion Prompt #1-151

What it is: Hovered icons each make a small move that fits their meaning
What the sample shows: As the cursor passes along 4 icons, each tile lights up: the bell swings, the heart fills and pops, the gear turns and the envelope flap opens.
Spec:
- A row of 4 icon tiles (64×64, centres 90 apart); the cursor rests 0.7 s on each, the tile background lights up over 0.2 s (easeOut) and the icon turns accent orange.
- Bell: swings ±18° from its top, one back-and-forth every 0.3 s, fading linearly to 0 within 0.7 s.
- Heart: fills in and pops to 1.25 and back to 1 within 0.35 s. Gear: turns 90° over 0.5 s with back-out (overshoot 1.7).
- Envelope: the flap opens over 0.35 s (easeOut) and the letter rises 10px; each icon resets after the cursor leaves, and the cursor is back at its start by 4.25 s.
When to use it: Use it on toolbars, navigation or settings screens where icon buttons should feel responsive.
