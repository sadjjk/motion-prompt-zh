Motion Prompt #1-053

What it is: Particles circle a dotted orb on tilted orbits to show the AI is working
What the sample shows: Around a slowly spinning dotted orb, particles with short trails orbit on three tilted paths at different speeds.
Spec:
- Orb radius 58 px, 240 dots on a Fibonacci sphere; nearer dots are larger and brighter. It spins once per cycle, viewed 0.32 rad from above.
- Three orbits tilted 0.35, 1.05 and −0.6 rad, at 1.14 × the orb radius, with 8 particles each.
- They go round 1, 2 and 1 (in reverse) times per cycle.
- Each particle trails 5 fading copies. Label "Working…" at 15 px on the right.
When to use it: Use it for general agent work or a running tool. Skip it for waits under 2 s.
