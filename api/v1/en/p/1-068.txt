Please use "Rippling letters" (文字のばね波紋), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-068

What it is: A headline whose letters bob like springs as the pointer passes
What the sample shows: As the pointer glides left to right, the big letters beneath lift and tint purple, then bounce down; a button sends a wave across them.
Spec:
- The 6 letters of MOTION sit 66 px apart, weight 900, 62 px, and only move up and down.
- Letters near the pointer lift by a Gaussian (σ 34 px), up to 34 px; each has a spring: per frame v += (target − y)×0.16, v ×= 0.8.
- The higher a letter rises, the more purple it turns (mixed by y / 50), and its floor shadow gets fainter and narrower.
- 'Send wave' runs a 1.1 s pulse at 1.35× amplitude from left to right; demo sweeps at 0.4–3.0 s and clicks at 3.4 s.
When to use it: Use it on brand-site headlines, portfolio names or big event-page type to add a tactile hover response.
