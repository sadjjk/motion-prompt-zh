Motion Prompt #1-060

What it is: A dragged card bows softly against the direction it moves
What the sample shows: A white card is dragged side to side; the faster it moves, the more its sides bow backwards, straightening as it slows.
Spec:
- Card 170 × 100 px, corner radius 16 px; its centre swings ±90 px sideways once per cycle (sine).
- The middle of the left and right edges bows against the motion, in proportion to speed, up to about 9 px.
- The content rows shift with it, by up to about 6 px.
- A drag pointer (5 px dot and 10 px ring) sits on the top edge, with a shadow below.
When to use it: Use it on sortable cards and rows or draggable panels to give dragging a tactile feel.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/en/e/1-060.txt
Shared helper code: https://sadjjk.github.io/motion-prompt-zh/api/v1/kit/aiux.js
