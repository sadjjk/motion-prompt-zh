What it is: A broadcast-news package look: blue studio, channel bug, LIVE badge, picture-in-picture, two-tier lower third, scrolling ticker and a breaking banner
What the sample shows: A blue studio with light shafts and a slowly turning wireframe globe. The channel bug 晨光新闻 comes in at top left, with a blinking 直播 badge and the time at top right. A picture-in-picture pops open on the right as a subway train pulls into the platform. A headline bar tagged 最新 and a detail bar slide in from the left, while the ticker at the bottom keeps scrolling. Midway a red 突发 banner sweeps across. On reset the bars, the picture-in-picture and the bug retract in turn and the studio lights fade.
Spec:

- Ticker: exactly 2 laps per cycle, so 6.4 s matches 0 s.
- 突发 banner: sweeps in at 2.6 s, out at 3.5 s.
- Reset: from 5.4 s everything retracts in reverse, 0.35 s each.
When to use it: Use it for news, current affairs and breaking-event explainers.
