Please use "Contextual cursor" (コンテキストカーソル), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-167

What it is: A cursor that turns into a big labelled circle for the content under it
What the sample shows: 3 work cards sit in a row. The cursor is a small dot; over a card it springs into a large dark circle showing an icon and an action word (Play, View, Drag), then shrinks back to a dot when it leaves.
Spec:
- The resting cursor is a 5 px radius dot.
- Over a card it grows to a 34 px radius (0.4 s cubic-bezier(.34,1.45,.64,1)).
- An icon and a label sit inside: Play, View or Drag depending on the card.
- The pointer enters at 0.3 s, visits the 3 cards in turn and leaves from 4.3–5.0 s.
When to use it: Use it on portfolios mixing videos, projects and carousels, to show what a click will do.
