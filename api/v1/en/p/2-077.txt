Please use "Knot sentence" (結びの言葉), one of the names in "Motion graphics, part 3".
Motion Prompt #2-077

What it is: A hexagonal knot where one band carries a sentence, then twists shut back to the standard logo
What the sample shows: A hexagonal knot on a light grey ground: bands retract one by one, each prints a word, then twists shut back to the standard logo
Spec:
- 539×208 card, 6 s cycle. A hexagonal knot (radius 62, band width 9.5, 6 bands) on a light grey ground.
- From 0.55 s the six bands retract in 60° steps (i=5 first, i=1 last), leaving only the top band; the camera pushes in 1.24× from 1.15 s.
- The remaining band edge prints: every word (1.75 s), in this knot (2.7 s), was a guess (3.65 s), about the (4.55 s), next one (5.35 s), each along the curve and growing.
- At 5.35 s band 6 tucks back under band 1; at 5.9 s the knot twists shut (rotate 8°, scale 0.94) and letters fly off; the whole sentence fades after 5.95 s.
- The twist and scale return sinusoidally, matching u=0. On-canvas text is English (proper nouns kept).
When to use it: Logo narratives, brand stories, product intros
