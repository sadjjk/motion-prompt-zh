Motion Prompt #2-077

What it is: Copies of a letter wrapped round a spinning cylinder
What the sample shows: Three cylinders, each wrapped in ten copies of one character, spin about a horizontal axis in alternating directions; near copies are larger and brighter, far ones hidden.
Spec:
- 3 cylinders sit 150 px apart; each wraps 10 copies of one character (50 px) at 36° steps on a 62 px radius.
- One turn per 4.7 s, neighbours turning the opposite way; the middle cylinder is coral, the outer ones white.
- Each copy is squashed vertically by its depth z, scaled by perspective 400 / (400 − 62z), at opacity 0.2 + 0.8z.
- Copies on the back (z < 0.05) are not drawn.
When to use it: Use it for a 3D-looking logo, a looping title backdrop or a tech opener.
