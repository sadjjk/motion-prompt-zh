Motion Prompt #2-056

What it is: Liquid motion: glossy fluid that swells, pinches off, drips and merges back together
What the sample shows: Drops bulge out of the top band, neck and break free, a small droplet follows, and each splashes flat into the pool below.
Spec:
- 6 lanes under the top band, each dripping once every 3.2 s (2 times per cycle, phases staggered).
- 0–40%: the drop bulges and necks; 40–75%: it breaks off and falls (a smaller droplet trails 0.12 behind); 75–100%: it lands in the pool and spreads flat.
- Metaballs merge on a half-resolution 270×104 buffer via 4 px blur + contrast 24, then take the #FF5C0F→#FF1A70→#8F1FFF gradient.
- A mask differenced against itself shifted 1.5 px gives a top-left glossy rim; the drop. wordmark (60 px) stays in the centre.
When to use it: Use it for drink and cosmetics ads, music or event teasers, and brand openers that need a smooth, fluid feel.
