Motion Prompt #3-087

What it is: Fix the video's first and last frames with two images and describe only the change in between
What the sample shows: Three panels sit side by side. The first frame on the left and the last frame on the right never move, each with a lock icon and a "first frame · locked" / "last frame · locked" label. Only the middle "process only" panel plays: the figure walks from left to right, stops and raises an arm, ending in exactly the last frame's pose. A timeline below is pinned to the first and last frames, and the blue progress and playhead travel only between them.
Spec:
- 9.4 s cycle. The first and last frames appear over 0.2–0.5 s and then stay locked.
- The process panel plays from 1.0 to 7.4 s: the figure walks from x −1.6 m to 1.6 m, stopping to raise an arm at 82% of the process.
- The timeline runs from the centre of the first frame to the centre of the last; the playhead follows the process and the corner label reads "process NN%".
- From 8.8 to 9.4 s the picture fades back to the opening frame.
When to use it: For image-to-video when the opening and closing pictures are already decided and the AI only has to fill in the middle.
