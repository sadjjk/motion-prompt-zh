Motion Prompt #1-172

What it is: Cards rise in turn as numbers count up and sparklines draw
What the sample shows: 3 data cards float up one after another. Each number counts from 0 to its target, a small sparkline draws in from the left, and the growth tag fades in at the top right.
Spec:
- 3 cards float up from 30 px below and fade in (0.6 s out3, 0.12 s apart).
- Numbers count up to their targets from 0.5 s over 1.2 s (power2.out).
- The sparkline draws from 0.6 s over 1.0 s (inOut3); the growth tag fades in from 1.6 s over 0.4 s.
- Numbers, sparklines and tags are also staggered by 0.12 s per card; everything fades out from 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it for admin dashboards, monthly reports and KPI overviews.
