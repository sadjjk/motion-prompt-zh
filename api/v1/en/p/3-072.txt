Motion Prompt #3-072

What it is: Direct sun through a window frame casts a crisp grid of shadows on the person and the wall
What the sample shows: Light in the shape of 3×3 panes falls slantwise on the person and the light wall, with shadow everywhere else; as the clock runs from 15:00 to 15:40 the grid slides right and leans further.
Spec:
- Scene: wall #F3EEE6, floor #E4DCCF from y 168; a head-and-shoulders figure #5A6884 (head centre x 270, y 78, radius 29 px).
- Panes: 3×3 panes of 52×46 px with 9 px frame bars; the panes stay lit and everything else is multiplied with #AEA9BB as shadow (hard edges).
- Over one pass the grid moves from x 96 to 176 and its slant (sideways shift per 1 px of y) grows from 0.42 to 0.72.
- The top-right clock runs from 15:00 to 15:40.
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For afternoon rooms, cafés or noir looks that should suggest passing time or stillness.
