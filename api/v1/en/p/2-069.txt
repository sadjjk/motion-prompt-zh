Please use "Coin flip" (コインフリップ), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #2-069

What it is: Letters spin like coins as they hop
What the sample shows: Four gold coins carrying characters hop up in turn, spin twice, land and bounce once; the back shows a star.
Spec:
- 4 coins of 36 px radius sit 96 px apart; they start at 0.35 s, 0.2 s apart, and each move lasts 1.7 s.
- In the first 28% a coin rises 96 px (cubic ease-out) through one turn, and by 42% it falls through a second turn (the turn is drawn as scaleX = cos).
- A 17 px hop follows until 48%, landing at 54%; the shadow shrinks with height.
- The front shows the character, the back a star; the rim is a darker disc offset 4 px in the turn direction.
When to use it: Use it for playful captions about luck, prizes, rewards or games.
