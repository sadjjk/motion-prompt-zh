Motion Prompt #1-199

What it is: A button that shows a busy state after the click, then the result
What the sample shows: After the click, the subscribe button shrinks into a spinning circle, then widens in green with a check and "Subscribed", and resets.
Spec:
- One 4.8 s cycle. The cursor moves to the button at 0.2–0.95 s and clicks at 1.1 s.
- At 1.15–1.45 s the button narrows from 140 to 32 px (cubic ease-in-out), the label fades and the colour drops to 0.75 opacity as a disabled state.
- Once narrowed, a white arc of radius 8 px spins once every 0.8 s.
- At 2.7–3.05 s it widens back to 140 px and turns green; a check draws at 2.9–3.2 s and "Subscribed" fades in at 2.95–3.2 s.
- At 4.1–4.45 s it returns to the orange button and its label.
When to use it: Use it on submit, save or pay actions that take time, to block double clicks and show progress and the result.
