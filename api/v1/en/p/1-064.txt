Motion Prompt #1-064

What it is: Video captions that pop in word by word in time with speech
What the sample shows: When playback starts, words bounce up one at a time, key words swell and tilt before landing in yellow, and finished lines fold up and vanish.
Spec:
- Words follow the speech rhythm: .30 s each plus a random 0–.08 s; key words get an extra .12 s before and .22 s after.
- Normal words pop in over .35 s from 12 px below at .6 scale, easing cubic-bezier(.3,1.6,.5,1).
- Key words swell to 1.45× and tilt -3° within .6 s, then settle back to 1× while turning yellow #FFE066.
- A finished line folds up 70° and rises 26 px as it fades (.45 s); 26 voice bars below change height every 90 ms.
When to use it: Use it as motion captions in short videos, product demos and podcast clips to stress key numbers or selling points.
