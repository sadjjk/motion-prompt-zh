Motion Prompt #3-054

What it is: Head and shoulders fill the frame, so small expressions lead
What the sample shows: Head and shoulders fill the frame: the eyes shift to one side, there is a blink, the body leans back a little, and finally the gaze returns to the front.
Spec:
- Frame: 1.4 m to 1.85 m (shoulders to the top of the head); camera 2.6 m away.
- Gaze: shifts 0.05 m to one side from 0.4 s to 0.9 s and returns from 3.7 s to 4.4 s.
- A blink at 1.55 s; the body leans back 0.05 m from 0.9 s to 1.3 s and returns with the gaze.
- The background is clearly blurred; top right reads "特写 CU" and the scale band runs from the shoulders to the head.
- The camera never moves; one pass = 4.7 s and the first and last frames match.
When to use it: For moments where the audience should catch a feeling, a reaction or a small change in expression.
