Motion Prompt #2-074

What it is: Blurry ink gathers into crisp letters, then bleeds away
What the sample shows: On rice-paper beige, four characters in turn tighten from soft ink blots into crisp shapes, hold, then spread and vanish.
Spec:
- 4 characters (68 px) starting at 0.3 s, 0.25 s apart; one character's cycle is 3.6 s.
- 0–55%: blur from 16 px to 0 px and scale from 1.3x to 1x (smoothstep), appearing over the first 15%.
- 55–80% holds sharp; 80–100% spreads to 16 px blur and 1.2x while fading.
- The blurred ink is thresholded with contrast(16) so blots merge at the edges, then multiplied onto the paper.
When to use it: Use it for titles with an East Asian ink, calligraphy, tea or sake feel.
