Motion Prompt #2-076

What it is: A framed stamp slams onto the page
What the sample shows: Two red framed stamps, “urgent” and “approved”, slam onto a document in turn from large and at an angle; the paper jolts and an imprint ring spreads.
Spec:
- Two stamps (170×100 px, angled −9° and 7°) land at 0.35 s and 0.9 s.
- Each goes from 2.4x to 0.93x in 0.24 s (cubic ease-in), then back to 1x over 0.12 s.
- On landing the paper jolts by 2.5 px·e^(−14t) and an outline ring grows to 1.3x and fades within 0.36 s.
- 160 seeded punched-out dots and 6 thin scratches make the ink patchy; the stamps fade over 3.9–4.4 s.
When to use it: Use it to mark a decision: approved, passed, sold out or limited.
