Motion Prompt #3-079

What it is: In a dark room only a screen is lit, its cool white light reaching the face and hands from the front
What the sample shows: The room goes dark and only the laptop in front of the person glows; cool white light from slightly below reaches the face and chest and falls off fast, jumping in brightness whenever the screen changes.
Spec:
- Scene 1: the head-and-shoulders figure; the room turns to the dark scene with #0F1116 at 0.8 laid over it.
- Source: a laptop (116×50 px, seen from behind) at (270, 176); light on the person runs from #DCE8FF to shadow over a radius of 120 px.
- Spill: #BED7FF over a radius of 110 px added at 0.32 opacity; a 2 px line of light leaks from the top edge of the screen.
- Screen changes: at 1.9 s and 3.1 s, for about 0.06 s, the light radius jumps by up to 1.18×; top right reads "6500K".
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For late-night work, gaming or scrolling scenes that should feel lonely or absorbed.
