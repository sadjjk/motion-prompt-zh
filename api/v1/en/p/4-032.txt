Motion Prompt #4-032

What it is: A card flourish on green felt where playing cards fan out and flip face up and back
What the sample shows: Cards fly from a deck on green felt into a fan, flip face up one by one, turn back over, then gather into the deck again.
Spec:
- Green felt radial gradient #1F5A3E → #0A2016. 24 cards of 38 × 54 px.
- Over 0.2–1.95 s they fly from the central deck into a fan with a total spread of 1.7 rad, bobbing 6 px while fanned.
- Over 1.9–3.8 s they flip face up one by one, and over 3.9–5.35 s back to the dark red crosshatched back one by one.
- Over 5.4–6.26 s they gather back into the deck in reverse order, 0.4 s per card.
When to use it: Openers for magic, board game or casino content, game event promos, or a presentation moment that fans out a set of options.
