Motion Prompt #4-078

What it is: On toned linen, thin long strokes block in the big shapes, then short thick dabs of paint pile on top, each catching light on one edge and shadow on the other
What the sample shows: A still life of a bottle and a pear on linen. Background, table, bottle and pear are blocked in thinly in that order, thick dabs build on top, and near-white highlights go on the side facing the top-left light.
Spec:
- Tan linen #B8865B with a 3 px cross weave. A green bottle stands at x 236–276 and a yellow pear at the right, against a dark background over a brown table edge at y 132.
- 0.3–1.8 s: long block-in strokes 30–50 px long and 14 px wide at 70% opacity cover the canvas, background first, then table, bottle and pear; on the bottle and pear they are shortened to stay inside the shape, down to 10 px.
- 1.8–3.8 s: short impasto dabs 12–20 px long and 8–10 px wide pile on in the same order, again kept inside the bottle and pear; each is lighter on the side toward the top-left light and carries a 1.5 px light edge on one side and a dark edge on the other.
- 3.8–4.6 s: the thickest near-white highlights #F2E6C8 go down the left of the bottle and on the pear.
- At 5.4–6.0 s the painting fades back to bare linen; at 0 s and 6.4 s the card is bare linen, so the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it for galleries, fine art, premium products or anything with a classical mood.
