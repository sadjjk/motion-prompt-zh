Motion Prompt #1-184

What it is: A button that turns notifications on with a swinging bell
What the sample shows: A raised white pill button shows a gold bell on the left and the label 「开启通知」 on the right. The cursor clicks: the pill turns blue and narrows, the label slides up into 「已开启」, the bell swings and sound arcs spread out. A second click turns it white again, a slash cuts across the bell with a small shake, and the slash fades to restore the first frame.
Spec:
- 542×191 card, 4.5 s cycle. A raised pill button 76 px tall, with a gold bell in a radius 27 well on the left and the label on the right.
- Click at 0.9 s. Over 0.92–1.32 s the pill turns blue and narrows to fit the shorter label; the old label slides 16 px up and out as the new one rises in.
- From 0.95 s the bell swings about its hook at 0.42 rad and 2.4 Hz, damping out, with the clapper trailing 0.1 s behind.
- Sound arcs start on both sides at 0.98, 1.16 and 1.34 s, each spreading 19 px out from radius 24 and fading over 0.65 s.
- Second click at 2.55 s: it returns to off over 2.58–2.98 s, a slash draws across the bell over 2.60–2.85 s with a 0.15 rad shake, and the slash fades over 3.75–4.15 s.
When to use it: Use it for subscribe-to-updates buttons, app notification settings or stream reminders where notifications are switched on and off.
