Motion Prompt #1-229

What it is: Liquid in a glass jar chases the target value, its surface tilting and rippling while it moves, and splashes up when it hits the top at full
What the sample shows: In a glass jar on a dark background, the liquid chases each new target, overshoots a little and its surface tilts and sloshes. Finally it hits 100% and splashes, and the number on the right turns teal.
Spec:
- The target starts at 20% and rises to 55% at 0.6 s, 85% at 2.0 s and 100% at 3.2 s, then drops back to 20% at 5.4 s.
- The level chases the target with the spring x'' = 40(target − x) − 9x', integrated from 0 s in 1/120 s steps; anything past 100% stops at the top.
- The left-right height difference of the surface is proportional to the level's speed, up to 0.35 times the jar width. 2 ripples, each a whole multiple of the cycle, run on the surface.
- The first time it reaches 100%, 8 droplets fly up and fall back along parabolas, gone within 1 s.
- At 6.0–6.4 s the remaining sway settles and the level is brought to exactly 20%, matching 0 s.
When to use it: Use it to show something filling up, such as storage or quota, goal progress or a water intake log.
