Motion Prompt #1-153

What it is: The hovered tile grows and the rest of the grid makes room
What the sample shows: In a 3×2 bento grid, the cursor rests on the top-left and then the bottom-right tile; each time that tile grows with a larger title while the others shrink to make room.
Spec:
- A 3×2 bento grid (471×144, 8px gaps) where every column and row starts at 1fr.
- When the cursor rests on a tile, its column grows to 2.4fr and its row to 1.8fr over 0.55 s (easeInOut power 3) while the other columns and rows shrink.
- The focused tile's title grows from 13px to 18px.
- Timeline: top-left tile 0.45–2.0 s, bottom-right tile 2.0–3.6 s, then the cursor leaves and the grid evens out again.
When to use it: Use it for feature overviews, dashboards or portfolios where grid tiles should take turns in the spotlight.
