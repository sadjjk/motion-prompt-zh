Motion Prompt #1-175

What it is: A small image grows to fill the screen as you scroll
What the sample shows: A small rounded image sits in the middle of a dark screen. As scrolling progresses it grows to fill the frame, its corners turn square and the scene inside eases back; late in the scroll a title rises in from below, and scrolling back reverses it all.
Spec:
- Scroll progress goes 0 → 1 from 0.4–4.6 s, with the scrollbar on the right in sync.
- The image grows linearly from 38% × 46% of the frame to 100%, with corners going 16 → 0 px.
- Inside, the scene scales from 1.3× down to 1× at the same time.
- From 70%–100% progress the title rises 20 px and fades in.
- From 5.4–6.3 s the scroll returns and everything plays in reverse.
When to use it: Use it in travel stories, product features and brand stories to give one image a dramatic reveal.
