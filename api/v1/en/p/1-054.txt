Motion Prompt #1-054

What it is: A bright meridian scans a dotted globe to show the AI is searching
What the sample shows: A dotted globe turns while a vertical line of light sweeps over it, brightening the dots it passes.
Spec:
- Orb radius 58 px, 240 dots; it spins once per cycle.
- The scanning meridian goes round 2 times per cycle (2 × the spin), drawn 1 px wide at 0.35 opacity.
- Dots near the meridian brighten by up to +0.75, falling off as exp(−Δ²/0.05) with the angle Δ.
- Label "Searching the web…" at 15 px on the right.
When to use it: Use it while the AI is searching the web, retrieving documents or looking something up.
