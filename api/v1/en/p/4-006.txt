Motion Prompt #4-006

What it is: A glitch-art poster torn apart by signal interference
What the sample shows: SIGNAL LOST splits into RGB ghosts on a dark poster while horizontal slices jump sideways under coloured noise bars and scanlines.
Spec:
- Poster text SIGNAL LOST in 64 px black weight on dark #0B0B0E, with red accents #FF3B5C.
- The 3 RGB channels split 3–9 px apart horizontally, peaking 3 times every 6.4 s.
- Interference changes 10 times per second: each frame shifts 7 horizontal slices 2–20 px tall by up to ±32 px, plus 5 red or cyan noise bars.
- A scanline every 3 px; one cycle has 64 frames and the last reuses frame 0, so the loop is seamless.
When to use it: Use it to open game, music or tech videos, on error or maintenance pages, or as a cyber-styled poster.
