Motion Prompt #1-256

What it is: A foil card where 24 rainbow rays burst from a central emblem and swirl as the angle changes
What the sample shows: Rainbow rays spread from the centre of a plum card and turn and change colour each time it sways; a rose-gold eight-point star sits in the middle.
Spec:
- Card 126 × 168 px (3:4), corner radius 0.045 × width, thickness 0.036 × width; it swings on its own in a 6.4 s loop: yaw 24°·sin a, pitch 11.5°·sin 2a, roll 1.6°·sin a and a 1.3 % bob.
- 24 rays from the centre (cos(angle × 24) to the power 2.4) multiplied by fine diagonal hairlines; hue = azimuth + radius × 1.35 + tilt × 1.6. The emblem is a rose-gold eight-point star on a gold frame.
- Layers from back to front: base pattern (−0.082 W), emblem (−0.03 W), then surface foil and a bevelled metal frame; tilting shifts each layer by its depth, so the card reads as deep.
- The light direction turns with the angle and a diagonal sheen crosses the face; cross-star glints twinkle on frame corners and the emblem tip.
When to use it: Use it for achievement badges, rank-up or winner announcements and premium membership cards.
