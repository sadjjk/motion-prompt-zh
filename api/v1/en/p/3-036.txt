Motion Prompt #3-036

What it is: A very small subject shot so it fills the frame
What the sample shows: A tiny fly agaric half buried in moss fills the height of the frame: a round dome cap covered in rows of small white warts, white membrane scraps on the rim, a slender white stem with a skirt ring just under the cap, and a bulbous base wrapped in scale rings. Focus starts far behind with everything soft, then pulls onto the mushroom. Big foreground grass blades, the grass behind and the coloured bokeh blobs of the background stay blurred by how far they are from the focus plane. On the cap an ant walks from right to left, turns and walks back. The camera pushes in very slowly, and the "1 cm" scale bar at the bottom left grows with it. In a small side-view diagram at the bottom right, a thin blue band marking the sharp zone moves with the focus. At the end focus returns to the distance and the push-in eases back.
Spec:
- One pass is 4.7 s. Focus pulls from far (depth 0.95) onto the mushroom (0.5) over 0.5–1.4 s and goes back over 4.2–4.7 s.
- Blur = depth difference from the focus plane × 14 px; the 16 grass blades sit in 3 layers at depths 0.06–0.9, and the 6 background bokeh blobs scale by only 30% of the push-in.
- Very slow push-in: up to 1.08× over 0.5–4.2 s, back over 4.2–4.7 s; the scale bar is 1 cm = 64 px and grows with the push-in.
- Ant: right to left over 0.3–2.1 s, turns at 2.3 s, walks back over 2.5–4.3 s and turns again at 4.5 s; 4 steps each way.
- A 92 × 46 px depth-of-field diagram at the bottom right; "微距 MACRO" at the top right.
When to use it: For insects, flowers, food or product textures, details the eye barely sees.
