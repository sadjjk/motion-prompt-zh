Motion Prompt #2-098

What it is: Bars stretch and shrink like playing audio
What the sample shows: On a player panel 44 thin rounded bars stretch and shrink unevenly about the centre line, the played part in coral, while a ripple spreads around the play button on the left.
Spec:
- 44 bars (3.6 px wide) grow symmetrically about the centre line, up to 72 px.
- Each bar runs 12–18 segments per 4.7 s (about 0.26–0.39 s each).
- Each segment eases (sine in-out) to a fixed random height between 0.12× and 1×.
- The left 17 bars are coral (played); the play button ripples every 1.175 s.
When to use it: Use it for podcasts, music, voice notes or any audio feature.
