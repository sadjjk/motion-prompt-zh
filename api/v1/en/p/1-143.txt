Motion Prompt #1-143

What it is: Cards picked by clicking, with a bar counting the choices
What the sample shows: The cursor picks 3 of 4 cards in turn; each gains a blue outline and check, a bar springs up from the bottom and its count rolls up, and "Clear" deselects all and drops the bar.
Spec:
- 4 image cards, 110×100, 14 apart; the cursor clicks cards 1, 3 and 4 at 0.8, 1.5 and 2.2 s.
- A picked card shrinks to 0.96 in 0.25 s (easeOut) and gets a 2.5px blue outline; the round check at top right swells to 1.25 and settles, fills blue and draws a white check.
- On the first pick a 220×38 action bar springs up to y 136 in 0.4 s with back.out(1.4); the count in "N selected" rolls up in 0.25 s.
- At 3.4 s "Clear" is clicked: the picks are undone in 0.25 s and the bar drops away over 3.45–3.8 s.
When to use it: Use it for bulk photo selection, template picking or interest onboarding where several items are chosen before moving on.
