Motion Prompt #4-034

What it is: A print cut into wood with gouges and printed in just 2 colours, black and paper
What the sample shows: Only 2 colours, black and paper. The sky is rows of parallel black gouge lines that thicken towards the top, with the sun cut away completely. The sea is a big black mass; spray and currents are white cuts that follow the wave shapes, and the crests are upturned triangular nicks. A fisherman in a straw hat (no face drawn) leans back on the rocking boat and hauls up a net, showing the fish inside. Gulls flap overhead. Wood grain shows faintly in the black and coarse paper texture sits over everything.
Spec:
- 6.4 s cycle; continuous loop with no reset segment.
- The front and back waves each roll forward 1 lap per cycle; the fine ripples go 2 laps.
- The boat rides up and down with the front wave, and the fisherman leans back to haul the net 1 time per cycle.
- Gulls flap 3–5 times per cycle. The sky and headland are drawn directly every frame so they stay crisp when enlarged.
When to use it: Use it for posters, book covers, folk tales and sea stories that want strength and a handmade feel.
