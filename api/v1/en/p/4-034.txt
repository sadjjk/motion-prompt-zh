Motion Prompt #4-034

What it is: A cyan laser reading the barcode on a packing slip
What the sample shows: A white packing slip settles onto a grey desk, a cyan laser sweeps the barcode left to right and switches off, and a green "Verified · ready to ship" rises in.
Spec:
- A white packing slip, 491×180, on a pale grey desk #e2e8f0→#cbd5e1. The barcode has 29 bars (width ×0.8, 5.6px gaps; 399px wide and 68px tall overall).
- The slip fades in over 0.18–1.02 s, rising 9px and straightening from -0.6° (quartic ease-out).
- A cyan laser #06b6d4 switches on over 0.9–1.14 s, sweeps 412px from the left over 1.02–3.6 s with a quadratic ease-in-out, and switches off over 3.78–4.2 s.
- A green status "Verified · ready to ship" #059669 rises 3px into view over 3.66–4.2 s. The slip fades over 5.4–6.0 s, leaving the empty desk by 6.4 s.
- Versus Radar sweep: the scan line passes over the barcode once in one direction and then gives a result, instead of circling continuously.
When to use it: Use it for logistics, warehouse, inventory or e-commerce fulfilment promos, or to signal that an item or check has been verified.
