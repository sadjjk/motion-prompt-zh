Motion Prompt #2-093

What it is: A word is struck out and a better one handwritten
What the sample shows: A slanted coral bar strikes through the white word 便宜 from the left and it dims; a curved arrow points down and a yellow brush-style 实惠 is written out character by character behind a pen dot.
Spec:
- Over 0–0.3 s the old word fades in; over 0.4–0.85 s a 7 px bar at −4° draws through it from left to right.
- Over 0.85–1.1 s the old word dims to 0.4; over 0.9–1.15 s a curved arrow draws.
- The new word, in yellow brush script, writes out left to right from 1.15 s, 0.45 s apart and 0.4 s per character, with a pen dot.
- Over 4.0–4.5 s everything fades out.
When to use it: Use it to stress a better wording in copy, for before/after messages or corrections.
