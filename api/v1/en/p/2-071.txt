Motion Prompt #2-071

What it is: Text ripples like a flag in the wind
What the sample shows: Four characters hang from a pole on the left; each narrows, tilts and lifts slightly later than the last, so a wave runs to the right.
Spec:
- A pole on the left (8 px wide, yellow knob on top) with 4 characters (58 px) running to the right.
- Each character, pivoting on its left edge, takes scaleX = cos(38°·f), skewY −9°·f and moves up 4 px·f.
- f goes 0→1→0 over 1.175 s (cosine), delayed 0.17 s per character.
- Even-position characters are coral, odd ones white.
When to use it: Use it for cheers, slogans and sports titles.
