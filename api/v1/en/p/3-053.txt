Motion Prompt #3-053

What it is: Framed from the chest up, centred on speech and expression
What the sample shows: The person, chest up and slightly left of centre, says two lines: the mouth moves and a subtitle appears with each line, there is a blink in the pause between them, and a "！" pops up above the head at the end.
Spec:
- Frame: 1.2 m to 1.9 m, bottom edge at the chest; camera 2.2 m away, the person 0.35 m left of centre leaving the right side for the subtitle.
- Speech in two parts, 0.4 s to 2.0 s and 2.9 s to 4.3 s; the mouth opens and closes with each, with one wide opening late in each part.
- A 239×26 px subtitle bar fades in and out with the speech, reading "今天的风，比昨天轻一点。"
- A blink in the pause at 2.55 s; a white "！" on a blue dot pops up above the head from 4.25 s to 4.5 s. The body sways slightly while speaking.
- Labels: top right reads "中近景 MCU"; the scale band runs from the chest to the head; background softly blurred; one pass = 4.7 s.
When to use it: For interviews, dialogue and monologues where speaking is the point.
