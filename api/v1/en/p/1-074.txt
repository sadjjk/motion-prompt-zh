Please use "Drum time picker" (ドラム式時刻ピッカー), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-074

What it is: A drum-style time picker whose values curve away around a wheel
What the sample shows: Dragging a wheel rolls the numbers to rest on the centre line, far values tip away and fade, and the alarm time and countdown update.
Spec:
- 3 wheels for AM/PM, hour and minute, 34 px per step; the value on the 36 px tall light bar at the centre is selected.
- A value d steps from centre tips back (rotateX d × 22°, max 80°) and fades to 1 − |d| × 0.28 opacity, no lower than .15.
- Wheels ease with cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); 120 ms after stopping, the title and 'rings in X h Y min' update.
- Demo: hour from 6 to 9 at 0.5–1.5 s, minute from 30 to 45 at 2.0–3.0 s, AM to PM at 3.5–4.1 s, all back at 5.4–6.3 s.
When to use it: Use it for picking times or dates in alarm, booking and calendar apps.
