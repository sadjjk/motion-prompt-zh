Motion Prompt #1-124

What it is: Cards are dealt one by one into a fan
What the sample shows: Pressing Deal sends 5 cards up from the bottom in turn into a fan; later, Collect sends them back down starting with the last card.
Spec:
- The cursor clicks Deal at 0.5 s; 5 cards (60×84) leave the bottom one by one, 0.12 s apart, 0.55 s each.
- They fan out to −24°…+24° (12° apart) around a shared pivot 132 below the card bottoms; the angle uses back-out with overshoot 1.7 while the cards rise 150px with easeOut power 3.
- At 3.1 s Collect is clicked; from 3.2 s, starting with the last card, each card folds back and drops 0.1 s apart over 0.35 s (easeInOut power 3).
When to use it: Use it to reveal a set of card-style products, plans or perks, or for game-like moments.
