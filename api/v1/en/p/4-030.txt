Motion Prompt #4-030

What it is: An infinity room where coloured lights recede endlessly in a dark mirrored space
What the sample shows: The camera glides forward through lights in 4 hues hanging in darkness, mirrored in the floor, their brightness breathing in waves into the depth.
Spec:
- Black background #020205. Lights in 4 hues 320 / 40 / 185 / 265; about 80% of 17 slots per row are lit, rows 0.55 apart.
- The camera moves forward 4 rows (2.2) every 6.4 s; the layout repeats every 4 rows, so the loop is seamless.
- Brightness breathes 2 times per cycle, phase-shifted with depth; lights fade at the near and far limits.
- Floor reflections are at 0.35 brightness; floor grid lines move with the camera, and a vignette darkens the edges.
When to use it: Openers for music, nightlife or art exhibition promos, immersive hero backgrounds, or a waiting screen before a livestream.
