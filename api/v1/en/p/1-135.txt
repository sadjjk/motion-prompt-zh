Please use "Flow field" (フローフィールド), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-135

What it is: Fine lines drift along an invisible current, and the cursor stirs it
What the sample shows: Thin purple and blue lines stream across a dark background like wind or water and fade away; where the cursor passes, the flow curls into a swirl.
Spec:
- 220 fine lines advance at 60 px/s along a flow field made of 2 summed sines whose phase turns once every 6.4 s.
- Each trail shows the last 0.6 s; a line lives 1.6 s (4 generations per cycle) and fades in and out with a sine.
- Birth positions come from the index and generation, and each frame re-integrates from the birth point, so no state is kept.
- Around the cursor (Gaussian σ 70 px) the flow twists tangentially into a swirl; in the demo it takes hold from 0.9–1.2 s and releases from 4.4–4.8 s.
When to use it: Use it as a hero or portfolio background, or wherever a generative mood fits.
