Motion Prompt #1-246

What it is: Pressing refresh draws all four swatches into circles at once; they switch to the new colours at their roundest and spring back out into their slots
What the sample shows: On a deep night-blue gradient sit two pills: on the left a dark slate pill holding a bar of four colour swatches, on the right a narrow coral-pink key holding only a white refresh icon. It starts on a ramp from light cyan to blue-violet. When the pointer rests on the key the white arrows fade a little; each press sinks the key and turns the arrows half a turn. All four swatches draw into circles at once, switch at their roundest to a set of orange, green and violet, and spread back a little too far before settling in their slots, while the left pill dips and bounces with them. The third press brings back the first set.
Spec:
- The original 300 px layout is drawn at 1.4 scale: swatches 58 px tall with a 14 px corner and 4 px seams; 10 px padding inside each pill, 6 px between the pills, a 30 px wide refresh key.
- Refresh is pressed at 1.2 s, 3.0 s and 4.8 s; each change runs 0.76 s and all four swatches share one clock.
- At 40% of the run each swatch is a circle 0.66 of 58 px across and the colour swaps there; the return uses a back-out (1.7) that overshoots before it settles.
- The left pill dips by 3.5% and recovers, the refresh key sinks to 0.96, and the icon turns 180° per press (600ms).
- Three sets are made in OKLCH (ramp, triad, accent) and ordered light to deep; the third press returns to the first set.
- The ground is a deep night-blue radial gradient, the swatch pill dark slate and the refresh key coral pink with a white icon; the deepest swatch is kept at lightness 0.56 or above so it still stands out on the dark ground.
When to use it: Use it for palette generators, theme settings, colour panels in design tools and brand-setup onboarding.
