Please use "Indeterminate to determinate" (不確定から確定への進捗), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-089

What it is: An indeterminate progress bar that turns into step-by-step determinate progress
What the sample shows: On a deploy card, streaks sliding through the bar give way to the same bar filling step by step, log lines float in and tick off, and the status pill turns green.
Spec:
- A 256 × 6 px track; while queued, 2 streaks stretch and slide through on a 1.6 s cycle, streak 2 delayed by 0.55 s, both arriving at 60% of the cycle.
- In the demo the bar switches to determinate progress at 1.8 s and advances through 5 steps to 22%, 38%, 70%, 85% and 100%.
- Step i lasts (650 + 120i) ms × 0.6 and the fill eases over 0.5 s; log lines float up 8 px one by one and spinners turn into checks.
- The status pill goes queued #D6731E → building #6A57F2 → ready #119C72 with 0.4 s colour changes; the run completes at about 4.5 s.
When to use it: Use it for deploys, builds or exports whose duration is unknown at first and becomes measurable partway through.
