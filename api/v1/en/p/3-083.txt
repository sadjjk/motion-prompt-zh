What it is: Whether light is warm orange or cool blue, measured in kelvin (K)
What the sample shows: A kelvin scale sits at the lower right; its pointer moves 5500 K → 2000 K → 10000 K → 5500 K, and the picture turns orange at low values and blue at high ones.

- Kelvin path: 5500 → 2000 K over 0.4–1.4 s, → 10000 K over 2.0–3.0 s, → 5500 K over 3.6–4.6 s (each ease-in-out).

- Scale: a 150×34 px white tag at the lower right with a 2000–10000 K colour strip and a triangular pointer; top right shows the value rounded to 100 K.
When to use it: To explain white balance and colour casts, or to show time of day and mood through colour.
