What it is: An Islamic girih tiling of eight-point stars and crosses that lights up from the centre
What the sample shows: Turquoise tiles with cobalt eight-point stars light up one by one outward from the centre on dark navy, each star scaling open.
Spec:
- Dark navy background #0F1E3A. 11 × 7 tiles of 62 px, the whole grid tilted 0.12 rad.
- Each tile: turquoise #1F8A8A ground, cream cross, cobalt #1C3F94 eight-point star (16 vertices), gold #D9A93C outline and 8 radiating lines.
