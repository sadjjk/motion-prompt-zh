Motion Prompt #4-089

What it is: On a green board, bundles of parallel traces gather onto a copper bar, pale cyan light flows down a wide channel and LEDs light up in turn, showing where data or power flows as a circuit-board diagram
What the sample shows: On a green board, a copper bar spreads out from the middle and 11 traces on each side join it. Pale cyan light flows down the channel, two rows of 8 LEDs light from the middle outwards, and the silkscreen appears.
Spec:
- A green board darkening towards the bottom with a faint dot grid every 6 px. 0.2–0.6 s: a wide teal channel fades in down the middle, plus a narrower channel that bends from the bar to the bottom right.
- 0.5–1.1 s: a copper bar 400 px long spreads out from the middle. 0.9–2.2 s: 11 parallel traces on each side are drawn from the inside out, each running out of the channel and then sloping down onto the bar. From 2.0 s: 16 pale cyan streaks flow down the wide channel and loop, and dashed light runs along the side traces and the lower channel.
- 1.0–1.5 s: two rows of 8 LEDs, the J2 connector with 4 pads and the bus lines fade in. 2.6–3.8 s: the LEDs light 0.14 s apart from the middle outwards, and the bus lines glow at 3.6–4.0 s.
- 3.8–4.6 s: silkscreen labels G9–G16, G1–G8 and J2 appear, with '锦屏 JINPING' top left and '九毫秒 NINE MILLISECONDS' top right. 10 soft bokeh spots drift in brightness throughout.
- At 5.4–6.0 s everything but the background fades out; at 0 s and 6.4 s the card shows only the background, so the loop restarts cleanly.
When to use it: Use it for networks, data flow, power grids, or anything about hardware and chips.
