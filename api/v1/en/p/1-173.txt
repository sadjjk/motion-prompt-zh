Please use "Table crosshair highlight" (表のクロスハイライト), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-173

What it is: The pointed cell's row and column light up and the cell enlarges
What the sample shows: A pointer moves across a 4 × 4 table of numbers. The whole row and column of the cell it rests on tint softly into a cross, and that cell fills with the accent colour with its number bold, white and larger. Leaving the table clears it.
Spec:
- The row and column are tinted with the accent at 0.1 opacity (0.25 s out3).
- The pointed cell fills with the accent, its number turns bold white and it scales to 1.12×.
- The pointer moves to 4 cells at 0.9, 1.8, 2.7 and 3.6 s, resting 0.5 s on each.
- From 4.1–4.8 s it leaves the table and all highlights fade.
When to use it: Use it on pricing comparisons, score tables and spec sheets where rows and columns are cross-checked.
