Please use "Regrouping bubbles" (グループが組み替わるバブル), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-093

What it is: A bubble chart where bubbles jostle and regroup by the chosen category
What the sample shows: A cluster of coloured bubbles jostles apart into 4, then 3 groups as tabs switch, then merges back; labels above each group show budget totals.
Spec:
- 36 project bubbles with radius (4.5 + √budget × 5) × 0.85, coloured by 4 teams.
- Force layout: pull toward the group centre with strength 5, velocity damping e^(−5·dt), and 3 collision passes with 1.5 px spacing.
- In the demo, by-team at 1.0 s forms 4 clusters, by-status at 2.6 s forms 3, and all at 4.2 s merges them; each switch adds a random kick of ±30.
- Hovering drops other bubbles to 0.45 opacity and shows a tooltip; group labels move with their cluster, show the budget total, and fade in 0.3 s after a switch.
When to use it: Use it in project-management or portfolio-analysis dashboards to show the same data from different groupings.
