Motion Prompt #1-158

What it is: A round plus button widens on hover to reveal its label
What the sample shows: When the cursor lands on a round plus button, it stretches to the right into a pill, the plus turns and the label 'New project' appears; it folds back into a circle when the cursor leaves.
Spec:
- A 52px round accent button showing only a plus sign; the cursor moves onto it in 0.6 s.
- Over 0.5 s (easeInOut power 3) the button widens to the right into a 140px pill and the plus turns 90°.
- The label fades in only after the expansion passes halfway (progress 0.5).
- The cursor leaves at 3.4 s toward the lower left and the button folds back into a circle.
When to use it: Use it for floating add buttons that stay compact and explain themselves on hover.
