Motion Prompt #1-142

What it is: Slide a row left to reveal delete and remove it
What the sample shows: A finger drags the second row left to uncover a red "Delete" area; on release the row slides out, its height collapses and the rows below move up, then the row returns.
Spec:
- A 4-row list, 280 wide with rows 38 high.
- The finger presses row 2 at 0.8 s and drags it 150px left over 0.8–1.5 s (easeInOut power 3); the red "Delete" area underneath fades in with the distance.
- Released at 1.5 s, the row slides out past the left edge in 0.3 s (easeIn power 3).
- Over 1.8–2.1 s the row height collapses from 38 to 0 and the rows below move up.
- Over 3.7–4.2 s the row grows back to 38 in place and fades in, returning to the start.
When to use it: Use it in mobile lists such as notifications, mail or to-dos where items are removed quickly.
