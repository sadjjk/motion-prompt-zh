Motion Prompt #3-086

What it is: An edit where every cut lands on the music's beat and the strong beats switch to the most striking shot
What the sample shows: A beat bar along the bottom has a beat line every 0.5 s, with a taller blue accent line every 2 beats. The picture changes only on the beats: on an accent it cuts to a close-up of the face that pops slightly larger and settles back; on the weak beats it alternates between wide and medium shots. Colour blocks above the bar record which shot each segment used, turning solid as the playhead passes.
Spec:
- 9.4 s cycle. Beat 1 falls at 0.4 s, then one every 0.5 s: 17 beats, the last at 8.4 s.
- Every 2nd beat (beats 1, 3, 5 …) is an accent: cut to a close-up popped to 1.07×, easing back as e^(-6t).
- Weak beats alternate wide and medium; the blocks are blue for close-ups, light blue for medium and grey for wide.
- From 8.8 to 9.4 s the picture fades back to the opening wide shot.
When to use it: For travel shorts, product beat edits and any cut that should ride the music.
