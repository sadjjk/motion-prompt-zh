Please use "Hold to confirm" (長押しで確定), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects".
Motion Prompt #1-104

What it is: An action runs only after the button is held until its progress fills
What the sample shows: The first hold is released early and the fill drains back; the second lasts the full 1.2 s, the file card disappears and the button reads "Deleted".
Spec:
- Holding the 220×46 "Hold to delete" button fills a dark bar from left to right at a steady rate; the action runs only after a full 1.2 s.
- Hold 1, 0.70–1.40 s: released after 0.7 s (about 58%); the fill drains to 0 over 0.35 s with easeOut (power 3).
- Hold 2, 2.00–3.20 s reaches full and runs at 3.20 s: the file card above fades out and flattens over 0.35 s, and the button reads "Deleted".
- Reset over 4.30–4.70 s: the fill drains with easeInOut (power 3), the card fades back in and the label returns to "Hold to delete".
- Versus a normal click: a single click does nothing, and letting go early visibly drains the progress.
When to use it: Use it for irreversible actions such as delete, transfer or submit, to prevent accidental taps.
