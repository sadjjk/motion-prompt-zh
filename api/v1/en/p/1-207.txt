Motion Prompt #1-207

What it is: A 3D landscape drawn in glowing contour lines that slowly undulates and rises under the cursor
What the sample shows: A perspective landscape of 13 blue contour levels rolls; the ground swells into a hill under the cursor, and a click sends a white ring of light across the lines.
Spec:
- The height field is a sum of 3 sine waves whose phase turns once per cycle.
- Marching squares on a 64×32 grid trace contours at 13 levels, drawn in perspective: farther lines dim, higher lines brighten.
- A bump of height 1.1 and Gaussian radius 0.28 is added under the cursor's position on the ground.
- A click at 3.5 s spreads a ring of light at 1.6 units per second, turning the lines it crosses white.
- The cursor enters at 0.3–1.0 s and leaves at 4.5–5.2 s, taking the bump with it.
When to use it: Use it in hero sections for outdoor, mapping, data or space products that want quiet depth.
