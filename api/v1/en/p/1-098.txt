Motion Prompt #1-098

What it is: A layer appears by growing from just under full size while fading in
What the sample shows: Clicking "Open" grows the dialog from 0.95× to full size as it fades in, stopping without overshoot; closing plays the same path in reverse.
Spec:
- The page fills 0–386 on the left. The cursor reaches the 110×36 "Open" button at 0.95 s and clicks at 1.0 s.
- Open amount a = easeOutCubic(p), with p going 0→1 over 1.0–1.25 s (0.25 s). Page scrim opacity 0.36·a.
- Dialog 220×112, radius 12, centred at (193, 92). Scale 0.95 + 0.05·a, opacity a; a rises monotonically to 1 with no overshoot.
- Close: the cursor moves to × by 3.0 s and clicks at 3.3 s; p goes 1→0 over 3.3–3.5 s (0.2 s), replaying the open curve backwards, shrinking to 0.95× as it fades.
- The curve panel on the right (386–542): solid blue is easeOutCubic, dashed grey is the elastic pop-in backOut (passes 1.0, then settles).
- Versus Modal dialog: it starts closer to full size and is shorter. Versus Elastic pop-in: no overshoot.
When to use it: Use it to open and close dialogs, popovers and menus quietly, without drawing too much attention.
