Motion Prompt #4-007

What it is: An amber LED dot-matrix sign scrolling a station message
What the sample shows: On an angled LED board, amber dot letters scroll right to left, with lit dots glowing softly.
Spec:
- 64 columns × 7 rows of amber LED dots using a 5 × 7 font: 5 columns per glyph plus 1 spacer column.
- The message NEXT TRAIN · PLATFORM 07 · ON TIME scrolls right to left once every 6.4 s, stepping column by column.
- Angled perspective: dot size and pitch are 0.55× at the left end and 1.3× at the right; base dot 2.2 px, pitch 7.2 px.
- Lit dots #FFE3A0 carry a glow 2.6× the dot radius; unlit dots are #2A1C10.
When to use it: Use it to open transport, city or retro-tech videos, as an announcement banner, or as looping venue signage.
