Motion Prompt #1-189

What it is: A side nav whose items hang off a trunk by branch lines; opening grows new branches and picking an item lights its path
What the sample shows: A white side nav has a vertical trunk on the left, with each item joined by a rounded branch. When the cursor clicks 'Products', the items below are pushed down and three child branches grow one by one with a glowing tip as the children fade in. Clicking 'Cases' sends a light along the path from the trunk to it and the item turns orange; a final click on 'Products' folds it all back.
Spec:
- 5 top-level items sit 20 px apart; 3 children sit 18 px apart under 'Products', and opening pushes the items below down 54 px.
- There are 3 clicks, at 1.0 s, 2.25 s and 3.75 s, each sending a ripple from the cursor tip.
- Over 1.05–1.5 s the items below move down, and from 1.1 s the child branches grow along their length 0.13 s apart, 0.4 s each, with a small glowing dot at the tip.
- From 1.36 s the children fade in over 0.26 s each, with the same 0.13 s stagger.
- From 2.3–2.88 s a light with a trail runs along the line from the trunk to 'Cases'; on arrival the node fills, a ring spreads and the item turns orange as selected.
- It folds back over 3.8–4.28 s and the cursor returns to rest by 4.45 s, on a 4.8 s cycle.
When to use it: Use it in dashboards, documentation sites and file browsers where hierarchical navigation should make the current location obvious.
