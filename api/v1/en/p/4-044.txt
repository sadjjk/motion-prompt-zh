Motion Prompt #4-044

What it is: A pictogram chart that compares amounts by repeating same-size icons, so viewers can count instead of reading bars
What the sample shows: On beige paper, person icons stamp down one by one into three rows while the number at each row's end counts up; the largest row, A 城, turns red with a 最多 tag, then the icons lift away from right to left.
Spec:
- Background #EFE7D6, figures grey #8F877A; legend: 1 figure = 10 万人. Rows A / B / C 城 hold 12 / 8 / 5 figures, 24 px apart.
- From 0.5 s figures drop every 0.11 s from 1.5× to full size, squashing 18% on landing; a 0.2 s pause between rows; the number at the row end counts up with them.
- 3.9 s the largest row turns red #C8352B and a 最多 tag bounces in.
- From 5.4 s figures lift away column by column from the right, 0.045 s apart; the text fades out at 5.9–6.3 s.
When to use it: Use it for data explainers comparing populations, users or resources, where the count itself should be the picture.
