Motion Prompt #4-029

What it is: A one-sided Möbius strip swaying in space with stripes flowing along it
What the sample shows: A Möbius strip sways gently in a dark space while orange-pink and white stripes flow along it, front quietly becoming back.
Spec:
- Dark gradient background #1A1C2A → #0B0C12. Strip radius 1.7, band width 1, perspective projection.
- It swings about the vertical axis between -0.1 and 0.7 rad and tilts between 0.04 and 0.16 rad, each 1 swing per 6.4 s.
- 40 stripes, each 0.375 orange-pink gradient and 0.625 white, advancing 8 stripes per cycle.
- Shaded with diffuse light and a power-40 specular highlight; back-facing normals flip toward the camera so it reads as one surface.
When to use it: Openers for maths or science videos, hero sections for brands themed on infinity or loops, or presentation title slides.
