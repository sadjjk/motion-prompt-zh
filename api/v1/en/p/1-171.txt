Motion Prompt #1-171

What it is: Pressing copy spins the icon into a check and pops a tooltip
What the sample shows: A pointer presses the copy button beside a command line. The copy icon spins away, a check springs in, the button turns accent-coloured and a 'Copied' tooltip pops up above it; shortly after, everything returns.
Spec:
- The button is pressed at 1.0 s (shrinks to 0.92).
- The copy icon spins 90° and shrinks to 0 while the check scales up and the fill turns accent (0.35 s cubic-bezier(.3,1.5,.5,1)).
- The 'Copied' tooltip pops up from 6 px lower, scaling 0.8 → 1.
- After 1.5 s (at 2.5 s) everything reverses on the same curve.
