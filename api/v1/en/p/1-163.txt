Motion Prompt #1-163

What it is: A dashed selection box whose line keeps crawling, with corner handles
What the sample shows: In a design-tool canvas, the blue dashed box around a layer keeps crawling in one direction, and the size label below changes as the box grows and shrinks.
Spec:
- A blue 1.5px dashed selection box (dash 6, gap 5) whose dash offset moves −11 every 0.48 s (10 rounds per cycle), so the line keeps flowing one way.
- Each corner has an 8×8 handle with a white fill and a blue outline.
- The box resizes between 200×90 and 280×120: growing over 0.4–2.0 s and shrinking over 2.8–4.4 s (easeInOut power 3).
- A label below shows the rounded width × height live.
When to use it: Use it when introducing design tools, image editors or no-code products to show selecting and editing.
