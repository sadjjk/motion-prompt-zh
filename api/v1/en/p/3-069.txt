Please use "Soft focus" (ソフトフォーカス), one of the names in "Photo looks, lighting & styles".
Motion Prompt #3-069

What it is: Highlights spread softly and contrast drops, while the outlines stay
What the sample shows: A blurred, brightened copy of the whole scene is layered on; the sun and the lit edge of the person spread, the shadows lift slightly, and the outlines of hills, tree and person remain clear.
Spec:
- Scene: a landscape with the sun at (432, 54) and a head-and-shoulders figure (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px).
- Glow: a copy of the scene blurred by 7 px and brightened 1.12× is screened on at 0.6 opacity.
- Contrast: white at 0.1 opacity lifts the shadows; the original image stays underneath, so outlines are not blurred.
- Top right reads "SOFT n%".
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For portraits, weddings and nostalgic scenes that should feel soft and dreamy.
