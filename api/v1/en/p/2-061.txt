Motion Prompt #2-061

What it is: A handmade zine-style collage where cut-out pieces are slapped onto paper one by one like stop-motion
What the sample shows: A cut-out head slaps onto kraft paper, its scalp flips open like a lid, stars, a rocket and a swallow fly out, and letter blocks and a torn banner are pasted on in turn.
Spec:
- Kraft paper #DCC39A with a monospaced masthead. Every cut-out is pre-rendered at 2× resolution with an off-white #F2ECDF scissor rim, a drop shadow and ink #16130F halftone dots.
- Pieces slap down on a 12 fps stop-motion beat: a gentleman's portrait at 0.25 s (scale 1.3→1.12→0.96→1), a red disc #D2261C at 0.45 s, a newspaper scrap at 0.65 s.
- At 1.5 s the top of the head flips open along a zigzag cut (6 frames), a POP burst lasts 3 frames, then 5 pieces (star, Saturn, rocket, swallow, rose) arc out of the head.
- From 2.5 s a ransom-note letter block lands every 1/6 s, 1 at a time; a torn black banner slides in at 3.25 s. Resting pieces jitter ±1.2 px every 1/6 s. At 5.4–6.0 s a torn-edged sheet of kraft paper wipes in from the right to reset.
When to use it: Openings and social shorts for culture magazines, art exhibitions, podcasts and creative brands.
