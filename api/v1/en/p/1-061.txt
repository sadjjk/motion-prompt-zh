Please use "Rolling word cube" (単語キューブの回転), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-061

What it is: A headline whose keywords sit on the faces of a cube that rolls to the next one
What the sample shows: The cube rolls down one face at a time to reveal the next word, the caption below flips with it, and a progress bar fills between rolls.
Spec:
- 4 keywords on 4 faces; face height 72 px, half-depth 36 px, perspective 520 px. It rolls down 1 face every 1.6 s, each roll taking 0.9 s.
- Roll easing cubic-bezier(.5,-.25,.3,1.3) overshoots at both ends; the old front face dims to brightness .55 while the new one lights up.
- The caption flips: the old line slides up and out, the new one springs in from below (.7 s spring, .4 s opacity); the current dot scales to 1.3×.
- The bottom progress bar fills linearly over 1.6 s; 4 rolls make a full 360° and return to word 1.
When to use it: Use it in a landing-page hero or 'What we do' section to cycle through services, stages or selling points.
