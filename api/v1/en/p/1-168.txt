Please use "Crosshair cursor" (クロスヘアカーソル), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-168

What it is: Thin cross lines follow the cursor with live coordinates beside it
What the sample shows: On a dark background with a faint grid, a horizontal and a vertical line span the frame and cross at the cursor. A small ring marks the crossing, and a label at the lower right updates the coordinates as it moves.
Spec:
- The cross lines are 1 px wide and span edge to edge; a 6 px radius ring marks the crossing.
- The label's X and Y are the position scaled to 1920 × 1080, zero-padded to 4 digits.
- The label sits 12 px below and right of the cursor and flips to the other side near the right or bottom edge.
- The crosshair fades in from 0.4–0.8 s and out from 4.4–4.9 s.
When to use it: Use it for design tools, technical products and blueprint-style portfolios.
