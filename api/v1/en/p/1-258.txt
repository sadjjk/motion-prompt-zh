Motion Prompt #1-258

What it is: A holographic card whose spiral ripples glow rainbow from the centre and roll outwards as the angle changes
What the sample shows: On an indigo card, spiral ripples around a ringed planet roll outwards and change colour each time it sways.
Spec:
- Card 126 × 168 px (3:4), corner radius 0.045 × width, thickness 0.036 × width; it swings on its own in a 6.4 s loop: yaw 24°·sin a, pitch 11.5°·sin 2a, roll 1.6°·sin a and a 1.3 % bob.
- Waves are sin(radius × 32 − azimuth × 4) to the power 2.3, a four-armed spiral; hue = radius × 2.1 − tilt × 1.5. The emblem is a planet with a tilted ring on a silver frame.
- Layers from back to front: base pattern (−0.082 W), emblem (−0.03 W), then surface foil and a bevelled metal frame; tilting shifts each layer by its depth, so the card reads as deep.
- The light direction turns with the angle and a diagonal sheen crosses the face; cross-star glints twinkle on frame corners and the emblem tip.
When to use it: Use it for space or astronomy themes, music and event keepsake cards and night-time rewards.
