Motion Prompt #2-049

What it is: Flat vector animation: solid colour shapes with no gradients and a bouncy, squash-and-stretch hero
What the sample shows: A coral dot drops from an ultramarine sky and hops 5 times; each landing springs up flat coloured buildings, then a wordmark bounces in.
Spec:
- Sky #2B2BFF, ground #FFF6E9, and a single coral dot #FF5A4E of radius 16 px that falls in from the top over 0.2 s.
- 5 hops during 0.4–4.4 s (0.8 s each, 72 px high): an 18% squash for 0.16 s on landing, a 10% stretch in the air, and 3 ghost trails.
- 6 landing spots, each with 2 buildings behind it (12 in total), popping up with elasticOut over 0.6 s at 0 / 0.1 s after the landing.
- The popwise wordmark (46 px) bounces in at 4.6–5.2 s; buildings, text and dot shrink away at 5.4–6.0 s.
When to use it: Use it for app explainers, product intros and cheerful brand openers where you want a friendly, simple look.
