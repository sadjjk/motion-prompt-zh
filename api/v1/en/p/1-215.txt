Motion Prompt #1-215

What it is: A field of small cubes that turn toward the cursor and spin in a wave when you click
What the sample shows: Twelve by four cubes sit at an angle; those near the cursor tilt toward it, and a click at 3.2 s sends a wave that spins them one by one.
Spec:
- Cubes 24 px on a side, 44 px apart in a 12×4 grid, drawn in parallel projection; at rest each is turned 0.5 rad about x and 0.62 rad about y.
- With weight e^(−(d/80)²) for distance d from the cursor, a cube tilts up to 1.1 rad toward the cursor and gets up to 1.5 times brighter.
- A click at 3.2 s sends a wave out at 300 px/s; each cube it passes spins 90° about y. Every face has the same colour and is shaded only by its direction, so after the spin a cube looks exactly as before.
- Colour runs from teal #2EC4B6 on the left to violet #7A5CFF on the right. The cursor enters at 0.4–1.0 s and leaves at 4.6–5.3 s.
When to use it: Use it behind game or creative-studio heroes when the background should react to the cursor in 3D.
