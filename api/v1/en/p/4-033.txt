Motion Prompt #4-033

What it is: A thermal receipt slowly feeding out of a register's paper slot
What the sample shows: A dark paper slot fades in and a white receipt eases out from the top, revealing the shop name, 3 item lines and "Total · Shipped" under a dashed rule, before everything fades.
Spec:
- A dark paper slot, 341×188 (#1f2937, inner border #374151), with an 11px lip at the top shaded #4b5563→#1f2937. The receipt is 301×130 on #f9fafb.
- The slot fades in and rises 5px over 0.36–0.96 s (quadratic ease-out).
- The receipt feeds out 165px over 1.08–3.96 s with a quadratic ease-out, then another 5px over 4.08–5.28 s with a sine ease-in-out, its top edge stopping at 16% of the slot height.
- On the paper: shop name, 3 item lines, and "Total · Shipped" under a dashed #9ca3af rule. Everything fades over 5.4–6.0 s, back to the empty background by 6.4 s.
- Versus Split-flap board: the information appears as paper feeds out, not by flaps flipping to new characters.
When to use it: Use it for e-commerce, payment or POS promos, order-complete and shipping notices, or videos that reveal a monthly report or results.
