Motion Prompt #4-033

What it is: The Edo-period woodblock print style: ink outlines with a few flat colour blocks
What the sample shows: In the rain a big wooden bridge spans the frame, and 2 people with umbrellas walk across it in opposite directions, passing each other. Outlines are in ink with a few flat colours on top, printed slightly off-register from the line. The top of the sky and the bottom of the water use graded printing; the distant hills are flat grey-blue with no outline. Rain falls as fine cut lines at a fixed angle, rings spread on the water, and a foreground pine branch cuts off the top-right corner.
Spec:
- 6.4 s cycle; continuous loop with no reset segment.
- Colour blocks are printed 2 px across and 1.5 px down from the ink line.
- Each of the 2 walkers crosses from off-screen to off-screen once per cycle (18 steps), the umbrella hiding the face.
- 110 rain lines fall 2 times per cycle, rain rings on the water spread 2 times, and the wave pattern shifts by one motif per cycle.
When to use it: Use it for Japanese-themed, travel and seasonal visuals, or whenever you want the mood of a rainy day.
