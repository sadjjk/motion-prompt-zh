Please use "Per-section background shift" (セクションごとの背景切り替え), one of the names in "Interaction & data motion".
Motion Prompt #1-071

What it is: A product page whose background colour flows from section to section as you scroll
What the sample shows: Scrolling melts the background into the next flavour colour, the central can tilts as it swaps colour and label, and the copy slides up.
Spec:
- Scrolling passes 4 flavours, with the background blending continuously between neighbouring colours: #FFAB91 / #C0EB75 / #A5B4FC / #FFC8DD.
- The central can (74×140 px) changes colour and label per section: #E64A19 / #5C940D / #4C3BCF / #D6336C.
- The can tilts by its offset from the nearest section (offset × 24°) and the label slides sideways (offset × −60 px).
- 4 section dots on the right, the current one at 1.5×; demo scrolls over 0.4→4.8 s and back over 5.4→6.3 s.
When to use it: Use it on product pages for drinks, cosmetics or any multi-flavour, multi-colour line, showing one variant per scroll section.
