Motion Prompt #1-150

What it is: A sidebar that folds down to a strip of icons
What the sample shows: The cursor clicks the button at the foot of the sidebar; it narrows to its icons as the labels fade, the stat cards and chart on the right widen, and a second click later restores it.
Spec:
- An admin layout with a 150-wide sidebar on the left (4 nav items; the current one, "Reports", on a light blue fill).
- The cursor clicks the collapse button at the bottom at 0.9 s; over 0.95–1.4 s (easeInOut power 3) the sidebar narrows to 52, icons only, and the labels fade out in 0.2 s while shifting 8px left.
- The 3 stat cards and the line chart on the right widen in step with the sidebar.
- A second click at 2.9 s widens it back to 150 over 2.95–3.4 s, with labels fading in over 3.2–3.4 s; the button arrow flips 180° with the state.
When to use it: Use it in admin panels, dashboards or docs with a left nav where the content sometimes needs more room.
