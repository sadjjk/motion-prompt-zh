Motion Prompt #2-090

What it is: Text with real depth turns to show its side
What the sample shows: 立体 with a white face and darker coral layers stacked behind for depth turns slowly once around the vertical axis, showing its thickness side-on.
Spec:
- A white face with 14 coral layers behind it (2.6 px deep each, darker further back).
- It turns once around the vertical axis every 4.7 s at a constant rate (scaleX = cos).
- A 12° tilt shifts the layers slightly upward.
- When it faces away the draw order flips so the deepest layer is in front.
When to use it: Use it for logos, titles or game-style headings.
