Motion Prompt #1-118

What it is: Opens with a shortcut and filters commands as you type
What the sample shows: The ⌘K keys press, the page dims and a panel appears; typing "Settings" narrows the list with matches in blue, the highlight moves down a row and is confirmed.
Spec:
- A ⌘ K keycap hint at the bottom right; both keys press down 2px at 0.5 s.
- Over 0.6–0.85 s the page dims to 0.4 and the 300×159 panel fades in while scaling 0.94 → 1 with easeOut power 3.
- "Settings" is typed one character at a time at 1.3 s and 1.5 s; the list narrows from 5 items to 3 and then 2, with matching characters in blue.
- At 2.3 s the highlight moves down one row over 0.2 s (easeInOut power 3); Enter darkens it briefly at 2.9 s, and the panel and dim layer fade out over 3.1–3.4 s.
When to use it: Use it in developer tools, dashboards and editors where keyboard users want quick actions.
