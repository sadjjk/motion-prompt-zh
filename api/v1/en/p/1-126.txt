Motion Prompt #1-126

What it is: Cuts the image into strips that slide away alternately up and down
What the sample shows: The picture in the frame splits into 6 vertical strips that slide away left to right, up, down, up…, revealing the next picture; the same move later brings the first back.
Spec:
- Two geometric pictures alternate inside a 431×128 frame.
- The current picture is cut into 6 vertical strips; each slides 148px out, 0.08 s apart, 0.5 s each with easeInOut power 3 — strips 1, 3, 5 go up and strips 2, 4, 6 go down.
- Timeline: picture A holds until 0.9 s, slices away over 0.9–1.8 s to reveal B, B holds until 3.3 s, then slices away over 3.3–4.2 s back to A.
When to use it: Use it for hero sliders, portfolio switches or section breaks where an image change should feel bold.
