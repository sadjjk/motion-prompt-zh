Motion Prompt #2-062

What it is: A flat explainer style built from thick white-outlined stickers and a single accent colour
What the sample shows: A phone sticker slaps down with a bounce and callout lines draw out; the camera flies to a periodic table that lights up in a cascade, then to a chart that grows bar by bar.
Spec:
- Blue-grey background #E9EEF2, slate #25313D and just 1 accent colour, #FF5A36. Stickers have a white outline and a soft shadow and slap down with an overshooting ease.
- A phone sticker lands at 0.2 s, 2 title stickers at 0.35 and 0.5 s, and from 0.6 s 4 callout lines label the parts with element badges in turn.
- At 1.6–2.2 s the camera flies diagonally to a periodic table (dipping to 0.9 zoom mid-flight). 118 tiles pop in, the first 10 elements light at 2.45 s, the other 60 cascade on at 2.5–3.2 s, and the counter reaches 70.
- At 3.5–4.1 s the camera flies to a bar chart; from 4.15 s 70 small squares stack up one by one into 5 bars (23, 16, 12, 11, 8), and an orange 'about 70' sticker slaps down at 4.95 s. The camera flies back to the start at 5.4–6.2 s.
When to use it: Science and knowledge explainers, product teardown videos, data reports and infographic shorts.
