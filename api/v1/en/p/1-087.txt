Motion Prompt #1-087

What it is: A pay button that morphs into a progress ring and ends with a check mark
What the sample shows: The pay button shrinks into a circle on click, a ring fills while 3 steps light up, it turns green with a check, and a receipt bounces up from below.
Spec:
- On click the button shrinks from 214 px wide into a 44 px circle over 0.5 s with overshoot; the label fades in 0.2 s.
- After a 0.25 s delay a 29 px-radius ring appears and fills in 2.6 s (power-2.2 ease-out).
- 3 steps — requesting, bank check, done — light up at 2%, 45% and 99.9% progress, each dot springing to 1.3×.
- The full ring turns green #119C72 and, after 0.2 s, draws a check in 0.45 s; 0.9 s later a receipt card springs up from below in 0.6 s.
When to use it: Use it in e-commerce checkouts and in-app purchases to show payment progress and success.
