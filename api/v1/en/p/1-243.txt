Motion Prompt #1-243

What it is: Round options packed in a honeycomb; the chosen one swells and shoves its neighbours aside, and the whole cluster leans a little toward the pointer
What the sample shows: On a pale lavender gradient seven circles sit in a honeycomb, six around one, each in its own hue: violet, coral, amber, green, sky blue, magenta and cyan. Unchosen circles are pale tints; only the chosen centre is solid violet with a white disc. The pointer drifts in from the right and the cluster leans toward it; resting on the coral circle deepens its tint. A press swells that circle to solid coral and reveals a white rounded square while the centre fades back to a pale tint, and the others are shoved away, a little smaller and tilted, settling in a wave from the inside out. A press on the upper-left green circle brings up a white triangle and the cluster rearranges around it. A last press on the centre restores the starting layout, and the lean fades as the pointer leaves.
Spec:
- Circles are 44 px across on a 45 px pitch. At Pull 55 the chosen one scales to 1.281, the rest are pushed about 14.1 px and shrink up to 9% with a 2.75° tilt.
- Strength and delay fall off with distance as exp(-(distance - 1) / 2.8), with 22 ms of delay per pitch.
- Spring stiffness runs from 440 to 580 and Bounce 55 gives a damping ratio of 0.636; position, width, height and rotation each ride their own spring.
- The right circle is pressed at 1.0 s, the upper-left at 2.6 s and the centre at 4.0 s; over 220 ms the chosen circle goes from its pale tint (34% hue over white) to solid colour and its white shape fades in.
- With Give 50 the cluster leans up to 4.5 px toward a nearby pointer, fading out 44 px beyond its edge.
- The pointer enters at 0.3 s and leaves by 4.8 s, and the frame returns to 0 s.
When to use it: Use it for small sets of visual choices: picking an icon, theme or avatar, onboarding preference pickers and emoji reactions.
