Motion Prompt #3-049

What it is: The person is tiny; the frame is mostly about the place
What the sample shows: From a distant, slightly high camera, mountains, buildings, trees and clouds fill the frame while a person only about 20 px tall walks across and back.
Spec:
- Framing: camera 2.2 m high, 30 m from the person, focal length 330 px; the person (about 1.8 m tall) is about 20 px tall.
- Action: walks 6 m to one side and back each pass (12 m in total) at cosine speed, with 8 stride cycles.
- Setting: mountains, buildings, trees and clouds fill the frame; the clouds drift by 2 m.
- Labels: top right reads "大远景 ELS"; the scale figure at bottom right is shaded from head to toe (the whole body fits with room to spare).
- The camera never moves; one pass = 4.7 s and the first and last frames match.
When to use it: To establish where a character is, or to make a person look small or alone in a place.
