Motion Prompt #1-213

What it is: Car light trails streaming along a winding night highway that speed up into a hyperspace warp while you hold down
What the sample shows: Red and white light trails stream along a night road; when the cursor holds from 2.3 s the speed climbs, trails stretch, the view widens and tints violet, and on release it drops back to cruising.
Spec:
- A perspective night road: horizon at y = 76, the road bends into the distance by 0.012·z²·sin(phase).
- Red tail lights recede on the 2 right lanes and white-blue headlights approach on the 2 left lanes, 14 cars per lane.
- Traffic position p(u) = 3·u/6.4 + 2·S(u), where S eases from 0 to 1 over 2.4–4.2 s.
- The cursor presses on the road at 2.3 s and releases at 4.0 s; meanwhile speed climbs, trails stretch, and the view widens 1.35 times with a violet tint.
- p covers exactly 5 laps per cycle, so the loop is seamless.
When to use it: Use it as a hero for mobility, gaming or any product that wants a sense of speed.
