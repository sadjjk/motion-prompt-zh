Motion Prompt #3-080

What it is: Fog makes the distance paler and whiter, light spreads in the air and the layers recede one by one
What the sample shows: Fog rolls into the evening street; of the four layers — far hills, buildings, trees with street lamps, and the person in front — the further ones turn closer to the fog colour and lose contrast; a fog band drifts along the horizon and each lamp gets a large halo and a cone of light below.
Spec:
- Scene: an evening street corner (sky #C9D1DA, far hills #56677C, buildings #4F5D73, trees #34493F, person #232A38).
- Fog layers: fog colour #D5DADF is laid on at 0.42 after the hills, 0.32 after the buildings and 0.22 after the trees and lamps, so further layers are covered more often; none goes over the person.
- Fog band: 7 white puffs are drawn on a 68×26 canvas and stretched over y 50–150 at 0.7 opacity; the puffs drift sideways 1–2 times per pass.
- 3 street lamps (x 114, 292, 494): a halo #FFD68C of radius 18–44 px around each bulb (0.6 at the centre) and a 44 px wide cone spreading down; top right reads "FOG n%".
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, ease-in-out).
When to use it: For early mornings, after rain or mysteries when the picture should feel deep and quiet.
