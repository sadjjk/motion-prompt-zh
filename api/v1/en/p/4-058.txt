Motion Prompt #4-058

What it is: A cozy farming-game look: a top-down farm drawn in 4 px pixels with a wooden HUD for the clock and money
What the sample shows: A fenced green farm seen from above. The wooden HUD at top right ticks the clock forward 10 minutes at a time from 6:00. The farmer walks the path and waters 5 columns in turn, darkening the soil. The crops grow through 3 stages: sprout, leaves and ripe pumpkin. The farmer comes back and harvests column by column, +5 floats up and today's income rises. Finally a night blue covers the scene, and when it lifts it is 6:00 again with seeds in the ground.
Spec:
- Ground #6DB54A, 1 pixel = 4 px; field of 5 columns × 2 rows.
- From 0.6 s each column is watered 0.32 s apart; a sprout shows 0.5 s after watering.
- Leaves from 2.6 s and pumpkins from 3.2 s, staggered by column.
- From 3.7 s each column is harvested 0.28 s apart, +5 per plant, +50 in total.
- The clock moves 10 minutes every 0.45 s from 6:00.
- 5.4–5.9 s night darkens the scene; 5.9 s it resets to 6:00 and seeds; dawn by 6.4 s.
When to use it: Use it for farming, seasons, growth processes and habit-building topics that show something growing step by step.
